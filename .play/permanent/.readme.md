# .play/permanent

durable, **tracked** bhrowser playbooks — the ones worth a home in git.

## .the split

| dir | tracked? | for |
|-----|----------|-----|
| `.play/temporary` | no (gitignored) | throwaway scratch — one-off probes, wizard drives, quick reads. delete freely. |
| `.play/permanent` | **yes** | reusable playbooks a future traveler would re-run: a generic collector, a repeatable search, a demo of a workflow. |

## .the rule of promotion

a scratch playbook earns `.play/permanent` (or a role-scoped skill dir) only once it is **reused or demo-worthy**:

- **reused** — you have run it, or a variant, in two or more distinct sessions
- **demo-worthy** — it shows a workflow a future traveler would want to copy

until then it stays in `.play/temporary`, where it is gitignored and costs nobody a review.

## .where durable playbooks usually belong

a playbook that serves one role's skill belongs **under that skill**, not here. example: the
provider-census workflow lives at
`src/domain.roles/referrer/skills/census.providers/` — its `frame.*`, `federal.census.fetch`,
`project.federal.coverage`, `review.*`, and `probe.*` playbooks are the tracked, reusable demos of
that skill, co-located with the skill they exercise (`rule.prefer.most-common-denominator`).

reach for `.play/permanent` only for a playbook that is **cross-skill** — a generic browser
tactic (a search collector, a page harvester) that no single role owns.
