# mechanism.pge2.nociceptor-sensitization

## .what

**PGE2** binds EP receptors on peripheral nociceptors and lowers their activation threshold → **hyperalgesia** (pain).

## .clade

**vertebrata.** EP receptor–mediated peripheral sensitization is conserved across vertebrates.

## .relation-to-human

**conancestral.** the cat feels this pain by the same machinery as the human. high-confidence transfer.

## .role-in-cascade

converts prostaglandin output into the clinical sign of pain — the peripheral component that a COX-2 inhibitor relieves.
