# mechanism.inflammation.vascular-response

## .what

prostanoids dilate vessels and raise vascular permeability → the **edema, redness, and heat** of inflammation.

## .clade

**vertebrata.** the inflammatory vascular response is a deeply conserved vertebrate reaction.

## .relation-to-human

**conancestral.** same vascular signs in cat and human. high-confidence transfer.

## .role-in-cascade

the visible inflammation signs; the anti-inflammatory effect of a COX-2 inhibitor damps this response.
