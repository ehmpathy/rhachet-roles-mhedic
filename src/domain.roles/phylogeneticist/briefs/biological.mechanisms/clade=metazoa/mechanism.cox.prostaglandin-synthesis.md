# mechanism.cox.prostaglandin-synthesis

## .what

cyclooxygenase (COX / prostaglandin-endoperoxide synthase) oxidizes arachidonic acid into **PGH2**, the branch point for all prostanoids (PGE2, PGI2, TXA2).

## .clade

**metazoa.** COX enzymes are present across metazoans, far older than vertebrates.

## .relation-to-human

**conancestral.** humans and all mammals inherit COX from this deep metazoan origin. mechanism-of-action insight transfers with high confidence.

## .role-in-cascade

the committed step that converts liberated arachidonic acid into the prostaglandin precursor. every nsaid acts here.

## .transfer

any COX-targeted insight (nsaid mechanism of action) applies across all descendant clades, human included.
