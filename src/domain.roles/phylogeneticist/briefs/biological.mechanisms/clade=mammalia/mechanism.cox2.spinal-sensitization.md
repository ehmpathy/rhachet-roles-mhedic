# mechanism.cox2.spinal-sensitization

## .what

injury induces **COX-2** in the spinal cord; spinal PGE2 produces **central sensitization** — amplified, spread pain.

## .clade

**mammalia.** conserved mammalian spinal pain amplification.

## .relation-to-human

**conancestral.** same spinal mechanism in cat and human. high-confidence transfer across mammals.

## .role-in-cascade

the central component of pain — part of why COX-2 inhibition relieves pain beyond the injury site.
