# mechanism.pge2.hypothalamic-fever

## .what

**PGE2** acts on **EP3** receptors in the hypothalamic preoptic area → raised thermoregulatory setpoint → **fever**.

## .clade

**mammalia** for the PGE2/EP3 central mechanism. a deeper prostaglandin-linked febrile homolog extends into ectothermic vertebrates as behavioral fever.

## .relation-to-human

**conancestral** (mammalian). same central fever mechanism in cat and human. high-confidence transfer across mammals.

## .role-in-cascade

the fever clinical sign. the antipyretic effect of an nsaid acts here.
