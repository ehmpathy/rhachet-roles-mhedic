# mechanism.cox.isoform-split

## .what

two COX isoforms with divided labor:

- **COX-1** — constitutive. its prostaglandins protect gastric mucosa, sustain renal blood flow, and drive platelet thromboxane for hemostasis.
- **COX-2** — inducible. cytokines upregulate it at injury sites; its prostaglandins drive pain, inflammation, and fever.

## .clade

**mammalia** for the constitutive-vs-inducible division of labor. the deeper COX-1 / COX-2 gene duplication predates mammals (early vertebrate), but the characterized maintenance-vs-inflammatory split is mammalian.

## .relation-to-human

**conancestral.** both cat and human inherit both isoforms and the same division of roles. the shared COX-1 maintenance role is why the whole nsaid class shares a gastrointestinal + renal risk profile across species.

## .role-in-cascade

defines what a COX-2-selective drug **spares** (COX-1 → gut mucosa, renal perfusion, platelets) versus what it **blocks** (COX-2 → inflammation). the entire coxib safety rationale rests here.
