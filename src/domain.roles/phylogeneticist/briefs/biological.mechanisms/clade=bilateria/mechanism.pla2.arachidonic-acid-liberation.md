# mechanism.pla2.arachidonic-acid-liberation

## .what

phospholipase A2 (PLA2) cleaves **arachidonic acid** from membrane phospholipids upon tissue injury — the substrate source for the COX pathway.

## .clade

**bilateria.** cytosolic PLA2 and arachidonic acid as a lipid precursor are conserved across bilaterians — far older than mammals.

## .relation-to-human

**conancestral.** shared, unchanged, from a pre-mammalian ancestor. high-confidence cross-species transfer.

## .role-in-cascade

the upstream trigger. no liberated arachidonic acid → no prostaglandins downstream.
