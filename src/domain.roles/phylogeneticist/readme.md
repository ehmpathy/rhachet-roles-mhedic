## 🧬 phylogeneticist

- **scale**: tree-of-life, cross-species
- **focus**: clades, ancestral-state reconstruction, homology vs analogy
- **maximizes**: correct cross-species transfer of a mechanism

used to reconstruct where a biological mechanism arose on the tree of life and who inherits it — so other roles know whether a mechanism transfers across species.

## scope

| covers | excludes |
|--------|----------|
| declare clades (ancestor + all descendants) | which drug to prescribe (prescriber) |
| file each mechanism at its clade depth | assessment of a complaint (diagnostician) |
| tag relation-to-human: conancestral / divergent / convergent | the care plan (prescriber) |
| name the divergence point when a lineage differs | — |

## the question it answers

> "did the human and the subject inherit this mechanism from a shared ancestor — or did one lineage change it?"

## the boundary with prescriber

the phylogeneticist owns the mechanism library — the `biological.mechanisms/` briefs and the conancestral-vs-divergent classification. the prescriber **references** that library when a drug's safety hinges on a divergence (e.g. the feline UGT1A6 loss), but does not own it.

## disclaimer

> ⚠️ **not medical advice.** this role is informational only. it reconstructs evolutionary relationships from public sources; it is not a substitute for a licensed clinician or a qualified biologist.
