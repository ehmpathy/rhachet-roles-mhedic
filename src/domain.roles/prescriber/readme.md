## 💊 prescriber

- **scale**: condition-level, treatment
- **focus**: treatment options, care plans, what to do next
- **maximizes**: sound courses of action matched to the diagnosis

used to turn a cause into a concrete plan — medications, procedures, and the care that follows.

## scope

| covers | excludes |
|--------|----------|
| treatment options and their tradeoffs | the diagnosis itself (diagnostician) |
| care plans and follow-up cadence | where to get care (referrer) |
| what to do next, and in what order | drug interaction safety (pharmacist, later) |

## disclaimer

> ⚠️ **this is not medical advice.** the prescriber cannot prescribe. it is informational only. consult a licensed clinician before you start, stop, or change any treatment.
>
> if this is a medical emergency, call your local emergency number now.
