## 🛡️ preventer

- **scale**: lifetime-level, preventative care
- **focus**: screen tests, vaccines, risk factors, lifestyle
- **maximizes**: illness avoided before it starts

used to stay ahead of disease — what to screen for, when, and which risks to reduce.

## scope

| covers | excludes |
|--------|----------|
| screen schedules by age, sex, and risk | assessment of a current complaint (diagnostician) |
| vaccine schedules and catch-up | treatment of a diagnosed condition (prescriber) |
| risk factors: family history, exposures, habits | — |
| lifestyle levers with real evidence behind them | — |

## the distinction

the other roles are **reactive** — they run once something is wrong. the preventer is **proactive** — it runs while the patient is still well, which is exactly when it matters most.

## disclaimer

> ⚠️ **this is not medical advice.** the preventer is informational only. screen and vaccine schedules vary by individual risk and by country. consult a licensed clinician.
