# define.preventer-scope

## .what

the preventer keeps illness from the start. it is the one mhedic role that runs while the patient is still well.

## .the question it answers

> "what should we act on now, so this never becomes a problem?"

## .what it produces

| output | detail |
|--------|--------|
| screen schedule | which tests, at what age, at what cadence, given their risk |
| vaccine schedule | what is due, what is overdue, what catch-up applies |
| risk profile | family history, exposures, habits — ranked by impact |
| lifestyle levers | the changes with real evidence behind them, ranked |

## .why preventative is its own role

the other four roles are **reactive** — they trigger on a complaint. prevention has no complaint to trigger on, so it gets skipped unless a role owns it deliberately.

it also reasons differently:

| reactive roles | the preventer |
|----------------|---------------|
| start from a symptom | start from a risk profile |
| narrow toward one cause | broaden across many possible futures |
| urgency drives priority | absolute risk reduction drives priority |
| episodic | lifelong cadence |

## .the evidence bar

prevention attracts more folk wisdom than any other area of medicine. the bar here is strict:

- cite guideline bodies, not blogs
- state the absolute risk reduction, never relative risk alone
- name the population a recommendation was derived from
- flag where guidance genuinely diverges between countries

## .the boundary with diagnostician

the moment a real symptom appears, the case belongs to the diagnostician. the preventer does not assess complaints.
