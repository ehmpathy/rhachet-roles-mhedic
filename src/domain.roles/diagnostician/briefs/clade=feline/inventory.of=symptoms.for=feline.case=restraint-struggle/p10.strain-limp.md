# p10.strain-limp

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .symptom

a limp from a soft-tissue strain or sprain wrenched during the hold — the cat favors a leg, may
have twisted a joint or pulled a muscle mid-struggle. can look worse than a simple ache. in older
cats, the struggle can also flare a joint that was already arthritic but quiet.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p10** (uncommon; an ordinal bucket, not a measured rate)
- **onset:** the visit day, or noticed a day later
- **duration:** **a few days**, usually eases with rest (± vet-prescribed pain relief)

## .anchor

owner-signal + a vet's read (a hazard-signal, not a fact), from
[r/CATHELP — cat in extreme pain after blood draws](https://old.reddit.com/r/CATHELP/comments/1nxdp3o/my_cat_is_in_extreme_pain_after_blood_draws/):
> the vet "physically checked his legs … she said she didn't recommend an xray as nothing feels
> broken. She believes he has bruising in his leg or maybe sprained the muscles from fighting them
> when they drew blood." X-rays later: "nothing broken or out of place" but "clearly has arthritis."

## .crosses into a hazard when

the cat **won't bear weight at all**, a limb looks at a wrong angle, or the limp does not ease in a
few days → see the companion `../inventory.of=hazards.for=feline.case=restraint-struggle.md`.
