# p50.muscle-soreness-stiffness

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .symptom

sore, stiff muscles after a hard hold — careful movement, less jump, guards the body. like a human's
day-after ache from a strained muscle. often **worse the next day** as the inflammation peaks, which
is why a cat can seem fine right after the visit, then sore a day later.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p50** (common after a real struggle; an ordinal bucket, not a measured rate)
- **onset:** the visit day, often worse at 24 h
- **duration:** **~1–3 days**, eases with rest

## .anchor

owner-signal + a vet's read (a hazard-signal, not a fact), from
[r/AskVet — cat in extreme pain after blood draws](https://old.reddit.com/r/AskVet/comments/1ny0t5t/cat_in_extreme_pain_after_blood_draws/):
> the vet "believes he's just very sore from fighting them during blood draws" / "maybe sprained the
> muscles from fighting them when they drew blood."

## .crosses into a hazard when

the pain is severe, or the soreness does not ease past ~3 days → see the companion
`../inventory.of=hazards.for=feline.case=restraint-struggle.md`.
