# p10.persistent-limp

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .symptom

a limp that **outlasts** the normal 1–2 day soreness window, or that **begins days later** rather
than right after the draw. this is the pattern that does **not** fit a simple draw-site bruise.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p10** (uncommon for a draw alone; an ordinal bucket, not a measured rate)
- **onset:** persists past ~2 days, or delayed onset at day 2–5
- **duration:** open-ended — this is a flag, not a self-limited symptom

## .anchor

owner-signal (a hazard-signal, not a medical fact), from
[r/AskVet — cat limping and in pain after blood draw](https://old.reddit.com/r/AskVet/comments/1s56l2e/cat_limping_and_in_pain_after_blood_draw_emergency/):
> a delayed ~day-3 crash after a draw where a vein blew and both legs were wrapped — the RVN reply:
> "I would find it unusual for her to be ok for a few days after the blood draw and then develop the
> lameness on the leg."

the professional read (a re-attribution signal), from
[r/AskAVeterinarian](https://old.reddit.com/r/AskAVeterinarian/comments/1qt60ia/cat_limping_after_urgent_care_visit_reaction_to/):
> "While it may have coincidentally happened around the time of a blood draw I would not think they
> would be related."

## .crosses into a hazard when

a persistent/delayed limp is **already** the hazard threshold for this exposure — the draw alone
rarely explains it, so it points to a stacked cause (vaccine soreness, restraint strain, arthritis
flared by the struggle) or an unrelated issue. → book a vet re-exam; see the companion
`../inventory.of=hazards.for=feline.case=blood-draw-venipuncture.md` and the miki-case disentangle
`../2.2.3.itemize-inventory.yield.md`.
