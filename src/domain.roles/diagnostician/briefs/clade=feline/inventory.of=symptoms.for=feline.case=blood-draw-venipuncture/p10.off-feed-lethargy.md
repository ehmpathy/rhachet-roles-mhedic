# p10.off-feed-lethargy

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .symptom

a brief "shell-shocked" spell after the visit — quiet, off food/water, more sleep. usually the
stress of the restraint and the visit, not the needle itself; eases as the cat settles at home.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p10** (uncommon — happens, not the usual case; an ordinal bucket, not a measured rate)
- **onset:** the hours after the visit
- **duration:** **hours–~1 day**, then back to normal

## .anchor

owner-signal (a hazard-signal, not a medical fact), from
[r/AskVet — cat limping and in pain after blood draw](https://old.reddit.com/r/AskVet/comments/1s56l2e/cat_limping_and_in_pain_after_blood_draw_emergency/):
> "She seem pretty shell shocked for a few hours, but by the next day she seemed back to normal.
> Same with the day after that."

## .crosses into a hazard when

the cat still will not eat or drink past ~24 hours, or stays flat/hides → a general "get seen"
sign; see the companion `../inventory.of=hazards.for=feline.case=blood-draw-venipuncture.md`. (this
overlaps the vaccine-side signals when a visit stacked both — appetite/hydration is the symptom that
most earns a call regardless of cause.)
