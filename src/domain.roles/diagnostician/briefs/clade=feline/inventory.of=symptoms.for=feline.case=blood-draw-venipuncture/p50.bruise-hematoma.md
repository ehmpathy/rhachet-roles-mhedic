# p50.bruise-hematoma

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .symptom

a small bruise or soft/firm lump at the draw site, where a little blood leaked from the vein into
the tissue (a hematoma). tender to the touch; may look darker under the fur. more likely when a vein
"blows" or when pressure was not held long enough after the needle came out.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p50** (common — an inherent risk of any draw; an ordinal bucket, not a measured rate)
- **onset:** at / soon after the draw
- **duration:** fades over **days–~2 weeks** as the body reabsorbs the blood (like any bruise)

## .anchor

From [dvm360 — minimizing venipuncture-induced hematomas](https://www.dvm360.com/view/minimizing-venipuncture-induced-hematomas):
> "Formation of hematomas of varying size is an inherent risk associated with venipunctures. They
> are not only 'unsightly;' they become especially problematic in patients that require multiple
> diagnostic or therapeutic venipunctures."

From [NaturalPetsHQ — cat leg swollen after a blood draw](https://naturalpetshq.com/cat-leg-swollen-after-blood-draw/):
> "A swollen leg after a blood draw is usually the result of a small bruise under the skin called a
> hematoma. This happens when a little blood leaks out of the vein during or immediately after the
> draw."

## .crosses into a hazard when

the bruise is **large, hot, or spreads**, or there is discharge → see the companion
`../inventory.of=hazards.for=feline.case=blood-draw-venipuncture.md` (large hematoma / infection).
