# p50.injection-site-soreness

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .symptom

sore leg / limp — no jumps, slow or careful sits, favors the back legs. **bilateral in this case:**
both thighs were injected (FeLV left; FVRCP + rabies right), so a sore-both-back-legs pattern is
exactly what the exposure predicts. driven by the deliberate adjuvant inflammation at each site.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p50** (common — a routine, expected mild symptom; an ordinal bucket, not a measured rate)
- **onset:** hours–3 days after the shot
- **duration:** **~1–3 days**

## .anchor

From [AVMA — Vaccinating your pet](https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare/vaccinations):
> "Tenderness and swelling at the vaccination site / Tiredness / Mild fever / Decreased appetite ... These minor side effects usually go away on their own within 24 hours. If they last for more than a day or two ... contact your veterinarian."

From [International Cat Care](https://icatcare.org/articles/vaccinating-your-cat):
> "The most common side effects are mild, and include lethargy/tiredness, eating less or tenderness at the injection site - usually lasting no longer than a few days."
