# p50.mild-fever

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .symptom

mild fever — a slightly raised body temperature as the immune system responds. often shows only as
extra sleep and warmth.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p50** (common — a routine, expected mild symptom; an ordinal bucket, not a measured rate)
- **onset:** hours–48 h after the shot
- **duration:** **24–48 h**

## .anchor

From [PDSA — Cat and Kitten Vaccinations](https://www.pdsa.org.uk/pet-help-and-advice/pet-health-hub/other-veterinary-advice/cat-and-kitten-vaccinations):
> "they are likely to be mild (high temperature, low energy, and a reduced appetite), and pass within 24-48 hours."

From [AVMA — Vaccinating your pet](https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare/vaccinations):
> "Tenderness and swelling at the vaccination site / Tiredness / Mild fever / Decreased appetite"

## .crosses into a hazard when
