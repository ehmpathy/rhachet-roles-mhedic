# p50.pain-arthritis-posture

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .cause

**pain / arthritis / painful defecation posture** — the cat is physically able to pass stool but
*reluctant* because squatting or getting to the box hurts.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p50** (common, esp. in older cats; ordinal bucket)
- **causal-chain:** `musculoskeletal or perineal pain → reluctance to squat/defecate → feces held longer → dries & hardens → harder to pass (self-reinforcing)`

## .anchor

From [International Cat Care — Constipation in cats](https://icatcare.org/articles/constipation-in-cats):
> "Some cats may find getting in and out of the litter tray or moving into a position to pass faeces painful. An example of this is in older cats suffering from arthritis."

From [VCA Animal Hospitals — Constipation in Cats](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/constipation-in-cats):
> "Painful conditions that make it uncomfortable to pass stool (e.g., osteoarthritis)".

## .note

this is the **reluctance** pathway (vs the dehydration pathway). it self-reinforces: holding stool
dries it, which makes passing it hurt more. anal-sac disease or perineal pain acts the same way.
