# p20.pelvic-outflow-narrowing

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .cause

**extraluminal / pelvic outflow narrowing** — the colon or rectum is squeezed from *outside* by a
narrowed pelvic canal, mass, or hernia, so normal stool cannot pass.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p20** (a classic, identifiable structural cause; ordinal bucket)
- **causal-chain:** `narrowed pelvic inlet / external compression → passage physically restricted → feces back up → dry & impact`

## .anchor

From [Merck Veterinary Manual](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/digestive-system/diseases-of-the-large-intestine-in-small-animals/constipation-obstipation-and-megacolon-in-small-animals):
> "Extraluminal obstruction may be caused by compression of the colon or rectum from a narrowed pelvic inlet (caused by poorly healed pelvic fractures, enlarged sublumbar lymph nodes or prostate gland, or colonic stricture)."

From [VCA Animal Hospitals — Constipation in Cats](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/constipation-in-cats):
> "Pelvic injuries that result in a narrowed pelvic canal / Tumors or masses in the colon or rectum".

## .tell

feces squeezed through a narrowing may appear **thin or "ribbon-like."** a history of prior pelvic
trauma (a healed fracture from a fall/car) is a key clue — a cause the exam + a good history catch.
