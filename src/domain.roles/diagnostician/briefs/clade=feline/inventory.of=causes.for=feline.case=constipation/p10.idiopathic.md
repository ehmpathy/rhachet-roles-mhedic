# p10.idiopathic

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .cause

**idiopathic** — no specific cause found after appropriate workup. a diagnosis of *exclusion*, not
a first guess.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p10** as a *labelled* bucket here — but note it is only assigned **after** the
  others are ruled out; in the megacolon literature the idiopathic share of chronic cases is
  actually **high**. (ordinal bucket for "cause remains undetermined at the individual-cat level.")
- **causal-chain:** `unknown lever → (often) colonic hypomotility → retained, drying feces`

## .anchor

From [Cornell Feline Health Center — Constipation](https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/constipation):
> "In many cases, the specific cause of constipation in cats is undetermined, and in these cases, the condition is referred to as idiopathic constipation."

From [Merck Veterinary Manual](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/digestive-system/diseases-of-the-large-intestine-in-small-animals/constipation-obstipation-and-megacolon-in-small-animals):
> "Some diseases, such as megacolon in cats, are idiopathic."

## .why it is listed last

idiopathic is what remains **after** history (drugs, prior pelvic trauma), exam (pelvic canal,
neuro), and labs (electrolytes, kidney) fail to name a cause. it is a placeholder for "workup did
not localize," not an explanation — and it is why the correlation briefs must stay humble about
attributing a single exposure.
