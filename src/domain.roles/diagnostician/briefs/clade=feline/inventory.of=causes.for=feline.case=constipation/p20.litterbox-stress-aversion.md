# p20.litterbox-stress-aversion

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** consult a licensed veterinarian; if this looks
> like an emergency, call an emergency vet now.

## .cause

**litterbox aversion / stress / environmental** — the cat *withholds* defecation because the box
is dirty, contested, unpleasant to stand in, or the environment is stressful.

## .expectation

- **probability:** **p20** (a real, common behavioral contributor; ordinal bucket)
- **causal-chain:** `dirty/contested/aversive box or stress → cat holds stool → feces retained longer → dries & hardens`

## .anchor

From [Merck Veterinary Manual](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/digestive-system/diseases-of-the-large-intestine-in-small-animals/constipation-obstipation-and-megacolon-in-small-animals):
> "reluctance to defecate (due to stress, a dirty litter box, pain, or a tumor)."

From [International Cat Care — Constipation in cats](https://icatcare.org/articles/constipation-in-cats):
> "Their litter tray is dirty / There's competition for a litter tray with other cats in the household" ... "Wood pellet litter substrates can be hard and therefore painful for cats to stand on ... and lead to a litter tray aversion".

## .why it matters here

this is the **stress/withholding** pathway — directly relevant to the vaccine and blood-draw
correlation briefs, where the vet visit itself is the stressor that triggers withholding.
