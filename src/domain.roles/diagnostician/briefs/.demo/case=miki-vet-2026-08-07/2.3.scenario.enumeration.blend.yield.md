# 2.3.scenario.enumeration.blend.yield

> the single, deduplicated candidate lineup — the union of the imagination stream
> (`2.1.scenario.enumeration.from.imagination.yield.md`, SC1–SC12) and the literature stream
> (`2.2.scenario.enumeration.from.literature.yield.md`, LS1–LS14). each merged scenario carries a
> **provenance tag**: `both` · `imagination-only` · `literature-only`. the disagreement between the
> two streams is itself signal, per `howto.research-effect-socials-and-formals`.
>
> per `define.causal-chain-frame`: each chain reads
> `cause:exposure → cause:mechanism → effect:mechanism → effect:symptom`.
>
> **no weights yet** — the rank waits for phase 3. per `rule.require.superposition-until-elimination`,
> every branch stays live until an assay eliminates it; an `imagination-only` branch survives on
> severity even with zero corpus support (`absence-of-evidence ≠ evidence-of-absence`).
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.** the patient was not examined.

## .the effect to explain

bilateral hind-limb pain/limp (S1) + an **acquired "cannot" jump deficit** (S2, new post-visit) +
appetite loss (S3) + withdrawal (S4), all **dose-locked to an NSAID** (S5, onsior at 12 mg/day).

## .the blended lineup (B1–B20, provenance-tagged, unweighted)

### from the vaccines

- **B1 — injection-site local inflammation / soreness.** `both` (SC1 = LS1).
  `vaccine → local immune reaction in each thigh → myositis / soft-tissue pain → bilateral hind limp`.
  three streams converge (formal + professional + social). fits bilaterality + NSAID response.

- **B2 — systemic VAAE (malaise / fever / anorexia).** `both` (SC2 = LS2).
  `vaccine → systemic immune response → malaise + pyrexia → reduced appetite + limp cluster`.
  literature adds: risk scales with vaccine count (miki got 3 components), base rate ~52/10,000.

- **B3 — FCV limping syndrome / post-vaccinal polyarthritis.** `literature-sharpens` (LS4; imagination
  folded it loosely into SC2). the literature gives it its own distinct immune-mediated-polyarthritis
  mechanism. `FCV antigen (wild or vaccinal) → immune-mediated polyarthritis → multi-joint inflammation → migratory lameness ± pyrexia`.

- **B4 — vaccine delayed hypersensitivity (the NSAID-refractory worst case).** `both` (SC4 = LS3).
  `vaccine → type-I/III hypersensitivity → amplified local/systemic reaction → welt + pain an NSAID does not settle`.
  the literature supplies the concrete worst-case course (welt day 4, steroid needed).

- **B5 — injection-site sarcoma / musculoskeletal neoplasia (delayed cancer tail).** `both` (SC3 = LS5).
  `injection → chronic inflammatory focus → neoplastic transformation over months → mass + lameness`.
  low fit for *today's* acute pain; carried as a **delayed-hazard landmine** (a site lump weeks–months
  out), never eliminated by "too early".

### from the venipuncture

- **B6 — draw-site hematoma / soft-tissue bruise (transient).** `both` (SC5 = LS6).
  `needle in each hind leg → small subcutaneous hemorrhage → bruise pain → bilateral hind limp`.
  strong bilaterality fit; this is the owner's own hypothesis. literature marks it usually mild.

- **B7 — clipper / antiseptic / handle irritation at the draw leg (non-needle).** `literature-itemizes`
  (LS7; imagination left it inside SC5/SC12). `clip + swab + hold → superficial soft-tissue irritation → local soreness → mild limp`.

- **B8 — draw reads benign; a persistent limp points elsewhere (eliminative / near-null).**
  `literature-only` (LS8). the corpus vets repeatedly de-attribute a *persistent* limp from the needle
  — an **eliminative signal**, not a positive cause. weighs against B6/B7 as the cause of a multi-day limp.

### from the cystocentesis attempt

- **B9 — abdominal-wall / bladder-region pain.** `imagination-only` (SC7). **plausible but unattested**
  — no corpus support, leans on the assay. `cysto needle → abdominal/core discomfort → guarded posture → reluctance + reduced jump`.
  partial fit; weaker for the hind-limb-localized deficit.

### from the venipuncture — the can't-miss neuro branch

- **B10 — iatrogenic nerve injury (can't-miss).** `imagination-only` (SC6). **plausible but unattested,
  high-severity — survives on severity.** `needle near a hind-limb nerve → nerve trauma → neuro deficit → a true "cannot" (not "won't") jump loss`.
  the **S2 "cannot" quality** points at a motor deficit, not mere pain. corpus has zero reports (owners
  who post lack this diagnosis) — exactly the imagination-stream blind-spot cover.

### from the restraint / struggle

- **B11 — restraint / struggle soft-tissue sprain (usually transient).** `both` (SC8-mild = LS9).
  `forcible restraint + the cat fights it → muscle/ligament sprain → soft-tissue pain → limp, mostly eases in 1–2 days`.

- **B12 — restraint fracture / luxation (severe extension, can't-miss).** `imagination-only` (SC8-severe;
  literature reports only the mild sprain arm). **plausible but unattested, high-severity — survives on
  severity.** `forcible restraint / struggle → fracture or luxation of a hind limb / pelvis → acute pain + deficit`.
  a high-severity "cannot"-deficit candidate.

- **B13 — restraint spinal / back injury (can't-miss).** `imagination-only` (SC9). **plausible but
  unattested, high-severity — survives on severity.** `struggle against restraint → spinal/soft-tissue back injury → bilateral hind paresis + pain`.
  bilateral hind neuro deficit = high severity.

### the prior-disease branch

- **B14 — prior / latent arthritis unmasked or flared by the visit.** `literature-only` (LS10;
  imagination missed it entirely). the corpus vets reach for arthritis when a limp persists atypically.
  `handle + restraint stress on a joint with sub-clinical degenerative disease → flare of prior arthritis → joint pain → lameness that outlasts a soreness window`.

### from the drug itself (onsior / robenacoxib)

- **B15 — NSAID (onsior) adverse effect, amplified by an above-label dose.** `literature-only` (LS11;
  imagination treated onsior only as a confounder that masks pain, S5). the literature makes the drug
  its own hazard branch. the intake flagged miki at **12 mg/day** vs the 6 mg feline label unit.
  `robenacoxib above the label unit → COX-2 strain on kidney/GI → renal or GI adverse effect → new illness signs (NOT the limp)`.

### the systemic alternative (NOT caused by the visit procedures)

- **B16 — feline aortic thromboembolism (ATE / "saddle thrombus") (the top landmine).**
  `imagination-only` (SC10). **plausible but unattested in the corpus (zero ATE reports), catastrophic,
  time-critical — survives on severity, ranks near the top on veto.** `prior cardiac disease (the respiratory-distress episodes that prompted the probnp) → intracardiac thrombus → aortic embolus → acute bilateral hind-limb ischemia → severe pain + "cannot" use hind limbs`.
  a cat with cardiac suspicion + acute bilateral hind-limb pain + deficit is ATE until proven
  otherwise. the NSAID response does **not** clear it (analgesia can blunt ATE pain).

- **B17 — other systemic / metabolic process** (hypokalemic weakness, early neuromuscular disease).
  `imagination-only` (SC11, broad). a catch-all severe branch, low-anchored, kept live.

### the downstream complication (a consequence of the anorexia, not a cause of the limp)

- **B18 — anorexia → feline hepatic lipidosis cascade (can't-miss, downstream).** `literature-only`
  (LS12; imagination missed it). it does **not** explain the limp; it is the time-sensitive hazard that
  the appetite-loss (S3) opens. `reduced appetite for days → fat mobilized to a cat liver that handles it poorly → hepatic lipidosis → grave systemic illness`.

### the benign baseline + the null control

- **B19 — cumulative procedural soreness (benign anchor).** `both` (SC12 = LS13).
  `many procedures in one visit (2 vaccines + 2 draws + cysto) → summed minor soft-tissue soreness → transient limp`.
  the "generally sore" first read; self-limited, NSAID-responsive.

- **B20 — limp predates the visit (non-exposure null control).** `literature-only` (LS14).
  `a limp already present before any procedure → the visit is coincidental, not causal`. the honest null.

## .the flagged branches (what each stream uniquely contributes)

- **imagination-only, plausible-but-unattested.** all six lean on the assay (superposition keeps an
  unattested branch live until an assay eliminates it), but they split by *survival mode*:
  - **severity-protected can't-miss** — stay ranked-high even at low probability because a miss is
    catastrophic: **B10 (nerve injury)**, **B12 (fracture/luxation)**, **B13 (spinal)**,
    **B16 (ATE — the top landmine)**. severity is what holds their rank.
  - **assay-dependent, non-can't-miss** — kept live by superposition until the assay speaks, but not
    severity-protected in rank: **B9 (cysto abdominal pain, moderate)**, **B17 (other
    systemic/metabolic, broad catch-all)**. they lean on the assay to charge or clear them.

  the corpus contains **zero** reports of the severe neuro/vascular branches because owners who post
  online do not have those diagnoses — this is exactly the logic-first blind-spot cover the imagination
  stream exists to provide.

- **literature-only, a logic blind-spot worth a second look (imagination missed or under-specified):**
  B3 (FCV limping syndrome — its own mechanism), B7 (clipper/swab irritation), B8 (draw-benign
  eliminative signal), **B14 (prior arthritis flare)**, **B15 (NSAID as its own hazard)**,
  **B18 (anorexia → hepatic lipidosis)**, B20 (limp-predates-visit null). the corpus caught real
  branches first-principles logic under-reported.
