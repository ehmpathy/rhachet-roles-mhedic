# 9.2.treatment.superposition.conditional.yield

> **prescriber stone.** the **conditional branches** — scenario-specific treatments held in reserve,
> each keyed to the exact phase-4 assay result that would confirm its scenario. not started now;
> pre-decided, so the correct care is ready the moment the assay collapses the superposition.
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.** miki was not examined.
>
> **recommendation disclaimer.** every conditional order below is offered to the best of our
> knowledge from the information found in research — it carries **no guarantee** of outcome, and each
> (especially any dose) must be reviewed and set by a **licensed veterinarian** against miki's own
> real, hands-on-examined weight and scenario before any use. this is groundwork for that review, not
> a substitute.

## .the inputs

- `8.1.diagnosis.superposition.ranked.yield.md` — the ranked live scenarios + packages.
- `4.3.scenario.elimination.assay.plan.yield.md` — the result → elimination map (the triggers).

## .how to read a conditional order

each order is **"if [assay X] confirms [scenario Z] → then [treatment Y] + [prevention rider]"**,
keyed to the exact `4.3` result. the no-regret core (9.1) runs underneath all of these; a conditional
order is the *scenario-specific addition* once the assay collapses that branch.

## .the veto-driver conditionals (catastrophic — confirm → emergency care + prophylaxis)

### B16 — feline ATE
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 1 — distal-limb perfusion **cold / absent pulse / cyanotic, or any doubt**.
- **then:** act-as-if-ATE **now** — emergency referral; thrombolytic / anticoagulant + analgesia is a
  clinician's emergency decision. prognosis is guarded-to-poor (~37% survive the initial event, PMID
  8581544; "guarded to poor prognosis", PMID 16594585).
- **prevention rider:** if a cardiac cause is confirmed, **clopidogrel** for secondary prophylaxis —
  it cut recurrence vs aspirin and lengthened time-to-recurrence (443 vs 192 days, FATCAT PMID
  26776588). **dose not captured here — vet to set per the FATCAT protocol.** note: only 15% of CATE
  cats had prior recorded heart disease (PMID 26776588), so an absent cardiac history does not clear it.
- source: `accrue/inventory.of=aortic-thromboembolism.feline.md` (bhrowser, 2026-08-12).

### B13 — spinal / cord event
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 1 neuro **deficit / equivocal**, or `4.3` step 5 radiograph **instability /
  lesion**.
- **then:** no manipulation until cord + bone cleared; neuro-localize + radiographs; a decompression
  window (hours) is a clinician's emergency call.
- **prevention rider:** avoid manipulation-under-sedation before the radiograph gate clears (`4.3`
  tier-2 gate).
- source: `6.1` veto anchors; `4.3` step 5 spinal-stability gate.

### B5 — injection-site sarcoma (FISS)
- **trigger:** a firm injection-site mass that meets the **3-2-1 rule** (present > 3 months, > 2 cm,
  or grows within 1 month).
- **then:** biopsy → **wide surgical excision with clean margins**, which gives the best survival.
  > formal, PMID 28716129 (bhrowser, 2026-08-12): clean surgical margins are associated with the best
  > FISS outcome.
- **prevention rider:** site rotation + the 3-2-1 monitor from here on (Kass, PMID 29217316); avoid a
  same-site repeat injection.
- source: `accrue/inventory.of=injection-site-sarcoma.feline.md`.

## .the severe conditionals (confirm → scenario care + rider)

### B12 — fracture / luxation
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 5 radiograph **fracture / luxation** (or step-1 crepitus/instability).
- **then:** immobilize + orthopedic referral; hold weight-load.
- **prevention rider:** strict rest continues; gentle handle at future visits.
- source: `6.3` scenario care; `4.3` step 5.

### B10 — iatrogenic nerve injury
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 1 neuro **deficit** with a clean radiograph (no bone/cord lesion).
- **then:** time + supportive care; a neuro baseline anchors the recovery track. prognosis is guarded
  — 7/27 cats showed no improvement over a year (PMID 17614928).
  > formal, PMID 17614928 (bhrowser, 2026-08-12): IM injection was a cause in 1 cat; 13/27 recovered
  > fully within a year, 7/27 showed no improvement.
- **prevention rider:** injection-site technique (avoid the sciatic path) from here on.
- source: `accrue/inventory.of=iatrogenic-nerve-injury.feline.md`.

### B9 — uroabdomen tail
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 2 **azotemia / free fluid present** (the dry-tap baseline argues against,
  but a breach is not excluded).
- **then:** abdominocentesis + fluid analysis (creatinine gradient); IV fluids + stabilization; it is
  a medical emergency if a breach is confirmed (azotemia/hyperkalemia climb fast).
  > formal, PMID 23470168 (bhrowser, 2026-08-12): uroabdomen is a medical emergency with azotemia,
  > hyperkalemia, and urosepsis risk; prognosis follows the extent of injury.
- **prevention rider:** careful cysto technique.
- source: `accrue/inventory.of=uroabdomen.feline.md`.

### B18 — hepatic lipidosis
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 2 liver enzymes **elevated**, OR appetite drops past a day or two.
- **then:** assisted nutrition (the cornerstone of lipidosis care) + fluid support.
- **prevention rider:** maintain caloric intake; appetite watch continues.
- source: `6.1`/`6.2` hepatic-lipidosis bound.

### B15 — NSAID above-label (harm track)
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 3 onsior mg/kg **> target (~1 mg/kg)** or **unknown**, OR step 2 renal
  values **azotemic**.
- **then:** correct the dose; hold further NSAID; renal/hepatic support if a value is deranged.
- **prevention rider:** dose to label (robenacoxib ~1 mg/kg PO q24h, up to the labeled course) with
  baseline labs — see `accrue/inventory.of=robenacoxib-onsior.feline.md` (FDA label; PMID 38587872).
- source: `accrue/inventory.of=robenacoxib-onsior.feline.md`.

### B2 — vaccine-associated systemic reaction
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 1 **fever**, OR step 4 CBC **inflammatory leukogram**, OR bilateral + home
  systemic signs.
- **then:** NSAID (if renal-safe) + supportive care; monitor for escalation.
- **prevention rider:** site rotation; note the vaccine + site for future reactions.
- source: `6.2`/`6.3` VAAE care.

### B17 — systemic / metabolic
- **trigger:** `4.3` step 2 metabolic panel **derangement**.
- **then:** direct workup to the derangement found; treat the confirmed process.
- **prevention rider:** per the confirmed disease.
- source: `6.1` broad-screen bound.

## .the no-regret-covered branches (no separate conditional needed)

B14 (DJD flare), B11 (soft-tissue), B1 (injection reaction), B3 (FCV lameness), B4 (anaphylaxis,
window closed) confirm into **the 9.1 no-regret core** (rest + gabapentin + monitor); their
"conditional order" is simply to continue that supportive care, with the NSAID as a renal-safe upgrade
once baseline labs clear (the B15 gate). no scenario-specific reserve treatment is required.

## .done

every live scenario has a disposition: the veto-drivers (B16/B13/B5) and the severe branches
(B12/B10/B9/B18/B15/B2/B17) each carry a conditional order keyed to a specific `4.3` assay result,
cited with dose/route + label-target caveat where a dose is inferred (or marked "vet to set" where a
dose was not captured), and each carries its prevention rider (`6.2`); the no-regret branches confirm
into the 9.1 core. the recommendation disclaimer is carried. this feeds phase 9.3 (the veto).
