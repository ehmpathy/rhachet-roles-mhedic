# 3.1.scenario.disentangle.correlation.literature.yield

> the **base-rate half** of the disentangle: each blended scenario (B1–B20 from
> `2.3.scenario.enumeration.blend.yield.md`) weighted against the case's exposures from **formal ground
> truth only** — controlled incidence, honest denominators. the anecdote half is bracketed out on
> purpose (it is phase 3.2); the blend of the two is phase 3.3.
>
> honest-denominator rule: a base rate is (events / population), not an anecdote tally. where the formal
> corpus supplies a real denominator, it is quoted; where it does not, the **absence is stated as the
> result** rather than filled with a fabricated number (`rule.require.bhrowser-citations` forbids an
> uncited rate).
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.** these are population findings, not a
> diagnosis of this patient.

## .the one clean formal denominator: the vaccine arm

- **B1 (injection-site local reaction) + B2 (systemic VAAE).** formal population base rate: **51.6
  VAAEs per 10,000 cats vaccinated** (~0.5%), and **miki sits in the single highest-risk cell** the
  literature names — ~1 year old + multiple concurrent vaccines, with lethargy ± fever the most common
  presentation.
  > formal, PMID 17605670 (Moore 2007, 2,560 VAAEs / 1,258,712 doses / 496,189 cats): "**51.6
  > VAAEs/10,000 cats vaccinated** … risk **significantly increased as the number of vaccines
  > administered per office visit increased** … **greatest for cats approximately 1 year old** …
  > **Lethargy with or without fever was the most commonly diagnosed VAAE.**"
  > formal, PMID 39965621: "the **risk of VAAEs increases with the number of applied vaccines per
  > visit**."
  the base rate is low in absolute terms (~0.5%), but miki is in the top-risk stratum, so the
  case-conditional weight is materially higher than the population rate — this is the only branch where
  formal data both **anchors the mechanism and quantifies the denominator**.
  **denominator caveat:** 51.6/10,000 is the **aggregate all-VAAE** rate (any adverse event / all
  vaccinated cats); it is **not split** into local (B1) vs systemic (B2) mechanisms. so the single
  figure is a shared *upper* anchor for the two branches, not a mechanism-specific rate for either —
  B1 and B2 each own some fraction of it, undetermined by the source.

## .formal entity, named but without a clean population denominator

- **B3 (FCV limping syndrome / post-vaccinal polyarthritis).** documented entity, frequency stated only
  qualitatively ("occasionally"), with a demonstrated lag of up to ~12 days.
  > formal, PMID 35511315 (Balboni 2022): "the limping syndrome is **occasionally reported** during
  > acute FCV infections or **as consequence of vaccination**."
  > formal, PMID 37889723 (Lanave 2023): a limping-syndrome outbreak monitored with a **12-day lag**.
  honest denominator: **no controlled incidence** — "occasionally" is not a rate. the caveat: classic
  FCV limping is typically multi-limb ± fever, while miki's picture is hind-limb-localized, so the
  formal source proves the *category*, not *this* mechanism.

- **B4 (vaccine hypersensitivity / anaphylaxis).** the severe tail exists but the quoted figure is a
  **proportion of severe reactions, not a population base rate**.
  > formal, PMID 34789596: **130/316 severe reactions were anaphylaxis**.
  honest denominator: the 316 is a severe-reaction cohort, not the vaccinated population, so this bounds
  the *shape* of the severe tail, not its per-cat rate.

- **B5 (injection-site sarcoma / FISS).** named as an important differential for lameness; **no numeric
  incidence in the corpus**.
  > formal, PMID 22247324: an "**important differential** for … **lameness** … FISS … osteosarcoma."
  honest denominator: rate not established from the corpus; carried as a **delayed-hazard** branch on
  mechanism + severity, not on a base rate.

- **B18 (anorexia → hepatic lipidosis).** a mechanism with grave outcome, stated without a
  per-anorexic-cat incidence.
  > formal, PMID 29478399 + PMID 28978714: HL follows **prolonged anorexia**, grave if missed.
  honest denominator: no population rate for "limp-case anorexia → HL"; this is a **downstream
  time-hazard**, weighted by consequence, not by a base rate.

## .the informative absence of formal literature (a real formal result)

a documented negative search is itself base-rate signal: an event too **minor/transient** to have
generated controlled literature is, by that absence, argued **against** as the driver of a limp that is
*worse at day 2*.

- **B6 (draw hematoma) + B7 (clipper/swab irritation).** a pubmed search
  (`venipuncture complications hematoma cat`) returned **no cat-specific study of post-venipuncture
  lameness** — the hits were tangential (human thrombosis, fluid-therapy complications).
  > formal (negative result, 2026-08-10): no cat-specific post-venipuncture-lameness literature.
  honest read (formal-only): the absence of controlled literature means the formal layer supplies **no
  base rate** for this branch — it does not (here) establish the event's magnitude in either direction.
  the branch drops to **anecdote-level**, to be weighted in phase 3.2. (any "minor / transient" read
  of the draw injury comes from the anecdote sources and is deliberately withheld from this formal half
  — bracket, don't pool.)

- **B11 (restraint / struggle sprain).** **no peer-reviewed anchor** for "lameness from restraint" in
  cats.
  > formal (negative result, 2026-08-10): no formal veterinary literature for restraint-lameness.
  honest read: anecdote-level only; formal base rate absent.

## .literature-only branches with no formal denominator in the corpus

- **B8 (draw-benign eliminative)** — not a positive cause; an eliminative signal, no base rate applies.
- **B14 (prior arthritis flare)** — the corpus attests it only through clinician anecdote, not a formal
  feline-DJD prevalence figure; **no formal base rate accrued**. carried to 3.2 as anecdote-level.
- **B15 (NSAID onsior adverse / above-label)** — the **label limit is formal** (the FDA label, not a
  professional-tier vet site), and the hazard mechanism is formal, but **no population adverse-event
  rate** is in the corpus.
  > formal, FDA label (DailyMed — ONSIOR robenacoxib 6 mg Tablets for Cats, bhrowser-captured
  > 2026-08-08): "**6 mg Tablets for Cats … For Oral Use In Cats Only … not scored** … The dose … is
  > **1 mg/kg orally once daily, for a maximum of 3 days** … **not to exceed one dose per day** …
  > **Appetite should be monitored in cats receiving ONSIOR** … The use of ONSIOR **in cats with
  > cardiac disease has not been studied**. ONSIOR **has been shown to prolong the QT interval**."
  > formal, PMID 38587872 (2024 ISFM/AAFP): screen before prescribe; **CKD common in cats**; monitor.
  honest denominator: the above-label *exposure* is a formal fact — miki at **12 mg/day** exceeds the
  single 6 mg once-daily unit for a cat of her size ("not to exceed one dose per day") — but its
  adverse-*event* rate is not a formal number. two label facts also bear on this case directly:
  **appetite must be monitored** (ties to S3 anorexia) and **cardiac use is unstudied + QT-prolonging**
  (a formal caution beside the B16 cardiac/ATE concern).
- **B19 (cumulative procedural soreness)** — expected-common by construction; no controlled rate.
- **B20 (limp predates the visit)** — a null control; base rate not applicable.

## .no formal base rate; carried on severity, NOT on rate

the corpus contains **zero** formal incidence for these — they are the severe imagination-only branches
that formal veterinary literature under-reports (owners and cohort studies do not capture rare iatrogenic
or catastrophic events for this exact context). per `rule.require.superposition-until-elimination` they
carry on **severity**, not base rate, and must not be down-weighted for the absent denominator
(`absence-of-evidence ≠ evidence-of-absence`):

- **B9** (cysto abdominal pain), **B10** (iatrogenic nerve injury), **B12** (restraint
  fracture/luxation), **B13** (restraint spinal injury), **B16** (feline ATE — the top landmine),
  **B17** (other systemic/metabolic).

## .what the base-rate layer settles (and what it cannot)

- **it settles one point firmly:** the vaccine arm (B1/B2) is the only branch with a real population
  denominator, and miki sits in its highest-risk cell — so on formal base rate alone, the vaccine arm
  leads.
- **it cannot settle the rest:** every other branch is either qualitative-formal, an informative
  absence (anecdote-level), or a severity-carried gap. the thinness of the formal layer is the result
  — it is *why* the anecdote half (3.2) and the severity logic (superposition) carry real weight in the
  blend (3.3). no anecdote signal was mixed in here.
