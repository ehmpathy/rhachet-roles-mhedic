## .the envelope principle

acuity is **not** set by the most probable scenario — it is set by the most **time-critical
un-eliminated** one. a `P4 very-low` scenario whose worst-case deteriorates in hours sets a higher
floor than a `P1 primary` scenario whose worst-case is a slow, reversible flare. the floor is the
**max** over live scenarios of "how fast could this go bad," not the mean.

## .the emergency floor

**the acuity floor is EMERGENCY-until-cleared, forced by B16 (feline ATE).**

- B16 is `P4 very-low` on probability — but its worst-case (death within hours; only ~37% survive the
  initial event, PMID 8581544; "guarded to poor prognosis," PMID 16594585) is the most time-critical
  bound in the live set. by the envelope principle, **it sets the floor regardless of its low rank.**
- B13 (acute cord event) reinforces the same floor from a second time-critical branch.
- the floor is **"emergency-until-cleared,"** not "emergency-forever": the no-regret **perfusion check**
  (limbs warm, pulses present) clears B16, and the **neuro check** clears B13. once both are confirmed
  by a clinician, the floor drops to urgent/routine. until then — and miki was **not examined** — the
  envelope holds the floor at emergency.

> the case facts *lean* benign (the limp preceded onsior and onsior relieves it — a dose-reversible,
> inflammatory picture; no cold-limb reported). but "leans benign" is not "cleared": absent a
> perfusion + neuro exam, the time-critical branches stay live, so the envelope floor stays emergency.

## .the red-line go-now signs (from the worst-case bounds)

seek emergency care immediately if miki shows any of these — each maps to a time-critical worst-case:

- **a cold or limp leg, or a bluish / pale paw pad, or absent pulses** → ATE (B16)
- **a leg dragged, or held at an odd/awkward angle** → spinal cord event or fracture (B13 / B12)
- **labored or open-mouth breath, or sudden collapse / weakness** → ATE / cardiac decompensation (B16)
- **a sudden cry of pain, or refusal to bear any weight** → acute structural / vascular event
- **appetite loss that persists for more than a day or two** → the hepatic-lipidosis clock starts (B18)
