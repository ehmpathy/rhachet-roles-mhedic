## .the frame (what a "bound" is)

each survivor carries two cited bounds:

- **best-case** = the benign, self-limited, or fully-recoverable trajectory **if the scenario is
  true**. it sets how little a miss can cost.
- **worst-case** = the catastrophic or permanent trajectory **if the scenario is true and goes
  badly**. it sets how much a miss can cost — and for a catastrophic branch, it is the exact fact the
  phase-9 veto rides.

superposition holds: a `P4 very-low` catastrophic keeps its worst-case bound live for the veto,
regardless of its probability bracket.

## .the catastrophic veto anchors (stated plainly, with citations)

these are the worst-case bounds that phase 9 must not step on — a treatment that could worsen any of
these is vetoed until the branch is cleared:

- **B16 — ATE**: an acute embolic event; only **~37% survive** the initial episode (PMID 8581544),
  "**guarded to poor prognosis**" (PMID 16594585). the tell is a **cold, limp leg or bluish paw pad**
  with absent pulses (`inventory.of=lameness-emergency-redflags.feline.md`). **veto**: no assumption of
  benign soft-tissue pain until perfusion is confirmed.
- **B13 — spinal / cord**: a leg dragged or held at an odd angle is a can't-miss red-flag
  (`inventory.of=lameness-emergency-redflags.feline.md`). **veto**: no spinal manipulation until a
  cord event is cleared.
- **B5 — FISS**: a malignant, recurrence-prone tumour, fatal if uncontrolled (PMID 28716129).
  **veto**: any **persistent post-injection mass** earns a work-up, never a watch-and-forget.
- **B10 — nerve injury** (severe): permanent monoparesis is possible, "poor prognosis" (PMID
  17614928). **veto**: no repeat caudal-thigh insult to an already-deficit limb until neuro is clean.
- **B12 — fracture/luxation** (severe): a leg at an awkward angle is a red-flag
  (`inventory.of=lameness-emergency-redflags.feline.md`). **veto**: no forced ambulation until imaged.
