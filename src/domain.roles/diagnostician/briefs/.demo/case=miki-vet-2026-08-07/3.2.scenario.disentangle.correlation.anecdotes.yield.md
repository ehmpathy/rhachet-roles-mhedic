# 3.2.scenario.disentangle.correlation.anecdotes.yield

> the **anecdote half** of the disentangle: each blended scenario (B1–B20) weighted against the case's
> exposures from the **social/owner-report corpus** gathered in phase 2 — forward and backtrack
> correlations over the itemized threads. this half is **biased and high-recall** (owners post *because*
> there is a problem), so every forward number is an **upper bound, never a rate**. it is bracketed
> apart from the formal base rate (3.1) on purpose; the blend is 3.3.
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.** these are internet owner reports, not a
> diagnosis. every thread below was bhrowser-verified in phase 2 (see
> `2.2.scenario.enumeration.from.literature.yield.md` social citations + the `accrue/` social rows).

## .forward correlation — P(effect | exposure) over the corpus (UPPER BOUND)

> forward = of the posted cases with an exposure, the fraction where a limp/adverse effect was
> reported. **selection bias inflates every value toward 1** (owners post the problem cases), so each is
> an upper bound on the true conditional, not a base rate.

> ⚠️ **why the bound reads "≤1.0" and why that cap is vacuous.** a probability is *always* ≤1.0, so
> "≤1.0" is not a computed bound — it is the honest admission that this corpus **cannot bound the
> forward below 1 at all**. the reason: the denominator is absent. a real P(effect|exposure) needs the
> count of cats *exposed but with no limp*, and those owners **do not post** — so the "exposed, no
> effect" cell is structurally empty. the forward is therefore not "high", it is **uncomputable** from
> anecdotes; "≤1.0" marks *un-boundable*, not *near-certain*. the informative signal is the outcome
> distribution (below), never the numerator.

| exposure | posted cases | limp/effect reported (⚠️ =1 by construction, NOT a rate) | forward (upper bound = uncomputable ≤1.0) | note |
|----------|--------------|----------------------|-------------------------------------------|------|
| vaccine | A1, A2 | 2/2 | **uncomputable** (≤1.0 vacuous; no "exposed, no-limp" denominator) | A1 escalated (steroid); severe tail exists in the corpus |
| blood draw | A3, A4, A5 | 3/3 reported, but **2/3 de-attributed** | **uncomputable** (≤1.0 vacuous); the de-attributions push the *outcome* read low, not the numerator | the corpus's own vets argue the draw is *not* the driver |
| restraint | A6, A7, A8 | 3/3 | **uncomputable** (≤1.0 vacuous); course usually mild/self-limited | A8 escalates to prior-arthritis, not restraint injury |
| multi-procedure (benign) | A9 | 1/1 | **uncomputable** (≤1.0 vacuous) | expected-benign, "gone in 72h" |

⚠️ **the "limp/effect reported" fractions (2/2, 3/3, 1/1) are the sneakiest rate-looking numbers here —
and they are NOT rates.** each is exactly 1 **by construction**: every thread entered the corpus
*because* it reported a limp, so the numerator equals the denominator for every exposure automatically.
"3/3" does not mean "100% of restraint cases limped" — it means "all 3 posted restraint threads were, of
course, limp posts." read these as *how many threads the corpus holds per exposure*, never as an
attack rate.

the forward numbers are **structurally ≈1** for every exposure — that is the selection bias, not a
signal. the *informative* content is in the **outcomes column**, not the numerator: the draw threads
self-de-attribute (A4/A5), the restraint threads self-limit or re-point to arthritis (A6/A8), and the
vaccine thread carries the only escalation-to-treatment case (A1).

### the informative forward — outcome-graded, not incidence-graded

since the raw P(limp|exposure) ≈ 1 carries no signal, the recoverable forward signal is the
**outcome distribution** within each exposure's problem-posts — how those limps *resolved*:

| exposure | escalated (tx needed) | self-limited | de-attributed by clinician | outcome-forward read |
|----------|-----------------------|--------------|----------------------------|----------------------|
| vaccine | A1 (steroid) | A2 (owner-managed) | — | **carries a real severe tail** |
| blood draw | — | A3 (1–2 days) | A4, A5 | **mostly self-limited or not-the-cause** |
| restraint | — | A6 | A7/A8 → arthritis | **self-limited, or re-points to prior disease** |
| multi/benign | — | A9 (72h) | — | benign |

this graded view is the honest forward contribution: not "does exposure X cause a limp" (always yes in a
problem-corpus), but "when an owner posts a limp after X, how does it end" — which separates the
severe-tail exposure (vaccine, A1) from the self-limited/de-attributed ones (draw, restraint).

⚠️ **still an upper bound, not a rate.** the outcome grades are counts over the *same* selection-skewed
problem-posts (n=1–3 per exposure), so they carry the identical bias as the raw forward — an owner who
escalated to a vet is *more* likely to post than one whose limp faded. read the grades as **which
outcomes are present in the corpus** (a severe tail *exists* for vaccine; de-attributions *exist* for
draw), never as **how often** each outcome occurs. the presence of A1's steroid case proves a severe
tail is possible; it does not put a rate on it. small-N + selection bias = direction only.

## .backtrack correlation — P(exposure | effect) over the corpus

> backtrack = given the effect (a post-visit limp in a cat), which exposure the corpus most often
> attributes it to. this is the less-biased direction (all rows already have the effect).

**denominator = 9, not 10 (why A10 is dropped):** the backtrack conditions on the effect *post-visit
limp*. A10's limp **predated** the visit — it is a different effect class (a pre-visit limp), so it
cannot inform P(exposure | post-visit limp) and is excluded from the 9. it is not lost: A10 remains the
**null control** in the forward direction (it shows a limp with no exposure at all, the coincidence
baseline). so the two directions use two honest, different denominators — forward counts all posted
cases per exposure; backtrack counts only the 9 whose effect matches the conditioned effect.

| exposure attributed | threads (of the 9 non-null) | backtrack share | note |
|---------------------|------------------------------|-----------------|------|
| restraint / struggle | A6, A7, A8 | 3/9 | the largest single attributed bucket |
| blood draw | A3, A4, A5 | 3/9 | but 2 of 3 are **de-attributions** (net-negative signal) |
| vaccine | A1, A2 | 2/9 | includes the one severe (steroid) case |
| prior arthritis (unmasked) | A7, A8 | 2/9 (overlap) | the corpus's recurrent "second look" when a limp persists |
| benign/multi | A9 | 1/9 | — |

**non-exclusive caveat:** these buckets **do not partition** — a single thread can attribute to more
than one exposure (A7 counts in *both* restraint and prior-arthritis; A4/A8 is one thread across draw
and arthritis). so the shares **do not sum to 1** and a `3/9` is a *representation count*, not a
probability. read them as relative prominence within a biased corpus, not as P(exposure|effect) in any
population sense.

backtrack read: restraint and draw tie on raw representation, but the draw bucket is **hollowed out** by
its own de-attributions, so the net anecdote weight lands on **restraint** and, for a *persistent* limp,
on **prior-arthritis-unmasked** — the branch the formal half (3.1) had no denominator for.

## .the confounds (do NOT attribute a mixed case to one exposure)

- **A9** stacked multiple procedures in one visit — it cannot be assigned to vaccine vs draw vs
  restraint; it weights only "cumulative soreness," not any single arm.
- **miki's own visit is itself a mixed exposure** (2 vaccines + 2 draws + cysto + restraint, same day) —
  so the corpus's *single-exposure* threads (A1–A8) can each only raise a *contributor*, never crown a
  sole cause. the anecdote half cannot disentangle co-present exposures; that is 3.3's job with the
  formal bracket alongside.
- **A4 = A8** (one r/AskVet thread carries both a draw-de-attribution and an arthritis re-point) — it is
  one source, counted once per distinct signal, not double-weighted as two cases.
- **A7 is a within-thread confound** — the vet quote attributes the limp to *both* restraint-struggle
  ("sprained … from fighting") *and* prior arthritis ("bruising … arthritis") in the same thread. so A7
  must NOT be read as a clean restraint case even though it sits in the restraint forward row (A6,A7,A8):
  its cause is co-present restraint + prior-onset joint disease, not restraint alone. it is a
  *contributor* to both the restraint and prior-arthritis buckets, never a sole-cause vote for either —
  which is exactly why the backtrack shares are flagged non-exclusive above.
- **temporal confound (delayed onset) — A4, A6:** a limp that appears *days after* the exposure, not
  hours, admits an **interposed cause** in the gap — so the exposure→effect link weakens with the delay.
  A4's own vet de-attributes *because* of it ("ok for days then lame → points elsewhere"); A6 is a
  "delayed notice" that was "way better next day". this is a distinct confound *class* from
  exposure-blend: even a single-exposure thread cannot cleanly attribute a *delayed* limp to the
  procedure. so A4/A6's delayed-onset limps are contributors at most, and A4's delay is itself the
  clinician's stated reason to look elsewhere — the corpus supplies its own temporal-confound control.

## .the confound taxonomy (four distinct classes, all handled above)

| class | what it is | threads | how handled |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| selection | owners post problem cases → forward ≈1 | all | forward flagged uncomputable/upper-bound |
| exposure-blend | one visit stacks many arms | A9, miki's visit | contributor-only, never sole cause |
| dual-attribution | one thread names two causes | A4=A8, A7 | counted in both buckets, non-exclusive |
| temporal (delayed onset) | effect days later admits an interposed cause | A4, A6 | delayed limps are contributors at most |

## .what the anecdote layer adds (and its blind spots)

- **adds:** a real severe-vaccine case (A1, steroid), strong support for the **draw-eliminative** branch
  (B8, via A4/A5), and the **prior-arthritis** second-look (B14) that the formal layer could not
  quantify. restraint (B11) is the largest raw attributed bucket.
- **blind:** the corpus has **zero** rows for the can't-miss severe/iatrogenic/drug/systemic branches
  (B10, B12, B13, B15, B16, B17, B18) — anecdote silence here is selection bias, not evidence of
  absence; those branches are untouched by this half and stay live on severity.
- every forward figure is upper-bound-flagged; confounds (mixed exposures) are called out; no mixed case
  is attributed to a single exposure.

## .done

each corpus thread is itemized (A1–A10, tagged exposure/effect/outcome); forward P(effect|exposure) and
backtrack P(exposure|effect) are computed per exposure and mapped to B1–B20; every forward number is
flagged an upper bound; co-occurrence confounds are noted; and the corpus's blind spots (the severe
branches) are marked so 3.3 does not read anecdote-silence as absence.
