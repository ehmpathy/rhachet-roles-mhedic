## .the two-axis rule (why probability and severity stay separate)

a single blended score would let a strong probability-attack sink a catastrophic branch off the board —
the exact absence-drop 5.2 forbids. so this refine keeps **two axes**:

- **probability** (ordinal bracket): `P1 primary` › `P2 plausible` › `P3 low` › `P4 very-low`. no
  false-precision midpoint — a bracket, not a number.
- **severity** (consequence): `catastrophic` › `severe` › `moderate` › `mild`. set by the cost of a miss,
  never lowered by a probability-attack.

a branch earns phase-6 deep research on **either** a high probability bracket **or** an un-eliminated
severe/catastrophic severity — whichever is higher. `rule.require.superposition-until-elimination`:
a `P4 very-low` + `catastrophic` branch stays fully live for the veto.

| **deep-research (probability)** | B1, B11, B2, B14 | top probability brackets (P1–P2) | full bounds / prevention / care research |

low probability bracket — B16/B13/B5 ride the veto tier regardless of rank, exactly as
