## .the master attack lever (applies across the field)

the single most decisive case-feature is the **dose-locked reversibility**: miki's deficit **fully
reverses on onsior (a COX-2 inhibitor / NSAID)** and returns when it wears off. an NSAID relieves
**inflammatory pain**; it does **not** reperfuse an artery, decompress a cord, restore a severed axon, or
shrink a tumor. so this one feature is a strong honest attack on every **fixed structural / vascular /
neoplastic / neuro** branch, and it points toward an **inflammatory musculoskeletal / soft-tissue** cause.

- it **argues against**: B16 (ATE), B13 (spinal), B10 (nerve), B12 (fracture), B5 (FISS), and primary
  B17/B18 (metabolic/hepatic).
- it **argues for**: B1 (local), B11 (soft-tissue), B14 (arthritis flare), the local arm of B2.
- ⚠️ **but** it only *lowers* the structural branches — it cannot *drop* a catastrophic one, because no
  confirmatory exam (perfusion / neuro / radiograph) has been run, and a mechanism argument is not a
  ruled-out exam.

## .the fair attack on the unattested / catastrophic branches (why a weak attack cannot drop them)

the master lever (dose-reversibility) is a genuine, strong attack on B16, B13, B10, B12, B5 — it makes each
**less probable**. but probability is not the axis these branches ride:

- **B16 (ATE), B13 (spinal)** — irreversible + time-critical. the attack **lowers** their probability, but
  the only case that **drops** them is a confirmatory exam (perfusion, neuro, radiograph). a mechanism
  argument is not an exam. `absence-of-evidence ≠ evidence-of-absence`: we have no perfusion read, so we
  cannot claim "not ATE." they stay live under veto until their assay clears them.
- **B5 (FISS)** — the attack correctly drops it as the **acute-cause** candidate (no mass produces a
  dose-reversible limp), but its **persistent-watch** status is untouched: no mass today does not mean no
  mass in weeks. the attack lowers its acute rank, not its delayed veto.
- **B10 (nerve), B12 (fracture)** — the attack lowers them well (full functional reversal argues against a
  fixed lesion), but each is cheaply and definitively settled by a clean neuro exam / radiograph, so they
  are held only until that one assay reads clean — not dropped on the mechanism argument alone.

> the discipline: a **weak** attack (no confirmatory exam behind it) may re-rank a severe branch **down**,
> but may never **eliminate** it. only a hard eliminate (an actual clean exam) drops a catastrophic branch.

## .what evidence would most LOWER the overall field

the single observations that would most re-rank survivors downward:
1. **warm-pulse-pink perfusion** → drops B16 (hard).
2. **clean neuro exam** → drops B10, lowers B13.
3. **clean radiograph** → drops B12, lowers B13, drops the B14 bone-lesion tail.
4. **flat, non-painful injection/draw sites** → lowers B1 (+ cousins B6/B7).
5. **afebrile + normal CBC + no home systemic signs** → lowers B2, B17, B3 together.
6. **mg/kg at-or-below target + normal renal/hepatic** → lowers B15 (as a harm risk).

on absence-of-evidence alone** — each stays live under veto until a confirmatory exam clears it. this feeds
5.3 (the re-rank) where the steelman and the attack are weighed together.
