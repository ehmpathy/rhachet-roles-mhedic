# 3.assay.stone

## .what

decide WHICH checks would confirm or rule out each top-ranked cause. name the test, what it
tells us, and which candidate it targets. sequence them cheapest / most-urgent first.

## .boundary

the diagnostician names the tests; the **referrer** decides WHERE to get them, and a licensed
veterinarian performs them. the moment the question turns to treatment, that is the
**prescriber's** work.

## .inputs

- `2.2.differential.disentangle.yield.md` — the re-ranked candidate causes
- `2.1.differential.enumerate.yield.md` — the separator per candidate

## .emit

into `3.assay.yield.md`.

## .done when

each top candidate has at least one confirm-or-rule-out check, in a sensible order.
