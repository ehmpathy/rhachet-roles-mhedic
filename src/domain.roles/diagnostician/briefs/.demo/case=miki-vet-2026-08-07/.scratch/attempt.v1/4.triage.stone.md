# 4.triage.stone

## .what

judge acuity — emergency now vs. same-day vs. can wait. state the current status honestly, and
give a **watch-for** list of signs that would flip the case to emergency-now.

## .inputs

- `1.intake.yield.md`, `2.1.differential.enumerate.yield.md` — the decisive flags
- `0.seed.md` — her current reported state

## .do

- weigh the facts that lift or lower acuity
- mark which watch-for signs are **present now** vs. **not yet** — do not imply a sign is active
  when it is not
- carry the not-veterinary-advice disclaimer + the emergency carve-out

## .emit

into `4.triage.yield.md`.

## .done when

acuity is stated, current status is explicit, and the watch-for list is marked present vs. absent.
