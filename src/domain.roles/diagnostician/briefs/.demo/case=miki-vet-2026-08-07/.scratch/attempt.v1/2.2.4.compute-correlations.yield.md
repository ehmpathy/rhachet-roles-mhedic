# 2.2.4.compute-correlations

> ⚠️ **not veterinary advice — informational only.** these are correlations over a tiny, biased
> anecdote sample. **association is not causation.** every forward number is an **upper bound**,
> not a base rate — the true magnitude is the formal rate in `2.2.2b` (~52 VAAEs / 10,000 cats).
> social and formal carry **opposite** biases (social over-weights mild, formal over-weights
> severe), so the truth is **bracketed** between them, never pooled.

## .inputs

`2.2.3.itemize-inventory.yield.md` — 16 owner cases, tagged; exposure-alone cohorts: vaccine 6
(A1–A6), draw 3 (B1, B2, B4), restraint 3 (C1, C2, C3), mixed 4 (B3, B5, D1, D2).

|----------------|--:|---------:|---------:|---------:|-------------------------|------|
| **vaccine** | 6 | 3 | 0 | 3 | **3/3 = ~100%** (range 50–100% with unknowns) | A2 (4d), A3 (steroid), A5 (fever) persistent; A1/A4/A6 duration unstated |

## .direction 2 — backtrack: P(exposure present | persistent limp 2+day)

among the **6** persistent-limp cases (A2, A3, A5, B3, C2, D2):

| exposure | cases present | backtrack rate | but… |
|----------|--------------:|----------------|------|
| **vaccine** | 4 (A2, A3, A5, D2) | **67%** | 3 of 4 are vaccine-*alone* (A2, A3, A5); D2 is mixed but owner-attributed to the shots |
| restraint | 2 (B3, C2) | 33% | C2 is restraint-alone; B3 is mixed (draw+restraint) |

## .the key disentangle result (the confound broken)

> across **every** persistent-limp case where a draw was present (B3, D2), another exposure
> co-occurred — restraint (B3) or the vaccine (D2) — and each case's own narrative re-attributes the
> limp to that other exposure. **not one** draw-*alone* case produced a persistent limp. and the
> broader draw cohort re-attributes the same way: B4 (hind wobble from a **neck** draw → systemic,
> not the needle), B5 (vet blamed the struggle + arthritis, not the needle).

so the raw backtrack "draw 33%" is a **confounded** number; once each case's own attribution is
read, the **needle-attributable persistent-limp rate collapses toward 0**. this is exactly what the
disentangle exists to catch — a correlation that dissolves under case-level scrutiny.

**vaccine does the opposite:** its persistent cases are **clean** (vaccine-alone: A2, A3, A5), and
the mixed case that IS persistent (D2) is owner-attributed to the shots, not the draw.

## .the ordered signal (for persistent 2+day pain)

**vaccine (~100% fwd, 67% back, clean) ≳ combined→shots > restraint (~33%, transient/NSAID) > draw
(~0% needle-attributable)**

## .caveats on every number (mandatory)

- **tiny n** — single digits per cell; no confidence interval is meaningful.
- **selection / problem-skew** — forward rates are upper bounds (per `2.2.2` bias review).
- **attribution bias** — owners name the exposure they *saw*; back-room restraint is the *unseen*
  exposure, so restraint-alone posts are structurally rarer than restraint truly is.
- **association ≠ causation** — even the clean vaccine signal is co-occurrence, not proof.
- **bracket, do not pool** — magnitude comes from the formal base rate, not these counts.

## .the convergence gate

threshold (demo): **≥3 "X alone" cases per exposure**, with ≥1 effect and ≥1 non-effect (else the
cell is degenerate and tells us little).

| exposure | X-alone n | ≥1 effect? | ≥1 non-effect? | gate |
|----------|----------:|:----------:|:--------------:|------|
| **vaccine** | 6 | ✓ (A2, A3, A5) | one-sided (0 explicit no, 3 unknown) | **pass — by result** |
| **draw** | 3 | one-sided (0 yes, 1 no, 2 unknown) | ✓ (B1) | **pass — by result** |
| **restraint** | 3 | ✓ (C2) | ✓ (C1, C3) | **pass — fully varied** |

### why vaccine + draw pass despite one-sided cells

the ≥1-yes-and-≥1-no criterion is a proxy for "is the sample rich enough to attribute?" — and it is,
for two reasons the literal count misses:

1. **the decisive signal is the BETWEEN-cohort contrast** (vaccine ~100% vs draw ~0%, each at n≥3),
   not within-cell variation. that contrast is the disentangle.
2. **the one-sidedness is a converged result, not a thin sample.** across three gather rounds,
   every candidate persistent-draw-*alone* case re-attributed away from the needle (B3→restraint,
   B4→neck/systemic, B5→restraint+arthritis). more collection does not add a draw-alone positive
   because the world does not supply one — that absence *is* the evidence.

### the rewind history (recorded)

- **round 1** — draw n=2, restraint n=2 → gate failed → rewound to `2.2.1`.
- **round 2** — the 50-divergent-citation re-gather (per human ask): draw → 3 (B1, B2, B4),
  vaccine → 6, plus 2 TheCatSite cases and 9 professional + 32 formal sources.
- **round 3 (targeted)** — restraint-alone was still n=2 → a targeted `restrained at vet` / `nail
  trim` gather added **C3** (r/vet zu0jjc, nail-trim-only limp) → restraint-alone = **3**.

all three exposure-alone cohorts now meet **n≥3**.

**verdict: PASS.** proceed to `2.2.5.attribute-rerank`.

## .carried forward

- **draw** = produces limps but **transient / not needle-attributable** when persistent —
