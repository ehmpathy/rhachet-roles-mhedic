# 1.intake.stone

## .what

take the history. structure the raw seed into a clean clinical record — patient, reason for
visit, an absolute-dated timeline, medications, and the owner's questions.

## .inputs

- `0.seed.md` — the verbatim seed + ledger

## .do

- strip identity per `rule.forbid.pii` ("miki" is a persona handle, not a real identifier)
- convert every relative time ("yesterday", "now") to an absolute date
- separate the two tracks: the hind-limb lameness vs. the cardiac (pant → probnp) question
- list the knowledge gaps, then mark each closed vs. open as facts arrive

## .emit

into `1.intake.yield.md`.

## .done when

the timeline is absolute, medications carry the dose, and every gap is marked closed or open.
