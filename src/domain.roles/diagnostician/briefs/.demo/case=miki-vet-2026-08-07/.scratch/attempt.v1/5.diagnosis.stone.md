# 5.diagnosis.stone

## .what

land on the single most-likely cause — the "charge", after the differential lineup and the
evidence re-rank. state confidence, what would raise or lower it, and the open questions.

## .differential vs. diagnosis

the differential is the suspect lineup (all plausible causes); the diagnosis is the one you
charge. this stone is the conclusion, held apart from `2.1.differential.enumerate.yield.md`.

## .inputs

- `2.2.differential.disentangle.yield.md` — the re-ranked causes
- `3.assay.yield.md` — what tests would confirm
- `4.triage.yield.md` — the acuity

## .do

- name the primary assessment + confidence (provisional until a hands-on exam)
- answer the owner's original questions plainly
- keep it informational — defer treatment to the licensed veterinarian (prescriber boundary)

## .emit

into `5.diagnosis.yield.md`.

## .done when

a primary cause is named with confidence, the raise/lower factors are listed, and the status
(provisional vs. confirmed) is explicit.
