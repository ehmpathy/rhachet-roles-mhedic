# 2.2.5.attribute-rerank.stone

## .what

attribute the exposures by their combined two-direction weight, then feed that back to re-rank
the effect:mechanism candidates from `2.1`. the output of the disentangle.

fifth + final substone of `2.2.differential.disentangle`. only runs once `2.2.4` ruled **pass**.

## .inputs

- `2.2.4.compute-correlations.yield.md` — the two-direction table (gate = pass)
- `2.1.differential.enumerate.yield.md` — the effect:mechanism candidates to re-rank
- `2.5.formal cross-check` (if run) — trusted-source incidence for the exposure→lesion edges

## .do

1. **rank the exposures** by combined forward+backtrack weight — which action is most implicated.
2. **cross-check the edges** — for the top exposures, confirm a known `cause:mechanism →
   effect:mechanism` pathway against a trusted source (does vaccine→myositis have real incidence?).
3. **re-rank the effect:mechanisms** — reorder the `2.1` effect:mechanisms by the exposure weight + the
   edge evidence. state the rationale per rank.
4. **carry the caveats** — the rank is plausibility from biased signal, not epidemiology; it
   is a hypothesis for the clinician + the assay stage, not a verdict.

## .emit

into `2.2.5.attribute-rerank.yield.md` — the ranked exposures + the re-ranked effect:mechanisms with
rationale. this is the `2.2.differential.disentangle` result that flows to `3.assay`.

## .done when

exposures are ranked, the top edges are cross-checked, and the effect:mechanism candidates are re-ranked
with rationale + caveats.
