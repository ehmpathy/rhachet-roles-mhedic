## .step 3 — the re-ranked effect:mechanism candidates (was → now)

### 1. vaccine-associated reaction — injection-site myositis/soreness **or** FCV limping syndrome  ⬆ (was #2)

miki got vaccines in **both hind thighs** (distemper-combo + rabies right, felv left). the vaccine

(vaccine-alone, no confound) and it is the best formally-supported — **but read it at the bracket,
not its top: the honest floor is 50%.** 3 of the 6 vaccine durations are unstated, so the "~100%" is
the *top* of a **50–100%** range, not a firm rate.
the closest **symptom-picture** anchor is nearly identical: **A1 (iq414j)** — ~1yr, 9.2lb,
FVRCP-FeLV, back-leg limp with **~24h onset latency** (limp appeared ~24h post-vaccine), screams on

unstated** in `2.2.4` (not a known-persistent case; ~24h is onset, not duration). the **persistence**
of miki's picture is anchored instead by the day-2+ cases A2 (4d) / A3 (steroid) / A5 (fever). **A2
(1ss87vx)** got **onsior** for the same picture. on the current signal this reads as the lead
hypothesis.
- *sub-read:* localized injection-site soreness/myositis (most likely) vs systemic FCV limping
  syndrome (fever would favor this). a **rectal temperature** separates them.
- *the Sunday signal — split into what it does and does not show:*
  - **what it shows (a real lesion, not vaccine-specifically):** pain that **persists off all onsior**
    into day 4+ is body-map-consistent with a **real hind-limb lesion**, not a bruise that should
    have faded. persistent off-drug pain is the hallmark that separates a true lesion from transient
    soreness. this is a **lesion-vs-transient** signal — it does not, on its own, name *which*

  - **what it does NOT show (not a vaccine discriminator):** the **ungroomed hindquarters**

    (knee-area) and a restraint strain of a hind limb is likewise hind. a hind-quarter pain map is
    equally consistent with all three exposures, so it is **dropped as evidence for vaccine

### 2. restraint strain (rough hold in the back room)  ⬆⬆ (was #4)

**this rank derives from the matrix forward separation + one disclosed attribution judgment — NOT
from the unseen-hold inference.** `2.2.4` scores restraint fwd **33%** (firm, n=3) vs draw fwd **~0%**
(on a single known case, B1); the backtrack is a **tie** (2/6 each), broken only by the B3
attribution call (below). so restraint > draw = **one firm forward cell + the B3 judgment**, not
"matrix alone." restraint sits above the #3 diffuse "combined" catch-all not by matrix score (the
catch-all is not a matrix row) but on the **prefer-a-named-exposure-over-a-blur** rationale — the
disentangle favours the specific heavy arm it can name. that is the whole basis for the position.
- *the ~5-min back-room screams and the unseen-hold argument do NOT lift this rank.* the "owners
  rarely witness the back-room hold, so restraint is structurally under-counted" point is an
  **unfalsifiable absence** — no amount of data can weaken it — so it does **not** serve as
  affirmative evidence to lift the rank. instead it is carried forward as a **data-gap to probe at
  `3.assay`**: ask the owner what the back-room restraint looked like, and check for a witnessed /
  palpable strain. that converts the unfalsifiable absence into a **testable question**, which is
  exactly the assay stage's job.
- *B3→restraint is a case-narrative judgment, surfaced:* restraint and draw carry the **same** raw
  backtrack (2/6 each). draw collapses to ~0 because both its cases are mixed; restraint keeps its

  plausible **co-contributor** (or a lesion source in its own right); the persistence narrows

## .step 4 — caveats carried (mandatory)

- this rank is **plausibility from biased anecdote + small formal set**, not epidemiology.
- **bracket, do not pool** — every forward number is an **upper bound**; magnitude comes from the
  formal base rate (`2.2.2b`, ~52 VAAEs / 10,000 cats), not these counts. social over-weights mild,
  formal over-weights severe; the truth is bracketed between, never pooled.
- the rank is **ordinal / directional** (which exposure is more implicated), **not a magnitude
  verdict** — "vaccine ~100%" is a forward conditional inside a biased cohort, not a base rate.
- **attribution bias** — owners name the exposure they *saw*; the back-room restraint is the
  *unseen* exposure, so restraint-alone posts are structurally rarer than restraint truly is. this
  is carried as a **data-gap to probe at `3.assay`** (ask about the hold), never as evidence that
  lifts restraint's rank.
- **association is not causation**; even the lead (vaccine) is co-occurrence + mechanism, not proof.
- it is a **hypothesis for the clinician and the `3.assay` stage**, not a diagnosis.
- the **decisive flag is unchanged and overrides the rank**: lethargy + reduced appetite **on
  onsior** is both the most-common VAAE sign *and* onsior's own stop-and-call line → re-exam now,
  regardless of which mechanism leads.
