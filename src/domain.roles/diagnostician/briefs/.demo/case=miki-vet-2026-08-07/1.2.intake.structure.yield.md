### dose-detail gaps (carried to 1.3.intake.gaps)

- **E6 onsior — mg per dose is unknown**, and the patient's **body weight is unknown**. both are
  required to judge above/at/below label (feline robenacoxib target ≈ 1 mg/kg q24h). without them, an
  above-label determination is **not possible from this seed** — flagged as a gap, not silently
  dropped, so it cannot be assumed either way downstream.
- **E1/E2 vaccine product identities** are by category (distemper-combo, rabies, leukemia), not brand
  /lot — sufficient for reaction reasoning, insufficient for lot-specific adverse-event lookup.
- **E3–E5 use standard clinical volumes/gauges** not stated by the owner; route/site captured, exact
  quantity not — acceptable for a needle-trauma hypothesis, which does not hinge on volume.

- the effect set (S1–S5) has **onset after t0** (the visit) — no reported deficit predates the visit.
- the acquired-deficit node **S2** is the sharpest temporal anchor: present only **post-visit**, so
  its cause candidate set is bounded to the visit exposures E1–E5.
- the therapeutic exposure **E6 (onsior)** produces the **reversal pattern S5** — an anti-inflammatory
  dose-response that toggles S1–S4. this couples E6 to the effect set as a *relief/mask* cause,
  distinct from the *origin* cause among E1–E5.
- two causal questions fall out for phase 2:
  1. which of E1–E5 (or their combination) originated S1/S2?
  2. does the E6 dose-response constrain what the origin cause can be?
