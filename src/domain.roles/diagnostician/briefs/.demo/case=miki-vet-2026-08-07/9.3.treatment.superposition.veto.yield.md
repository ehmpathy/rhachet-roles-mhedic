## .why a veto (severity overrides probability)

a treatment attractive for the front-runner scenario (B1/B11 inflammatory strain) can be lethal
under a low-probability-but-catastrophic branch (B16 ATE, B13 spinal, B5 FISS). while those
branches stay live, the attractive treatment is **vetoed** — no matter its rank — until the assay
that eliminates the catastrophic branch releases it (`rule.require.superposition-until-elimination`).
because miki was **not examined**, no assay has run, so **every veto below is currently in force**.

## .the veto list (forbidden now → lift condition)

### V1 — full-mask analgesia (deep/opioid-level that would hide a critical limb)
- **vetoed by:** B16 feline ATE — a cold, pulseless limb is the ATE tell; analgesia that masks it
  can bury a death-in-hours emergency.
- **forbidden now:** any analgesia strong enough to mask distal-limb pain/perfusion signs, before a
  perfusion check.
- **lift condition:** `4.3` step 1 — distal-limb perfusion **warm, pulses palpable, pads pink**
  (B16 eliminated). prognosis note: only ~37% survive the initial ATE event (PMID 8581544);
  guarded-to-poor (PMID 16594585).
- **note:** the no-regret gabapentin comfort (9.1) is **not** vetoed — it eases pain without a mask
  of the cold/pulseless perfusion sign; it is the sanctioned interim analgesic under this veto.

### V2 — NSAID (robenacoxib / onsior) at or above label
- **vetoed by:** three corners at once — B16 (cardiac/QT), CKD (renal), B12 fracture (a structural
  mask). this is the master lever, so its veto is the most consequential.
- **forbidden now:** any further onsior dose before an exam + baseline labs.
- **lift condition (all three must clear):**
  1. `4.3` step 1 perfusion clears B16 (warm limb, pulses), **and**
  2. `4.3` step 2 renal values **non-azotemic** (CKD cleared for an NSAID), **and**
  3. `4.3` step 3 confirms onsior mg/kg **at/below ~1 mg/kg label target** (robenacoxib ~1 mg/kg PO
     q24h — FDA label; PMID 38587872), with the structural-mask concern (B12) weighed on the film.
- **note:** the limp preceded onsior and onsior relieved it — a dose-reversible inflammatory
  picture argues *for* the NSAID once the three gates clear; the veto is a safety hold, not a verdict
  against it.

### V3 — limb/spine manipulation + manipulation-under-sedation
- **vetoed by:** B13 spinal / cord event — manipulation before the cord + bone are cleared can turn
  a recoverable deficit permanent.
- **forbidden now:** no manipulation, no manipulation-under-sedation, before the radiograph gate.
- **lift condition:** `4.3` step 1 neuro **intact** **and** `4.3` step 5 radiograph shows **spinal
  stability / no lesion** (B13 eliminated).

### V4 — same-site repeat injection
- **vetoed by:** B5 injection-site sarcoma (FISS) — a same-site re-injection compounds the FISS
  risk; clean-margin excision gives the best survival if a mass appears (PMID 28716129).
- **forbidden now (a permanent rule):** no repeat injection at a prior site; rotate sites + run the
  3-2-1 monitor (a firm mass **> 3 months, > 2 cm, or that grows within 1 month**).
- **lift condition:** this is a **forward, permanent** prevention, not a branch to eliminate — it
  does not lift; it becomes permanent care. the 3-2-1 monitor clears an individual mass as benign,
  but the site-rotation rule stays.

## .the no-waste cap (the best-case corner bound)

from `8.3`'s best-case corner (B11/B1 self-limited strain): do **not** overtreat past a strain.
the paid/invasive workup — **cross-sectional scans (CT/MRI), exploratory surgery** — stays
**conditional on a localized result**, never routine. escalate only on a cited assay result or a
`7.3` tripwire, never on anxiety alone. this is not a catastrophic veto; it is the cap that keeps the
plan best-case-not-wasteful.

## .the assembled final plan (worst-case-safe, best-case-not-wasteful, no-regret-first)

### layer 1 — start now (the no-regret core, from 9.1)
runs immediately, safe at both envelope corners:
1. clinician **perfusion + neuro check** (the floor-lift — clears B16/B13).
2. **strict rest** / activity restriction.
3. **gentle, low-stress handle.**
4. **gabapentin** analgesia (~6.5 mg/kg PO q12h — PMID 23253881; **off-label + inferred-dose
   caveat**, vet to set against miki's real weight), paired with rest.
5. **appetite + hydration monitor** (the B18 early-warn).

### layer 2 — wait on the assay (the conditional branches, from 9.2)
each held in reserve, keyed to its `4.3` trigger: B16 ATE, B13 spinal, B5 FISS (the veto-drivers);
B12, B10, B9, B18, B15, B2, B17 (the severe branches). each carries its prevention/recurrence rider.

### layer 3 — the veto gate (this yield)
V1–V4 gate layer 2: no full-mask analgesia (V1), no at/above-label NSAID (V2), no manipulation
(V3), no same-site re-injection (V4) — until each lift condition clears. the no-waste cap holds the
paid workup conditional.

### the sequence in one line
> **perfusion + neuro check first** → if any red-line or perfusion doubt, **act-as-if-ATE now**
> (emergency); else run the no-regret core, hold every veto, and release each conditional branch
> only as its `4.3` assay result clears its veto.
