# 8.3.diagnosis.superposition.envelope.yield

> the **envelope** — the outer bounds of the whole live superposition: the **worst-case corner** (the
> floor the plan must be safe against) and the **best-case corner** (the cap the plan must not
> overtreat past), plus the **no-regret core** that helps at both corners and the **acuity floor**
> the plan inherits. this is the diagnostician's complete hand-off to the prescriber (phase 9).
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.** miki was not examined. this is a bounded
> hand-off, not a treatment order. if any `7.1` red-line sign is present, call your local emergency
> vet now.
>
> **recommendation disclaimer.** the no-regret core and the corner-bounds below are offered to the
> best of our knowledge from the information found in research — they carry **no guarantee** of
> outcome, and each must be reviewed by a **licensed veterinarian** against miki's own real,
> hands-on-examined scenario before any reliance. this is groundwork for that review, not a substitute.

## .the worst-case corner (the floor the plan must be safe against)

**the most severe un-eliminated trajectory across all 15 live scenarios is B16 — feline ATE:
death within hours; only ~37% survive the initial event (PMID 8581544); guarded-to-poor prognosis
(PMID 16594585).** reinforced by two more catastrophic corners:

- **B13 spinal** — permanent cord damage if a decompression window (hours) is missed.
- **B5 FISS** — fatal if a mass is caught late; clean-margin excision gives the best survival (PMID 28716129).

**the plan must never contraindicate against this corner.** the veto source it generates:

1. **avoid any analgesia that would mask a critical (cold, pulseless) limb** — B16.
2. **no manipulation until cord + bone are cleared** — B13.
3. **no same-site repeat injection** — B5.
4. **B16's veto fires empirically / pre-test** — a perfusion doubt acts-as-if-ATE now.

## .the best-case corner (the cap the plan must not overtreat past)

**the most benign trajectory across all 15 is B11/B1 — a self-limited soft-tissue strain or local
injection reaction: settles in days-to-a-week with rest + NSAID, no intervention beyond supportive
care.** the master lever supports this corner: the limp preceded onsior and onsior relieves it — a
dose-reversible, inflammatory picture.

**the plan must not overtreat past this corner:** no aggressive surgical or invasive workup is
warranted *for a strain* — the paid/invasive tests (radiograph, advanced imaging) stay **conditional**
on a localized finding, not routine. the no-waste bound: escalate only on a cited result or a
tripwire, never on anxiety alone.

## .the no-regret core (helps across the whole envelope, harmless at both corners)

from `6.3`, the care that is beneficial under the benign corner AND safe under the catastrophic
corner:

- **rest / activity restriction** — helps every musculoskeletal branch; shuts the ambulation vector
  for an un-cleared fracture/cord (B12/B13).
- **gentle, low-stress handle** — helps all; reduces clot-dislodge stress (B16) and spinal load (B13).
- **perfusion + neuro check** — the no-regret clearance: it lifts the emergency floor by clearing
  B16/B13; harm-none.
- **appetite + hydration monitor** — the B18 early-warn; harm-none.
- **gabapentin + comfort, paired with rest** — analgesia with no QT effect (safe where the NSAID is
  not, per the B16 conflict) and, paired with rest, no ambulation vector.

the **one cross-scenario conflict** the prescriber must arbitrate is the **NSAID (robenacoxib)**: it
helps the inflammatory core but carries three harm-vectors (cardiac/QT vs B16, renal vs CKD,
structural-mask vs fracture), each gated behind an exam + baseline labs, with gabapentin as the
interim substitute.

## .the acuity floor carried (urgency + bounds together)

from phase 7, carried so the prescriber inherits urgency and bounds as one artifact:

- **the floor is EMERGENCY-until-cleared**, forced by B16 (feline ATE), reinforced by B13.
- it **lifts** on a clinician's perfusion + neuro check; until then, and miki was not examined, the
  floor holds at emergency.
- below the floor: a **same-day** band (B12 clearance exam, B1-family NSAID-safety) and a **watch**
  band (the benign core + tripwires), per `7.2`/`7.3`.

## .done

both envelope corners are stated — worst-case (B16 ATE death-in-hours, reinforced by B13/B5) and
best-case (B11/B1 self-limited strain) — the no-regret core is named (rest, gentle handle, perfusion
+ neuro check, appetite monitor, gabapentin + rest) with the single NSAID conflict flagged for
arbitration, and the acuity floor (emergency-until-cleared) is carried. the diagnostician's hand-off
to the prescriber (phase 9) is complete: urgency, bounds, and no-regret core in one artifact.
