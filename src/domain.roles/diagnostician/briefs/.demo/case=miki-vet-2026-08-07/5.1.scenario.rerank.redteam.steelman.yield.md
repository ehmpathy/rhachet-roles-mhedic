> analytic best-case-for-each exercise, not a diagnosis. if miki worsens or shows red-flag signs, seek a
> licensed veterinarian now.

## .the severity-basis lens on the attestation-thin survivors (a view, not a separate class)

> ⚠️ these are **not** a separate class from the survivors above — B16, B13, B5, B10, B12, B15 all sit in
> the survivor table. this section is the **severity-basis view** of them: the survivors whose steelman
> rests on asymmetric loss rather than a formal denominator or anecdote bucket. it exists because a
> can't-miss survivor earns its rank on consequence, not attestation — and 5.2 must attack that ground,
> not demand a base rate the survivor never claimed.

these survivors lack a formal denominator or an anecdote bucket — but fairness demands their **severity**
case be made, because a can't-miss branch earns its place on consequence, not attestation:

- **B16 (ATE), B13 (spinal), B5 (FISS)** — all catastrophic, all corpus-blind in our data. their steelman
  is **not** probability but **irreversibility**: ATE is a limb/life risk and time-critical; a spinal cord
  injury is permanent if manipulated; FISS is fatal if missed until late. the strongest case *for* each
  staying high is the asymmetric loss — a miss is catastrophic and (for ATE/spinal) irreversible.
- **B10 (nerve), B12 (fracture)** — severe, thinly attested, but a plausible mechanical mechanism from the
  restraint; their steelman is that a treatable structural lesion should not be missed for lack of a base
  rate.
- **B15 (NSAID)** — the one "unattested-event" branch with a **hard exposure fact**: the dose figure
  itself is the steelman; no anecdote is needed when the exposure is documented.

## .the steelman completeness audit (all 20 — every scenario accounted)

> a steelman's **strength depends on what it rests on**. a case built on a formal denominator is a
> different animal from one built on an anecdote bucket, which is different again from one built on
> severity alone. 5.2 must know *which kind* it attacks, and no survivor may reach 5.2 without one. this
> audit classifies each scenario's steelman **support-basis** and confirms completeness across all 20.

support-basis legend:
- **formal** — a cited denominator / labelled rate (the strongest steelman footing)
- **anecdote** — an attested experience bucket (real but uncounted)
- **exposure-fact** — a documented case feature, no rate needed (e.g. the dose figure)
- **severity** — no rate/anecdote; the case rests on asymmetric loss (a can't-miss footing)
- **cousin** — inherits a survivor's steelman (a below-floor variant riding its live cousin)
- **closed** — no steelman by construction (eliminative branch / null control)

## .the anchor premise per survivor (the hinge each steelman rests on)

> a steelman is only as strong as its **single anchor premise** — the one assumption that, if false,
> collapses the whole case. an honest steelman names its own hinge (this is *not* the 5.2 attack — it is
> the steelman's own confession of where it is anchored, so 5.2 strikes the true joint, not a strawman).
> for each survivor: the one premise the strongest case cannot survive without.

| B# | scenario | anchor premise (the hinge) | if the hinge fails, the case… |
|----|----------|----------------------------|-------------------------------|
| B2 | systemic VAAE | the deficit is systemic, not local/mechanical | collapses to B1/B11 (local) |
| B1 | injection local reaction | the deficit localizes to a vaccine site | collapses to systemic or restraint |
| B14 | prior arthritis flare | occult DJD pre-existed the visit | collapses to an acute injury (B11/B12) |
| B11 | restraint sprain | the restraint applied enough soft-tissue torque | collapses to arthritis or systemic |
| B16 | feline ATE | perfusion is (or could be) compromised | collapses — a warm-pulse-pink limb voids it |
| B13 | restraint spinal | the cord took manipulable force | collapses to peripheral nerve (B10) |
| B5 | injection-site sarcoma | a persistent mass is (or will be) present | dormant — a watch, not an active case |
| B15 | NSAID above-label | the true mg/kg exceeds the feline target | collapses — an at-target dose voids it |
| B18 | hepatic lipidosis | anorexia was prolonged enough to mobilize fat | collapses — brief inappetence is benign |
| B4 | vaccine anaphylaxis | an acute post-vaccine event actually occurred | collapses — no witnessed event voids it |
| B10 | iatrogenic nerve injury | the injection tracked near a hind-limb nerve | collapses to a non-neuro source |
| B12 | restraint fracture/luxation | the struggle delivered fracture-level force | collapses to soft-tissue (B11) |
| B9 | cysto abdominal pain | the cysto caused wall injury / free fluid | collapses — a clean tap voids it |
| B17 | other systemic/metabolic | an occult systemic process is present | collapses — a clean broad screen voids it |
| B3 | FCV lameness syndrome | the vaccine carried a calici strain + ≤12d lag | collapses — outside the window voids it |

**the pattern:** the catastrophic survivors (B16, B15, B4, B9) hinge on a **single observable** that a
clean read voids outright — which is exactly why their assays sit first in the 4.3 plan. the systemic and
occult survivors (B2, B17, B18) hinge on a **process that a single clean panel only lowers** — which is
exactly why they persist in the irreducible set. the hinge type predicts the collapse behavior.
