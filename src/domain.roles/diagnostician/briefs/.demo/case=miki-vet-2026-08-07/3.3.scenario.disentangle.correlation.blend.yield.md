# 3.3.scenario.disentangle.correlation.blend.yield

> the **blend** of the two disentangle halves — the formal base rate (3.1) and the anecdote signal
> (3.2) — reconciled **by bracket, not by pool**. the anecdote number is the **upper bound**; the formal
> base rate is the **base rate**; the two are never averaged into a false midpoint. the output is a
> **coarse ordinal rank** (no false-precision numbers) plus the **(probability × severity) floor** that
> decides which scenarios earn a full workup in phase 6.
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.** this is an analysis artifact over
> population findings and internet owner-reports, not a diagnosis of this patient. if miki worsens or
> shows red-flag signs, seek a licensed veterinarian now.

## .the method — bracket, never pool

for each scenario the two halves are placed as the **ends of a range**, not merged:

- **lower end = formal base rate** (what controlled data supports; often "none" → an honest floor of the
  population signal, not a zero of probability)
- **upper end = anecdote signal** (the selection-inflated upper bound from 3.2; often "uncomputable ≤1"
  → an un-boundable cap, not a high rate)
- **the truth sits somewhere in the bracket** — and where the two ends **disagree**, the disagreement is
  itself recorded as signal (a wide bracket = high uncertainty = a reason to keep the branch live, not
  to split the difference).

a coarse **weight** (ordinal) is then read from *where the bracket sits + how the two streams agree*,
and a **severity** is assigned independently. the floor cut uses **both** — a branch passes on weight OR
on severity.

> ⚠️ **the coarse weight is NOT a pooled midpoint.** it is not the average of the two bracket ends —
> averaging a formal "none" with an anecdote "≤1" would fabricate a false ~0.5 that neither stream
> supports. instead the weight is read from *bracket position + agreement direction*: when both ends
> point up (vaccine arm) → `lead`; when formal is silent but anecdote is live (restraint, arthritis) →
> `moderate` **because the anecdote end alone carries it**, not because it was averaged down by the
> formal void; when both ends are silent (the landmines) → `low`/`minimal` on weight, but the branch is
> then carried by **severity**, never by a pooled number. the two raw ends stay visible in the `formal`
> and `anecdote` columns so the bracket is never collapsed.

## .the coarse scales (ordinal, no false precision)

- **weight (probability-ish):** `lead` · `moderate` · `low` · `minimal` · `n/a` (eliminative/null)

> ⚠️ **these tiers are an ORDER, not a magnitude — they carry no cardinal spacing.** `lead` is not "4×"
> `minimal`; the gaps between tiers are undefined on purpose. the anecdote set is selection-biased and
> the formal denominators are mostly absent, so the evidence licenses only *which outranks which*, never
> *by how much*. therefore the tiers must **never** be converted to a percentage, multiplied, or
> averaged — a false-precision number here would claim a magnitude the biased data cannot support. the
> only real numbers in this doc are the *cited formal base rates* (e.g. 51.6/10,000, PMID 17605670),
> which are quoted as-is in the bracket column and are **not** the coarse weight.
- **severity:** `catastrophic` (death/limb/irreversible) · `severe` (tx-urgent) · `moderate` (tx-needed)
  · `mild` (self-limited)

> ⚠️ **severity is rated on the WORST plausible outcome (the tail), never the modal outcome.** if a
> branch is usually benign but carries a rare catastrophic tail, it is rated by the tail — because the
> floor's job is to not-miss the tail. to rate severity by the typical case would let a rare-but-lethal
> branch be mild-rated and cut, which is the exact failure `rule.require.superposition-until-elimination`
> forbids. (this is why B9 cystocentesis is rated **severe**, not moderate: the usual outcome is minor,
> but the tail — uroabdomen / bladder-wall injury — is tx-urgent, and that tail sets the rating.)

## .the disagreements — where the two streams diverge (signal, not noise)

- **B14 prior arthritis + B11 restraint** — formal is **silent** (no denominator), but the anecdote
  backtrack lands its heaviest net weight here (restraint = largest raw bucket; prior-arthritis = the
  clinician "second look"). the divergence is the whole reason these clear the floor on **weight**
  despite a formal void: the anecdote layer sees what the formal layer has no denominator for. the
  bracket is *wide* (formal-none → anecdote-moderate) → keep live.
- **B15 NSAID** — the formal side confirms the **exposure** (12 mg/day exceeds the 6 mg once-daily
  feline label unit) while both sides are silent on the **event rate**. the divergence between a hard
  formal exposure-fact and an absent event-rate is why B15 rides on **severity**, not weight.
- **B3 FCV** — formal proves the *category* ("occasionally", 12-day lag) but miki's hind-limb-localized
  picture does not match the classic multi-limb syndrome; anecdote is owner-attributed only. the streams
  agree the branch is *possible* but neither confirms *this* mechanism → low weight, kept for workup.

## .the floor cut — (probability × severity), survivors named

the floor passes a scenario on **weight OR severity** — a low-probability / high-severity landmine
clears on severity alone (`rule.require.superposition-until-elimination`; `absence-of-evidence ≠
evidence-of-absence`).

**the explicit thresholds (so no landmine is cut by low probability):**
- **severity gate:** severity **≥ moderate** clears the floor *regardless of weight* — even `minimal`
  weight passes if severity is moderate/severe/catastrophic. only **mild** severity fails the severity
  gate. (this is why B9 at `minimal`-weight / `moderate`-severity is kept, while B19 at `low`-weight /
  `mild`-severity is cut — the difference is severity, never probability.)
- **weight gate:** weight **≥ low** clears the floor on the weight axis alone when severity is mild
  (B6/B19 sit at the boundary — mild severity, and their weight is not high enough to carry them, so
  they drop; a `moderate`+ weight would have carried them).
- **the veto direction is one-way:** a low/minimal weight can **never** cut a moderate+ severity branch.
  the only branches that fall below the floor are those that are **both** weak-weight **and**
  mild-severity — a landmine (severe/catastrophic) is structurally un-cuttable by low probability.

**pass to full workup (phase 6) — 15 scenarios:**

- **on weight (the probable drivers):** B2, B1 (vaccine arm — the only branch with a real formal
  denominator, miki in top-risk cell), B14 (prior arthritis), B11 (restraint), B3 (FCV, category-level).
- **on severity (the can't-miss landmines, low weight but catastrophic/severe):** B16 (feline ATE — the
  top landmine), B13 (spinal), B5 (FISS, delayed), B15 (NSAID above-label), B18 (hepatic lipidosis), B4
  (anaphylaxis), B10 (nerve), B12 (fracture/luxation), B9 (cysto), B17 (other systemic).

**below the floor — not carried to full workup (5 scenarios):**

- **B6, B7** (draw hematoma / irritation) — both streams weak *and* mild severity; the formal
  informative-absence and the anecdote de-attribution **agree**, and the cap is low-harm → the one place
  agreement + low severity lets a branch rest.
  > ⚠️ **the mild grade is safe only because the severe draw-complication tail is carved OUT into
  > separate kept branches.** a draw can rarely go severe (deep infection, nerve injury) — but those
  > tails are **not** hidden inside B6/B7; they live as their own landmine branches **B10 (iatrogenic
  > nerve/deep-tissue) and B9-adjacent**, which are **kept on severity**. so a cut of B6/B7 drops only
  > the genuinely-mild hematoma/irritation, never a severe tail — the severe tail was split off and
  > survives. this is the completeness check behind a mild grade: a mild cut is honest only if no severe
  > tail is buried in it.
- **B19** (cumulative soreness) — benign by construction, mild.
- **B8** (draw-benign eliminative) — an eliminative branch, not a positive cause.
- **B20** (limp predates visit) — the null control.

> note the asymmetry: a branch is dropped only when **both** streams are weak **and** severity is mild.
> any branch with catastrophic/severe severity is kept **regardless** of how low its weight — that is
> the severity veto that keeps the superposition honest.

## .the coarse rank (ordinal, for phase 6 order)

> ⚠️ **this rank does NOT pool weight × severity into a single priority score, and the tier NUMBERS are
> NOT a strict work-priority.** the two axes stay distinct: the weight-led tier (1–2, 4) orders the
> *probable* branches; the severity-led tier (3) orders the *can't-miss* branches independently. a
> landmine (B16) sits in the workup because of its severity axis, never because a pooled weight×severity
> number lifted it.
>
> **crucially, the numbers form GROUPS, NOT a strict "work tier 1 before tier 3".** the numbered list
> would, read naively, place the probable vaccine arm (tier 1) ahead of the catastrophic time-critical
> ATE landmine (tier 3) — but clinical safety inverts that for the time-critical catastrophic branch:
> **phase 6 rules out B16 (feline ATE) FIRST / in parallel** because it is fast-onset and
> reversible-only-if-caught-early, regardless of its low weight. so the tiers are two independent tracks
> worked concurrently — the probable-driver track and the can't-miss track — not a single ranked queue.
> to read the numbers as a strict priority would re-pool the two axes the floor deliberately kept apart.

1. **vaccine arm (B2, B1 — co-leaders)** — the only formally-anchored leaders; miki in the top-risk cell.
   *(systemic B2 is likely ≥ local B1 because "lethargy±fever" is the most common VAAE presentation, but
   the local-vs-systemic split of the 51.6/10k aggregate is **undetermined by the source** per 3.1's
   denominator caveat — so they are co-leaders, not a firm B2 > B1.)* *(weight axis)*
2. **restraint / prior-arthritis (B11, B14)** — the anecdote layer's heaviest net weight; formal-silent. *(weight axis)*
3. **can't-miss landmines** — low weight, carried entirely on severity. these are **grouped by severity
   level, NOT fully rank-ordered** — the corpus is blind on all of them, so a 1-through-10 sequence would
   be false precision (there is no evidence to place B10 above B12 above B17). only two honest sub-groups:
   - **catastrophic:** B16 (feline ATE), B13 (spinal), B5 (FISS, delayed) — *within this group, B16 (ATE)
     is worked first on clinical time-criticality (rapid, reversible-if-caught), not on a probability
     rank.*
   - **severe:** B15, B18, B4, B10, B12, B9, B17 — *unordered within the group; all worked in phase 6.*
   *(severity axis, kept separate; intra-tier order deliberately coarse to avoid false precision)*
4. **category-possible (B3 FCV)** — kept, hind-limb caveat. *(weight axis)*
5. **below floor (B6, B7, B19, B8, B20)** — not worked up unless a phase-6 result re-elevates them.

## .what the blend settles

- the vaccine arm leads on the **only** real formal denominator, corroborated by the anecdote severe
  tail — the one branch where both streams agree upward.
- the anecdote layer carries **restraint + prior-arthritis** into the workup where the formal layer had
  no denominator — the bracket's wide disagreement kept them live.
- the **severity veto** carries ten low-weight landmines (led by feline ATE) into the workup on
  consequence alone — none is down-weighted for the thin/absent denominator.
- only five branches rest below the floor, and each rests **only** because both streams are weak *and*
  the harm cap is mild.

## .done when

each scenario carries a **bracketed range** (formal lower / anecdote upper, never pooled), a **coarse
ordinal weight + severity**, disagreements are flagged as signal, and the **(probability × severity)
floor** is cut with the 15 survivors named (5 on weight, 10 on severity) and the 5 below-floor branches
named with their rest-reason.
