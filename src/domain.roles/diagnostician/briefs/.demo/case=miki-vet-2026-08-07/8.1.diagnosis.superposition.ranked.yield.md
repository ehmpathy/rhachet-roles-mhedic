# 8.1.diagnosis.superposition.ranked.yield

> the **diagnosis as a superposition** — the live scenarios, probability-ranked on one axis and
> severity-tagged on a separate axis, each carried with its full **consequence package** (best/worst
> bound, prevention, care, acuity band). this is **not** a single verdict; it is the ranked set of
> states miki could be in, each with its consequences attached.
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.** miki was not examined. **this is a
> ranked set of hypotheses, not a confirmed diagnosis.** a hands-on exam + the phase-4 assay plan
> collapse this superposition. if any `7.1` red-line sign is present, call your local emergency vet now.
>
> **recommendation disclaimer.** the prevention and care actions attached below are offered to the
> best of our knowledge from the information found in research — they carry **no guarantee of success
> or outcome**, and each must be reviewed by a **licensed veterinarian** against miki's own real,
> hands-on-examined scenario before any reliance. this is groundwork for that professional review,
> not a substitute for it.

## .the honest hedge (read first)

no scenario below is confirmed or excluded. the ranks are ordinal brackets (P1–P4), not
probabilities-as-numbers. severity is a **separate** axis — a `P4 very-low` branch that is
`catastrophic` stays fully live (`rule.require.superposition-until-elimination`). the **master
lever** is onsior's dose-reversibility: the limp preceded onsior and onsior relieves it, which lifts
the inflammatory-musculoskeletal branches and argues against a fixed structural/vascular lesion —
but "argues against" is not "eliminates", so every catastrophic branch is carried on the veto.

## .the ranked superposition (rank order, each with its consequence package)

### P1 primary — the inflammatory-musculoskeletal core (master lever points here)

**1. B11 — restraint / soft-tissue strain** · P1 primary · **moderate**
- best/worst: settles in days-to-a-week with rest + NSAID / a structural lesion hides under the
  strain label (covered by the veto branches below).
- prevention: low-stress handle at future visits.
- care: rest + gabapentin + onsior (no-regret core, `6.3`).
- acuity: **watch** (`7.3`).

**2. B1 — injection local reaction** · P1 primary · **mild**
- best/worst: self-limited soreness at the two injection sites, settles in days / a deeper local
  reaction or abscess (rare).
- prevention: site rotation, minimal-volume technique (`6.2`).
- care: rest + analgesia; onsior dose-reversibility fits.
- acuity: **watch**.

### P2 plausible

**3. B2 — vaccine-associated local reaction** · P2 plausible · **severe**
- best/worst: transient local inflammation, self-limited / the same inflammation that seeds the FISS
  watch (links to B5).
- prevention: site rotation; adjuvant-free vaccines where a clinician judges apt.
- care: NSAID + site watch.
- acuity: **watch** (+ FISS 3-2-1 tripwire).

**4. B14 — DJD / arthritis flare** · P2 plausible · **moderate**
- best/worst: the flare settles on NSAID / chronic progressive DJD beneath the acute limp.
- prevention: weight + joint management (`6.2`).
- care: NSAID + gabapentin; baseline renal labs before a continued NSAID course.
- acuity: **watch** → same-day if the limp returns each time onsior wears off.

### P3 low

**5. B18 — hepatic lipidosis** · P3 low · **severe**
- best/worst: never triggers (miki eats normally) / days-to-weeks of anorexia → hepatic failure.
- prevention: maintain intake; daily appetite watch.
- care: assisted nutrition if anorexia starts.
- acuity: **watch** → same-day on appetite drop.

**6. B9 — uroabdomen tail** · P3 low · **severe**
- best/worst: the cysto was a **dry tap** (no urine, no breach) → no active clock / an occult
  bladder breach → azotemia + hyperkalemia climb in hours (PMID 23470168).
- prevention: careful cysto technique.
- care: abdominocentesis + fluids only if a breach is found.
- acuity: **watch** (dry-tap = benign bound).

**7. B17 — systemic / metabolic** · P3 low · **severe**
- best/worst: the musculoskeletal picture holds, a screen is clean / an occult systemic disease.
- prevention: n/a.
- care: baseline panel to close the gap.
- acuity: **watch**.

**8. B3 — vaccine/FCV lameness syndrome** · P3 low · **moderate**
- best/worst: self-limited, resolves within ~12 days / a prolonged course.
- prevention: n/a (post-vaccine phenomenon).
- care: supportive + NSAID.
- acuity: **watch**.

### P4 very-low (probability) — but the catastrophic branches stay fully live on severity

**9. B15 — NSAID above-label (harm track)** · P4 very-low as cause · **severe (harm)**
- best/worst: no harm — self-refuted as the *cause* of the limp (the limp preceded onsior) / renal,
  GI, or hepatic toxicity from the 12 mg vs 6 mg dose.
- prevention: correct the dose; renal + hepatic baseline.
- care: dose review + baseline renal/hepatic panel.
- acuity: **safety track** — baseline labs same-day.

**10. B4 — vaccine anaphylaxis** · P4 very-low · **severe (recorded)**
- best/worst: the minutes-to-hours post-vaccine window has passed with no event → effectively closed.
- prevention: post-vaccine observation window.
- care: n/a now (window closed).
- acuity: **watch** (window closed).

**11. B16 — feline ATE** · P4 very-low · **CATASTROPHIC**
- best/worst: not ATE (limbs warm, pulses present, dose-reversible) / death within hours; only ~37%
  survive the initial event (PMID 8581544), guarded-to-poor prognosis (PMID 16594585).
- prevention: clopidogrel if a cardiac cause is found (FATCAT: median-to-recurrence 443 vs 192 days,
  PMID 26776588).
- care: emergency thrombolysis + analgesia — a clinician decision.
- acuity: **EMERGENCY-until-cleared** (`7.1`) — perfusion check lifts it.

**12. B13 — restraint spinal / cord event** · P4 very-low · **CATASTROPHIC**
- best/worst: no cord lesion (function reverses on analgesia) / permanent cord damage; a decompression
  window closes in hours.
- prevention: no manipulation until cleared.
- care: neuro exam + radiographs.
- acuity: **EMERGENCY-until-cleared** (`7.1`) — neuro check lifts it.

**13. B5 — injection-site sarcoma (FISS)** · P4 very-low (acute slot) · **CATASTROPHIC**
- best/worst: acute inflammation, not neoplasia / FISS — fatal if caught late; clean-margin excision
  gives the best survival (PMID 28716129).
- prevention: site rotation; the 3-2-1 lump monitor (Kass, PMID 29217316).
- care: wide excision if a mass that qualifies appears.
- acuity: **watch** → same-day biopsy on a 3-2-1 lump.

**14. B10 — iatrogenic nerve injury** · P4 very-low · **severe**
- best/worst: reversal (onsior relief argues against a fixed deficit) / a permanent deficit — 7/27
  cats showed no improvement (PMID 17614928).
- prevention: injection-site technique.
- care: time + supportive; a neuro baseline anchors the recovery track.
- acuity: **watch** (baseline exam near-term).

**15. B12 — restraint fracture / luxation** · P4 very-low · **severe**
- best/worst: no fracture (function reverses on NSAID) / a displaced fracture worsens with load.
- prevention: gentle handle + rest.
- care: radiograph, then immobilize if confirmed.
- acuity: **same-day** (`7.2`, clearance exam).

## .the shape of the superposition

- **the front-runners** (B11, B1) are benign, watch-band, and dose-reversible — the case *leans*
  benign.
- **the veto branches** (B16, B13, B5, B10, B12) are low-probability but carried at full severity —
  the emergency/same-day floor rides on these, not on the front-runners (the envelope principle).
- **the tail** (B18, B9, B17, B3, B4, B15) is monitor/safety — each attacked down but not out, held
  on a tripwire or a gap-close test.

## .done

assay plan collapse it. this feeds phase 8.2 (the collapse conditions) and phase 9 (the plan).
