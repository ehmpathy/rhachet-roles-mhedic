## .the frame (no-regret vs conflict)

- **no-regret** = a care step that **helps under its scenario and harms none of the others** — so it
  can be started now, before the diagnosis narrows. these seed the phase-9 no-regret core.
- **conflict** = a care step that **helps one branch but harms another** — it cannot be a blanket
  action; phase 9 must arbitrate it via the envelope (gate it behind clearance of the branch it would
  harm).

## .the no-regret core (helps its branch, harms none — start now)

| care step | helps | harmless to others because | citation |
|-----------|-------|----------------------------|----------|
| **rest / activity restriction** | strain B11, fracture B12, spinal B13, nerve B10, DJD flare B14 | rest never worsens a vascular, metabolic, or neoplastic branch | mechanism (restraint-lameness; lameness-emergency-redflags) |
| **gentle / low-stress handle** | all — reduces re-injury + stress | a calm handle harms no branch | `inventory.of=restraint-lameness.feline.md` |
| **perfusion check** (limbs warm, pulses present) | clears the **ATE veto** (B16) | a non-invasive check harms none | `inventory.of=aortic-thromboembolism.feline.md` + lameness-emergency-redflags |
| **neuro check** (motor, proprioception) | clears the **spinal/nerve veto** (B13, B10) | a non-invasive exam harms none | mechanism |
| **appetite + hydration monitor** | guards **hepatic lipidosis** (B18) | monitor harms none | `inventory.of=hepatic-lipidosis.feline.md` (PMID 28978714) |
| **gabapentin analgesia + comfort** | pain of B11/B14/B3/B1/B2/B12/B13 | controls pain **without NSAID renal/cardiac risk**; safe across branches (caveat: renal dose-down, mild sedation) | `inventory.of=feline-analgesia.feline.md` (PMID 23253881) |

**the no-regret analgesic is gabapentin, not the NSAID** — this is the key seed for phase 9: pain can
be treated *now*, safely, before the vetoes clear, because gabapentin sidesteps the NSAID conflict
below.

## .the central conflict — the NSAID (robenacoxib)

the NSAID is the one care step that **helps some branches and harms others**:

- **helps**: the inflammatory / musculoskeletal branches — B1, B2 (local reaction), B11 (strain), B14
  (DJD flare), B3 (FCV) — NSAID analgesia is label-indicated and effective
  (`inventory.of=robenacoxib-onsior.feline.md`).
- **harms / is unsafe under**:
  - **B16 ATE / cardiac** — robenacoxib **prolongs the QT interval** and is **unstudied in cardiac
    disease**; an NSAID in an occult-cardiomyopathy cat is a real hazard (same inventory, FDA label).
  - **B15 / B17 renal** — NSAIDs affect the kidney and **CKD is common in cats**; the label
    **requires baseline labs first** (same inventory; PMID 38587872).
  - **B12 fracture / B13 spinal** — effective analgesia can **mask** pain and invite harmful weight-
    load on an un-cleared fracture or spine.

**the arbitration phase 9 owes**: the NSAID is **gated** — it may be given only after the ATE/cardiac,
renal, and structural (fracture/spine) branches are cleared (perfusion + neuro check + baseline labs).
until then, the **no-regret substitute is gabapentin** (PMID 23253881), which treats the same pain
without the conflict. this is the envelope arbitration, not a blanket start-or-withhold.
