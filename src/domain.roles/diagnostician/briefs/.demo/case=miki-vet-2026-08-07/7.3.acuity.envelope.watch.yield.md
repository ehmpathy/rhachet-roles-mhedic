# 7.3.acuity.envelope.watch.yield

> the **watchful-wait tier** of the acuity envelope — the floor band. these are the live
> scenarios benign enough to observe at home, with an explicit **cadence** and **tripwires** that
> promote a watched case up to same-day (`7.2`) or emergency (`7.1`). this stone **closes the
> envelope**: every live scenario sits in exactly one acuity band.
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.** miki was not examined. watchful wait
> is licensed only under the condition stated below. **if any `7.1` red-line sign is present, this
> is not a watch case — call your local emergency vet now.**

- `7.2.acuity.envelope.sameday.yield.md` — the same-day tier.

## .the watch tier — observe at home

for the 12 watch-band scenarios, the plan is home observation on this cadence:

- **daily** — check the gait (is the limp better, same, or worse?), the appetite (is miki
  eat as normal?), and each hind limb's **warmth + paw-pad color** (a perfusion spot-check).
- **daily** — feel the two injection sites (right thigh: distemper/rabies; left thigh: leukemia)
  for a new or firm lump.
- **continuous** — stay alert for any `7.1` red-line sign (these override the cadence — go now).
- **on each onsior dose** — note whether the limp eases as expected; a limp that no longer
  responds is a tripwire.

## .the tripwires (what promotes a watched case up the envelope)

| tripwire observation | promotes to | which branch it guards |
|----------------------|-------------|------------------------|
| any `7.1` red-line (cold limb, dragged leg, breath distress, collapse) | **emergency now** | B16 / B13 / B12 |
| appetite drops, or miki skips meals past a day or two | **same-day** (`7.2`) | B18 anorexia clock |
| the limp worsens rather than eases on onsior | **same-day** (`7.2`) | B1-family / structural climb |
| a firm lump at an injection site: present > 3 months, or > 2 cm, or grows within 1 month | **same-day** exam | **B5 FISS** (the 3-2-1 rule) |
| limp returns each time onsior wears off, past the expected course | **same-day** exam | B11 DJD / chronic driver |

## .the license statement — watch is conditional, not free

per `rule.require.superposition-until-elimination`, watchful wait is **licensed only** while every
catastrophic branch is either **eliminated**, **held at a higher band**, or **covered by a
tripwire**:

- **B16 ATE** and **B13 spinal** — NOT in watch; they sit at the emergency-until-cleared floor
  (`7.1`), so a home watch never carries an un-held hours-clock branch.
- **B5 FISS** — the one catastrophic branch in the watch band — is covered by the **injection-site
  lump tripwire** (the 3-2-1 rule), which promotes it to a same-day biopsy the moment a mass
  qualifies. it is slow (months-years), so a watch with a lump-tripwire is a sound hold.

**therefore watchful wait is licensed for this case** — but only as long as the clearance exam
(`7.2`) remains awaited-not-refused and the tripwires are actually watched. if the human cannot
watch the tripwires, the case defaults up to the same-day tier.

## .done

the watch tier is stated with its cadence (daily gait/appetite/perfusion + injection-site checks,
continuous red-line alert, per-dose onsior response), the tripwires are listed and each mapped to
the branch it guards, and the envelope is **closed** — all 15 live survivors sit in exactly one
acuity band (2 emergency, 1 same-day, 12 watch). watchful wait is explicitly licensed only under
the superposition condition: no catastrophic branch is left both live and un-covered. this feeds
phase 8 (diagnosis superposition) and phase 9 (the plan honors the whole envelope).
