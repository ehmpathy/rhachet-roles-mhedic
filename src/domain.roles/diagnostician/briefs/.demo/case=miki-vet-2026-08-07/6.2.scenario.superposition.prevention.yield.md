## .the frame (what "prevention + recurrence" is)

each survivor carries:

- **prevention** = the path that stops a recurrence — a protocol, a technique, or an avoided exposure.
- **recurrence** = how likely it returns — untreated, and (where it differs) if the exposure repeats
  vs does not.

the exposure-fork matters most for the **procedure/injection branches** (B1, B2, B5, B10, B13, B12,
B9): the reaction does not recur on its own — it recurs only if the injection/handle is repeated.

## .the exposure-fork, stated plainly

for the injection/handle branches the prevention is the same fact read twice:

- **if the exposure does not repeat** — the reaction/strain/event settles and does **not** recur
  (B1/B2 local reactions settle; B11/B13/B12 strains do not return; B10 nerve does not re-injure).
- **if the exposure repeats** — the risk returns, and for vaccines it **scales with the number given
  per visit** (PMID 39965621). so the prevention *is* to space, minimize, or re-site the next
  exposure.

the two catastrophic branches with a **disease-intrinsic** recurrence (not exposure-gated) are B16
(ATE recurs from ongoing cardiomyopathy — needs clopidogrel, PMID 26776588) and B5 (FISS recurs
locally from incomplete margins, PMID 28716129). these do not settle just by avoidance of a repeat
injection.
