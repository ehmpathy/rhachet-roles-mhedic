## .the literature-derived scenario lineup

each LS names a reported cause, its causal chain, and the verbatim citation that reports it. **no
weights** — the rank waits for phase 3.

### from the vaccines

- **LS1 — vaccine injection-site local reaction (soreness/inflammation).**
  `vaccine → local reaction at the inoculation site → myositis / soft-tissue soreness → limp`.
  corroborates imagination **SC1**. three streams converge.
  > formal, PMID 37632050 (ABCD): VAAEs are "caused by an aberrant … immune reaction, **excessive
  > local reactions at the inoculation site**."
  > professional, Catster (Dr. Karyn Kanowski): "the **combination of a local reaction to the
  > injection and the immune response … can sometimes result in a feverish feline with a sore leg**."
  > social, r/AskVet iq414j (vet DrDead88): "**injection site inflammation/soreness isn't uncommon
  > with vaccines**."

- **LS2 — vaccine systemic adverse event (lethargy / fever / anorexia).**
  `vaccine → systemic immune response → malaise + pyrexia → reduced appetite + limp cluster`.
  corroborates imagination **SC2**; the literature adds that risk scales with vaccine count.
  > formal, PMID 39965621: "most commonly observed VAAEs in cats include mild … **anorexia, and
  > increased body temperature** … The **risk of VAAEs increases with the number of applied
  > vaccines per visit**." (miki got 3 vaccine components.)
  > formal, PMID 17605670 (Moore 2007, 2,560 cases): "~52 VAAEs/10,000 cats; risk greatest ~1yr;
  > lethargy ± fever most common." (miki's exact demographic.)

- **LS3 — vaccine delayed hypersensitivity (the NSAID-refractory worst case).**
  `vaccine → type-I/III hypersensitivity → an amplified local/systemic reaction → welt + pain an NSAID does not settle`.
  corroborates imagination **SC4**; the literature supplies the concrete worst-case course.
  > social, r/cats 1k84hxm: "**Day 4** … a **huge welt near his injection site** … an **anti
  > inflammatory medication that unfortunately did not work** … prescribed a **steroid** … **severe
  > hypersensitivity to his purevax rabies vaccine**."
  > formal, PMID 34789596: 130/316 severe reactions were anaphylaxis — the severe tail exists.

- **LS4 — feline calicivirus (FCV) limping syndrome / post-vaccinal polyarthritis.**
  `FCV antigen (wild or vaccinal) → immune-mediated polyarthritis → multi-joint inflammation → migratory lameness ± pyrexia`.
  **a branch the imagination lineup folded loosely into SC2 — the literature gives it its own,
  distinct mechanism.** a genuine two-stream gain.
  > formal, PMID 35511315 (Balboni 2022): "**limping syndrome is occasionally reported during acute
  > FCV … or as consequence of vaccination**"; polyarthritis confirmed.
  > formal, PMID 8191001: acute FCV arthritis with "**marked pyrexia, lethargy and lameness**."
  > formal, PMID 1328120: calicivirus from an arthritic joint — "**self-limiting … excellent
  > prognosis**." (names the usually-benign course.)
  > social, r/AskVet 1e3b17j: owner reports a post-vaccine limp + warm ears attributed to "a **side
  > effect from the calici virus**."

- **LS5 — injection-site sarcoma / musculoskeletal neoplasia (the delayed cancer tail).**
  `vaccine/injection → chronic inflammatory focus → neoplastic transformation over months → mass + lameness`.
  corroborates imagination **SC3** (delayed hazard).
  > formal, PMID 22247324: an "**important differential** for … **lameness** … FISS … osteosarcoma."

### from the venipuncture

- **LS6 — draw-site hematoma / soft-tissue bruise (transient).**
  `needle → small subcutaneous hemorrhage → hematoma bruise pain → transient limp`.
  corroborates imagination **SC5**; the literature marks it usually mild and self-limited.
  > professional, NaturalPetsHQ (TB Thompson DVM): "a slight swelling on a cat's leg after having
  > blood drawn is **common and usually mild** … usually the result of a small bruise under the skin
  > called a **hematoma**."
  > social, r/Pets 1pzza8u: "the **needle burst one of her blood vessels** … she has a limp." update:
  > "After **another day or two she is back to normal**."

- **LS7 — clipper / antiseptic / handle irritation at the draw leg (non-needle).**
  `clip + swab + hold the leg → superficial skin/soft-tissue irritation → local soreness → mild limp`.
  a sub-branch the imagination lineup did not itemize separately (it sat inside SC5/SC12).
  > professional, NaturalPetsHQ (TB Thompson DVM): "Another reason a cat may limp after a blood draw
  > is **irritation from the clippers, alcohol swab, or handling**."

- **LS8 — draw reads as benign; a persistent limp points elsewhere (a near-null branch).**
  `needle alone → minor site soreness → does NOT sustain a multi-day limp → a persistent limp is another cause`.
  the literature's own vets repeatedly de-attribute a persistent limp from the needle — an
  eliminative signal, not a positive cause.
  > social, r/AskVet mjjx4h (commenter paigejohnson1): "A **blood draw should not cause these kind of
  > symptoms** … Maybe there is just some soreness."
  > social, r/AskVet 1s56l2e (vet zojono): "**I would find it unusual for her to be ok for a few days
  > after the blood draw and then develop the lameness**."

### from the restraint / struggle

- **LS9 — restraint / struggle soft-tissue sprain (usually transient).**
  `forcible restraint + the cat fights it → muscle/ligament sprain → soft-tissue pain → limp, mostly eases in 1–2 days`.
  corroborates imagination **SC8**, but the literature reports the *soft-tissue sprain* form (mild),
  not the fracture/luxation severe extension — a magnitude the imagination stream must keep live.
  > social, r/CATHELP 1nn0y6y (top comment, 772 upvotes): "She may have **sprained it when they were
  > restraining her** … she probably **didn't start limping right away**." a second owner: "**pain
  > from being held down** … **The next day he was way better**."
  > social, r/CATHELP 1nxdp3o (vet): "**sprained the muscles from fighting them** … bruising."

- **LS10 — prior / latent arthritis unmasked or flared by the visit.**
  `handle + restraint stress on a joint with sub-clinical degenerative disease → flare of prior arthritis → joint pain → lameness that outlasts a soreness window`.
  **a branch the imagination lineup MISSED entirely — literature-specific.** a genuine two-stream
  gain: the vets in the corpus reach for arthritis when a limp persists atypically.
  > social, r/AskVet 1s56l2e (vet zojono): "a real possibility she has **begun to develop
  > arthritis** … heavy restraint."
  > social, r/CATHELP 1nxdp3o (vet): "sprain … bruising … **arthritis**."

### from the drug itself (onsior / robenacoxib)

- **LS11 — NSAID (onsior) adverse effect, amplified by an above-label dose.**
  `robenacoxib above the label unit → COX-2 inhibition strain on kidney/GI → renal or GI adverse effect → new illness signs (NOT the limp)`.
  **the imagination lineup treated onsior only as a confounder that masks pain (S5 dose-lock); the
  literature makes the drug its own hazard branch.** the intake flagged miki at 12 mg/day.
  > professional, Cats.com (Dr. Chris Vanderhoof DVM): "Flavored tablets in **6mg are approved for
  > cats**; the 10mg, 20mg, and 40mg sizes are **only approved for use in dogs and should not be used
  > in cats**. The tablets are **not scored, and should not be split**."
  > professional, PetMD (Stephanie Howe DVM): "Onsior is given **once daily for up to three days** …
  > Because Onsior is an NSAID it can **affect the kidneys and digestive system**."
  > formal, PMID 21736587: "recommended minimum dosage … is **1 mg/kg once daily** (range 1–2.4)."
  > formal, PMID 38587872 (2024 ISFM/AAFP): screen before prescribe; **chronic kidney disease common
  > in cats**; monitor.

### the downstream complication (not a cause of the limp — a consequence of the anorexia)

- **LS12 — anorexia → feline hepatic lipidosis cascade.**
  `reduced appetite for days → mobilization of fat to a cat liver that handles it poorly → hepatic lipidosis → grave systemic illness`.
  **a can't-miss DOWNSTREAM branch the imagination lineup MISSED.** it does not explain the limp; it
  is the hazard the appetite-loss (S3) opens, and it is time-sensitive.
  > formal, PMID 29478399: "the cat is one of relatively few species that suffer from HL … an
  > overweight cat that **stops eating for days to weeks**." grave if missed.
  > formal, PMID 28978714: HL "results from **prolonged anorexia**"; stress events a common trigger.

### the benign baseline + the null control

- **LS13 — cumulative procedural soreness (benign anchor).**
  `many procedures in one visit → summed minor soft-tissue soreness → transient limp`.
  corroborates imagination **SC12**.
  > social, r/vet zu0jjc (commenter Anonnnnnn1265): "give it at least **72 hours** … probably be fine
  > … just a **minor irritation** … I bet the limp will be gone."

- **LS14 — limp predates the visit (non-exposure null control).**
  `a limp already present before any procedure → the visit is coincidental, not causal`.
  the honest null the corpus preserves.
  > social, r/cats qffb7u — a limp that began *before* the visit (an uncounted baseline control).
