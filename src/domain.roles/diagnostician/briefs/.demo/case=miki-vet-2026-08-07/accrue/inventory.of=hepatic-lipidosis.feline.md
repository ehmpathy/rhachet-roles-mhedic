## .why cats are uniquely at risk

- the cat is one of few species that suffer HL; an overweight cat that **stops eating for days to
  weeks** can develop it, and it is grave if missed.
  > formal, PMID 29478399.

## .the trigger

- HL **results from prolonged anorexia**; a stress event is a common trigger.
  > formal, PMID 28978714.

## .the derived watch (case-neutral)

- a cat with **reduced appetite for multiple days** — especially after a stress event — carries a
  time-sensitive hepatic-lipidosis hazard; appetite and hydration are the signals to watch, and a
  clinician recheck is warranted before the anorexia extends.
  > derived from the two cited facts above — HL follows **prolonged anorexia** with a stress trigger
  > (PMID 28978714) and is grave if missed (PMID 29478399). no new external claim; a case-neutral
  > watch restated from the cited triggers.
