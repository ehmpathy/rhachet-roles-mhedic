## .the feline label unit (FDA label — the formal anchor)

- the **6 mg** tablet is the feline unit; for oral use in cats only; **not scored**. dose is **1 mg/kg
  once daily, max 3 days, not to exceed one dose per day**; cats **≥ 5.5 lb and ≥ 4 months**.
  > formal, FDA label (DailyMed — ONSIOR robenacoxib 6 mg Tablets for Cats, bhrowser-captured
  > 2026-08-08): "**6 mg Tablets for Cats … For Oral Use In Cats Only … not scored** … The dose … is
  > **1 mg/kg orally once daily, for a maximum of 3 days** … **not to exceed one dose per day** … cats
  > **≥ 5.5 lbs … and ≥ 4 months of age**."

- **appetite must be monitored**, and **cardiac use is unstudied + robenacoxib prolongs the QT
  interval** (a formal caution for a cat with cardiac suspicion).
  > formal, FDA label (DailyMed, 2026-08-08): "**Appetite should be monitored in cats receiving
  > ONSIOR** … The use of ONSIOR **in cats with cardiac disease has not been studied**. ONSIOR **has
  > been shown to prolong the QT interval**."

- the 10/20/40 mg sizes are **dog-only** and should not be used in cats (professional corroboration of
  the feline-unit limit).
  > professional, Cats.com (Dr. Chris Vanderhoof DVM), `cats.com/onsior-for-cats` (retrieved
  > 2026-08-10): "the 10mg, 20mg, and 40mg sizes are **only approved for use in dogs and should not be
  > used in cats**."

- eligibility: cats **4 months and older** and **at least 5.5 lb**.
  > professional, Cats.com (Dr. Chris Vanderhoof DVM), `cats.com/onsior-for-cats` (retrieved
  > 2026-08-10).

## .the schedule + minimum dose

- given **once daily for up to three days**.
  > professional, PetMD (Stephanie Howe DVM), `petmd.com/cat/pharmacy/robenacoxib-cats` (retrieved
  > 2026-08-10).

- recommended minimum dose ~**1 mg/kg once daily** (range 1–2.4).
  > formal, PMID 21736587.

## .the NSAID cautions

- as an NSAID it can **affect the kidneys and the digestive system**; veterinary supervision is
  required.
  > professional, PetMD (Stephanie Howe DVM), `petmd.com/cat/pharmacy/robenacoxib-cats` (retrieved
  > 2026-08-10).

- screen before prescribe; **chronic kidney disease is common in cats**; monitor.
  > formal, PMID 38587872 (2024 ISFM/AAFP NSAID guidelines).

- the label **requires baseline labs + a full history/exam before an NSAID course**, and
  **contraindicates the drug in cats with hypersensitivity/NSAID intolerance**.
  > formal, FDA label (DailyMed, 2026-08-08): "**All cats should undergo a thorough history and
  > physical examination before the initiation of NSAID therapy. Appropriate laboratory tests should
  > be conducted to establish hematological and serum biochemical baseline data prior to
  > administration of an NSAID.** … ONSIOR tablets **should not be used in cats that have a
  > hypersensitivity to robenacoxib or known intolerance to NSAIDs**."

- QT-prolongation carries a **drug-interaction caution**: avoid with other QT-prolonging drugs
  (antihistamines, prokinetics, certain anesthetics).
  > formal, FDA label (DailyMed, 2026-08-08): "the use of robenacoxib **with other drugs shown to
  > prolong the QT interval is not recommended** … antihistamines, prokinetic drugs, and certain
  > anesthetic drugs."

## .the derived flag (case-neutral)

- a total daily dose above the single 6 mg once-daily unit (e.g. 12 mg/day, or a split of a
  dog-only tablet) exceeds the feline label and warrants a clinician recheck of the prescription.
  > derived from the two label facts above — the 6 mg feline unit (Cats.com, retrieved 2026-08-10)
  > and the once-daily schedule (PetMD, retrieved 2026-08-10). no new external claim; a restatement
