## .what it is

- FISS are **malignant skin tumours of mesenchymal origin**; treatment is a challenge, and clean
  surgical margins correlate with disease-free survival.
  > formal, PMID 28716129 (Zabielska-Koczywąs 2017, review): "**Feline injection-site sarcomas (FISS)
  > are malignant skin tumours of mesenchymal origin**, the treatment of which is a challenge for
  > veterinary surgeons … a correlation between '**clean' surgical margins and disease-free survival
  > has been shown** … clean surgical margins are one of the most important factors for achieving a
  > **low recurrence rate**."

## .the worst-case trajectory (what powers the veto)

- a locally aggressive, recurrence-prone malignancy; incomplete excision drives relapse, and the
  disease is fatal if not controlled.
  > formal, PMID 28716129: challenge to treat; clean margins critical for a low recurrence rate (the
  > relapse hazard that the worst-case rides).

## .the best-case trajectory

- with **radical surgery + clean margins** (± radiotherapy), disease-free survival is achievable; a
  transient benign post-injection inflammatory nodule is the common, self-limited alternative that a
  persistent mass must be distinguished from.
  > formal, PMID 28716129: clean margins → disease-free survival; radical surgery with pre/post-op
  > radiotherapy is the most effective control.

## .prevention + recurrence

- **local recurrence** is the dominant failure mode; clean surgical margins are the key to a low
  recurrence rate.
  > formal, PMID 28716129: "clean surgical margins are one of the most important factors for achieving
  > a **low recurrence rate**."

- vaccine-based **prevention recommendations** (site rotation, non-adjuvanted products, fewer
  vaccines) exist but their evidence base is **contested** — a nuance to carry honestly.
  > formal, PMID 29217316 (Kass 2018): "Recently published guidelines have made specific vaccine
  > recommendations purported to potentially reduce the incidence of feline injection-site sarcomas
  > (FISS) … **such guidelines must be regarded with skepticism**" while more research is awaited.

- the broader injection-reaction recurrence axis: **risk scales with the number of vaccines per
  visit**, so fewer-per-visit reduces the reaction rate — `inventory.of=vaccine-adverse-events.feline.md`
  (PMID 39965621).
