> case-neutral, reusable facts on feline pain-control options — the NSAID path and the non-NSAID path
> (gabapentin) — for a musculoskeletal or trauma pain picture. lifted from the bhrowser-verified
> corpus per `rule.require.accrue-research`.
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only. dose decisions belong to a licensed
> clinician.**
>
> **retrieval**: all sources below were captured via the bhrowser (HEADFUL) on **2026-08-12**. PMIDs
> are found at `pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/<pmid>`.

## .the NSAID path (effective, but carries organ risk)

- robenacoxib (Onsior) is the label NSAID for feline musculoskeletal pain, but carries **renal/GI
  risk, a QT-prolongation caution, an unstudied-in-cardiac-disease caution, and a baseline-labs
  requirement** — see `inventory.of=robenacoxib-onsior.feline.md` (FDA label; PMID 38587872).

## .the non-NSAID path (gabapentin)

- gabapentin controls feline **musculoskeletal and trauma pain** at a modest dose and was
  well-tolerated in a long-term case series.
  > formal, PMID 23253881 (2013 case series, 3 cats): "the long-term use of gabapentin for
  > musculoskeletal pain or head trauma … average dose of **6.5 mg/kg q12h** … signs suggestive of
  > pain … **decrease with the administration of gabapentin** … **satisfactory pain management was
  > achieved, administration was easy and no obvious side effects** … a **valuable adjunct** for the
  > management of chronic pain in cats."

- the practical value: gabapentin gives analgesia **without the NSAID renal/cardiac risk**, so it is
  the pain option that does not trip the ATE/renal/cardiac veto. caveat: it is renally excreted (dose
  down in kidney disease) and can sedate.

## .the derived no-regret read (case-neutral)

- when a pain picture is present but an NSAID is contraindicated or not-yet-safe (cardiac suspicion,
  renal risk, an un-cleared fracture/spine), **gabapentin analgesia + rest** is the pain path that
  helps the musculoskeletal branches and does not worsen the vascular/renal/structural branches.
  > derived from the gabapentin tolerability above (PMID 23253881) and the NSAID cautions
  > (`inventory.of=robenacoxib-onsior.feline.md`). no new external claim.
