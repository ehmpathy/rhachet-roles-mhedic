## .the mechanism (injection is a documented cause)

- iatrogenic sciatic nerve injury has been documented after an **intramuscular injection**; a
  caudal-thigh injection can strike or irritate the sciatic nerve.
  > formal, PMID 17614928 (Forterre 2007; 18 dogs, 9 cats): "**Nerve injury occurred after
  > intramuscular injection (1 cat, 1 dog).**" (most cases were surgical; injection is the minority,
  > documented route.)

## .the worst-case trajectory

- a permanent monoparesis: some animals show no recovery, and the overall prognosis is poor.
  > formal, PMID 17614928: "Within 1 year, 13 patients recovered completely, clinical improvement
  > occurred in 7, and **there was no improvement in 7** … Iatrogenic sciatic nerve injury … **had a
  > poor prognosis**."

## .the best-case trajectory

- a neurapraxia (a stretch/contusion without transection) can recover fully — about half recovered
  completely within a year.
  > formal, PMID 17614928: "Within 1 year, **13 [of 27] patients recovered completely**, clinical
  > improvement occurred in 7."
