# inventory.of=vaccine-adverse-events.feline

> case-neutral, reusable facts on feline vaccine-associated adverse events (VAAEs), lifted from the
> bhrowser-verified corpus per `rule.require.accrue-research`. these are the *base rate + mechanism*
> — the magnitude a case-specific analysis borrows, not a claim about any one patient.
>
> ⚠️ **not veterinary/medical advice — informational only.**
>
> **retrieval**: all sources below were captured via the bhrowser (HEADFUL) on **2026-08-10**. PMIDs
> are found at `pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/<pmid>`.

## .incidence + demographics

- **~52 VAAEs per 10,000 cats** vaccinated; risk greatest around **1 year of age**; lethargy ± fever
  the most common presentation.
  > formal, PMID 17605670 (Moore 2007, 2,560 cases).

- risk **scales with the number of vaccines given per visit**.
  > formal, PMID 39965621: "The **risk of VAAEs increases with the number of applied vaccines per
  > visit**."

## .most common signs

- mild, self-limited: **anorexia and increased body temperature** are among the most commonly
  observed feline VAAEs.
  > formal, PMID 39965621.

## .mechanism

- an aberrant immune reaction, such as **excessive local reactions at the inoculation site**.
  > formal, PMID 37632050 (ABCD review).

- a **local reaction plus the systemic immune response** together can yield a feverish cat with a
  sore leg — vaccination over a limb localizes the soreness.
  > professional, Catster (Dr. Karyn Kanowski), `catster.com/cat-health-care/cat-limping-after-vaccine`
  > (retrieved 2026-08-10).

- small body size and multiple concurrent vaccines raise reaction risk.
  > professional, PetMD (Brittany Kleszynski DVM), `petmd.com/cat/general-health/cat-vaccine-reactions`
  > (retrieved 2026-08-10): "**kittens are at the highest risk because of their small size** … if
  > **multiple vaccines are given at once, the chance for a reaction increases**."

## .the severe tail (rare but real)

- a delayed hypersensitivity can be NSAID-refractory and need a steroid; anaphylaxis occurs.
  > formal, PMID 34789596: 130/316 severe reactions were anaphylaxis.
