## .the emergency signs (take the cat in immediately)

- **severe pain / a dragging leg / a leg held at an awkward angle / a cold, limp leg or bluish paw
  pad / difficulty breathing.**
  > professional, Cats.com (Dr. Nat Scroggie MRCVS), `cats.com/why-is-my-cat-limping` (retrieved
  > 2026-08-10): "take them … immediately: **Severe pain / Dragging leg / Leg hanging at an awkward
  > angle … Cold, limp leg or bluish paw pad / Difficulty breathing**."

- note: a **cold, limp leg or bluish pad** is the classic aortic-thromboembolism red-flag — the
  can't-miss vascular branch. a limp that reads benign can mask it early.

## .baseline interpretation

- a limp is generally a **sign of pain**, more visible in the **back legs since they bear more
  weight**; a limping cat can lash out when handled.
  > professional, PetMD (Jennifer Coates DVM), `petmd.com/cat/symptoms/why-is-my-cat-limping`
  > (retrieved 2026-08-10) + UrgentVet (Dr. Jim Dobies DVM), `urgentvet.com/cat-limping` (retrieved
  > 2026-08-10).

## .the systemic-concern add-on

- when a limp pairs with **not eating** or other systemic signs, escalate to the veterinarian rather
  than watch.
  > professional, PetMD (Brittany Kleszynski DVM), `petmd.com/cat/symptoms/why-is-my-cat-limping`
  > (retrieved 2026-08-10): "if your cat is … **not eating, contact your veterinarian**."
