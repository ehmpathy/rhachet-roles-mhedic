## .the worst-case trajectory (what powers the veto)

- an acute, intensely painful embolic event; even with care, most cats do not survive the initial
  episode.
  > formal, PMID 8581544 (100 cats with distal ATE reviewed): average age 7.7 years; "**the average,
  > long-term survival in the 37% of cases which survived**" — i.e. only ~37% survived the initial
  > event.
  > formal, PMID 11714239 (44 cats, 49 episodes): "**Heart failure was a concurrent diagnosis in 51%
  > of episodes and 36% of these cases survived**."

- overall a guarded-to-poor prognosis disease.
  > formal, PMID 16594585: "**Feline distal aortic thromboembolism is a frustrating disease that
  > warrants a guarded to poor prognosis.**"

## .the best-case trajectory

- among the ~1 in 3 cats that survive the acute event, limb perfusion and function can recover over
  time with anticoagulant/supportive therapy; but this is the minority outcome.
  > formal, PMID 8581544: the 37% that survived carried a measurable long-term survival — the benign
  > tail of a mostly-grave disease.

## .prevention + recurrence (secondary prophylaxis)

- ATE is **cardiogenic** and **recurs** without prophylaxis; **clopidogrel** cuts the recurrence risk
  vs aspirin and lengthens the time to a repeat event.
  > formal, PMID 26776588 (Hogan 2015, FAT CAT trial): "**Clopidogrel administration was associated
  > with significantly reduced likelihood of recurrent CATE compared to aspirin (p = 0.024)** and had
  > a longer median time to recurrence [**443 … days vs. 192 … days**]"; also reduced the composite of
  > recurrent CATE or cardiac death.

- a notable base-rate point: most cats had **no prior recorded heart disease** before the event.
  > formal, PMID 26776588: "**Only 15% (11/75) of cats had a history of heart disease recorded prior
  > to the CATE event.**" — so an absent cardiac history does not clear the branch.

## .the tell (why a limp must clear it)

- the classic sign is a **cold, limp leg or bluish paw pad** with absent pulses — a limp that reads
  benign can mask early ATE.
  > see `inventory.of=lameness-emergency-redflags.feline.md` (Cats.com, Dr. Nat Scroggie): "**Cold,
  > limp leg or bluish paw pad**" among the take-them-in-immediately signs.
