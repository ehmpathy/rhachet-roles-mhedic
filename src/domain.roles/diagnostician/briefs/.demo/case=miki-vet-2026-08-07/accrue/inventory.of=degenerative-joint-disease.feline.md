## .the base rate (it is common)

- feline DJD carries a **high prevalence** and is under-recognized.
  > formal, PMID 20210938 (critical review): "**The high prevalence of feline DJD** and lack of
  > information about" it (a call-out that DJD is both common and under-studied).

## .the worst-case trajectory

- a chronic, progressive osteoarthritis: pain and reduced mobility persist and worsen over months to
  years without management (chronic, not acutely fatal).
  > derived from PMID 20210938 — DJD is degenerative and highly prevalent; the review frames it as a
  > persistent, progressive burden rather than a self-limited event. no new external claim.

## .the best-case trajectory

- an acute flare (e.g. after handle stress) settles with NSAID analgesia and rest, and the cat returns
  to baseline mobility.
  > supported by `inventory.of=robenacoxib-onsior.feline.md` (NSAID analgesia is the label-indicated
  > relief) and `inventory.of=restraint-lameness.feline.md` (a handle-stress flare of sub-clinical
  > arthritis, vet-attested).
