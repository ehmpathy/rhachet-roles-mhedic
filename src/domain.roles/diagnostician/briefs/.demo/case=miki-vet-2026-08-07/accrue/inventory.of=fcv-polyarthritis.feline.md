## .what it is

- an immune-mediated polyarthritis linked to FCV antigen (wild-type infection **or** vaccination),
  which presents as an acute, often migratory lameness ± pyrexia.
  > formal, PMID 35511315 (Balboni 2022): "**limping syndrome is occasionally reported during acute
  > FCV … or as consequence of vaccination**"; polyarthritis confirmed.

## .presentation

- acute FCV arthritis carries "**marked pyrexia, lethargy and lameness**."
  > formal, PMID 8191001.

- a lag between antigen exposure and lameness onset is reported (up to ~12 days).
  > formal, PMID 37889723 (Lanave 2023).

## .prognosis

- usually self-limited with an excellent prognosis.
  > formal, PMID 1328120: calicivirus from an arthritic joint — "**self-limiting … excellent
  > prognosis**."
