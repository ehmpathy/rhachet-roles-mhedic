# whento.apply-causal-chain-frame

## .what

the triggers that call for the causal-chain frame (`define.causal-chain-frame`), and when it is
the wrong tool.

> ⚠️ **not medical / veterinary advice — informational only.**

## .reach for it when

- **an effect:symptom follows one or more cause:exposures** and you must attribute which lever
  drove it — the archetypal "she got X, now she shows Y" case.
- **several exposures fired together** (a visit stacked vaccine + draw + restraint) and they are
  **confounded** — the frame's cause-side split + disentangle stats are built for exactly this.
- **the same symptom has many candidate lesions** — you need the effect:mechanism enumeration to
  avoid fixation on the first guess.
- **prevention matters as much as treatment** — the cause:* side gives you the lever to change so
  it does not recur, even before the exact lesion is known.
- **a defect review** — when a routine action produced disproportionate harm (pain that outlasts
  the expected window), the frame separates "which action" from "what damage".

## .do NOT reach for it when

- **the cause is already unambiguous** — one exposure, one obvious lesion, a known pathway. the
  frame is overhead; just note it.
- **it is a pure emergency-triage moment** — acuity first (`4.triage`); trace the chain after she
  is safe.
- **there is no observable effect:symptom yet** — with no anchor, the trace has no fixed point;
  gather the presentation first (`1.intake`).
- **the question is treatment, not cause** — that is the prescriber's work; the frame stops at
  the diagnosis.

## .the tell

ask: *"do i know which action caused this, and through what pathway?"*
- confident on both → skip the frame, note the answer
- unsure on either → trace the chain
