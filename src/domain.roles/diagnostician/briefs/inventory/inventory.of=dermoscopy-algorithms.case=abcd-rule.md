## .facts

### fact: ABCD-rule-of-dermoscopy (Stolz) at total dermoscopy score > 4.75

- **fact**: the ABCD rule of dermoscopy at its standard positivity threshold of total dermoscopy score > 4.75 had, for image-based reads (10 datasets, 4242 lesions, 816 melanomas), pooled sensitivity 0.81 and specificity 0.72.
- **verbatim**: "ABCD at > 4.75 | 1 | 309 (73) | 0.83 (0.69 to 0.92) | 0.45 (0.39 to 0.51) | 10 | 4242 (816) | 0.81 (0.67 to 0.90) | 0.72 ..."
- **url**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6517096/
- **tier**: peer-reviewed (cochrane systematic review)

### fact: ABCD-rule-of-dermoscopy at higher threshold > 5.45 raises specificity

- **fact**: at the higher threshold ABCD > 5.45, in-person reads (4 datasets, 1203 lesions, 155 melanomas) had sensitivity 0.78 and specificity 0.93; image-based (7 datasets, 2471 lesions, 406 melanomas) had sensitivity 0.81 and specificity 0.81. the threshold governs the sensitivity/specificity trade-off.
- **verbatim**: "ABCD at > 5.45 | 4 | 1203 (155) | 0.78 (0.58 to 0.90) | 0.93 (0.79 to 0.98) | 7 | 2471 (406) | 0.81 (0.60 to 0.92) | 0.81 (0.69 to 0.89)"
- **url**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6517096/
- **tier**: peer-reviewed (cochrane systematic review)
