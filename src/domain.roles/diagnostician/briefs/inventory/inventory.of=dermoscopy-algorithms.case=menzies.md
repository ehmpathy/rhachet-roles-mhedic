### fact: Menzies method original criteria

- **fact**: four datasets (1856 lesions, 317 melanomas) evaluated the Menzies criteria as in the original 1996 paper; pooled sensitivity was 78% and specificity 63%.
- **verbatim**: "Four datasets evaluated the Menzies criteria using the method described in the original Menzies 1996 paper, pooled sensitivity was 78% (95% CI 38% to 96%), and specificity 63% (95% CI 39% to 81%), (1856 lesions and 317 melanomas)."

- **tier**: peer-reviewed (cochrane systematic review)
