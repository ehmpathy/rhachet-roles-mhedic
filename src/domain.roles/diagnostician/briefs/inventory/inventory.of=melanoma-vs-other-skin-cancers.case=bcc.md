## .facts

### fact: BCC is ~80% of skin cancers, slow, rarely metastasizes

- **fact**: BCC is the most common skin cancer (about 8 of 10), develops on sun-exposed areas, grows slowly, and very rarely spreads — but if untreated can invade bone or nearby tissue.
- **verbatim**: "Basal cell carcinoma (BCC ...) is most common type of skin cancer. About 8 out of 10 skin cancers are basal cell carcinomas. ... They tend to grow slowly. It's very rare for a basal cell cancer to spread to other parts of the body. But if BCC is left untreated, it can grow into nearby areas and invade the bone or other tissues beneath the skin."
- **url**: https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/basal-and-squamous-cell-skin-cancer/about/what-is-basal-and-squamous-cell.html
- **tier**: professional (American Cancer Society)

### fact: BCC appearance — pearly/shiny bump or scaly patch, scabs and bleeds

- **fact**: BCC often looks like a round, pearly or shiny bump or a raised scaly patch, grows slowly, rarely spreads beyond the skin, and can scab and bleed easily.
- **verbatim**: "Most common type of skin cancer ... Often looks like a round, pearly or shiny bump or a raised and scaly patch of skin. Grows slowly and rarely spreads beyond the skin. Can scab and bleed easily."
- **url**: https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/skin-cancer/types/common
- **tier**: professional society (American Academy of Dermatology)
