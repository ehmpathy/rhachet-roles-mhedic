### fact: 7-point checklist (7PCL) at >= 3 (standard positivity threshold)

- **fact**: the 7-point checklist at the standard threshold of >= 3 points had, for image-based reads (11 datasets, 3408 lesions, 798 melanomas), pooled sensitivity 0.80 and specificity 0.67; for in-person reads (2 datasets, 11,137 lesions, 127 melanomas) the same >= 3 threshold read sensitivity 0.67 and specificity 0.96.
- **verbatim**: "7PCL at >= 3 | 2 | 11137 (127) | 0.67 (0.46 to 0.83) | 0.96 (0.88 to 0.99) | 11 | 3408 (798) | 0.80 (0.63 to 0.91) | 0.67 (0.51 to 0.80)"
