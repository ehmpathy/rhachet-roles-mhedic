## .facts — the candidate set

### fact: a documented pigmented-lesion differential candidate set

- **fact**: a diagnostic-accuracy study's lesion set for pigmented-lesion assessment comprised melanoma, melanoma in situ, lentigo, benign naevi (compound, junctional and low-grade dysplastic naevi), dermatofibroma, seborrhoeic keratosis, and haemangioma.
- **verbatim**: "the study included only lesions with a final diagnosis of melanoma, melanoma in situ, lentigo, benign naevi (including compound, junctional and low grade dysplastic naevi), dermatofibroma, sebhorrhoeic keratosis and haemangioma"
- **url**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6517294/
- **tier**: peer-reviewed (cochrane systematic review, quoting Wolf 2013)

### fact: the recognised histological subtypes of cutaneous melanoma

- **fact**: the NCI PDQ lists melanoma subtypes: superficial spreading, nodular, lentigo maligna, acral lentiginous (palmar/plantar and subungual), and miscellaneous unusual types (mucosal lentiginous, desmoplastic, verrucous).
- **verbatim**: "Nodular. Lentigo maligna. Acral lentiginous (palmar/plantar and subungual). Miscellaneous unusual types: Mucosal lentiginous (oral and genital). Desmoplastic. Verrucous."
- **url**: https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/hp/melanoma-treatment-pdq
- **tier**: regulator/government (NCI PDQ)

### fact: melanoma often arises in normal skin, not only from a prior nevus

- **fact**: more than half of melanoma cases arise in apparently normal areas of skin (i.e. not from a visibly prior mole).
- **verbatim**: "Melanoma occurs predominantly in adults, and more than 50% of the cases arise in apparently normal areas of the skin."
- **url**: https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/hp/melanoma-treatment-pdq
- **tier**: regulator/government (NCI PDQ)

## .facts — base rates / number-needed-to-excise (NNE)

### fact: NNE for melanoma among excised nodular lesions

- **fact**: in a study of 1,319 excised nodular lesions, the number needed to excise (NNE) for any malignancy was 3.9 and the NNE for melanoma was 13.2 (about 13 nodular lesions excised per melanoma found); NNE for hypo/amelanotic melanoma was 27.3.
- **verbatim**: "A total of 1,319 excised nodular lesions formed the study set. The NNE for any malignancy was 3.9 (634/164), while the NNE for melanoma was 13.2 (634/48). NNE for hypo and amelanotic melanoma was 27.3 (327/12)."
- **url**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5661152/
- **tier**: peer-reviewed (Journal of the European Academy of Dermatology & Venereology)

### fact: primary-care lesion volume context

- **fact**: a systematic review of dermoscopy in primary care synthesised 23 studies representing 49,769 lesions, and found dermoscopy (with training) improved diagnostic accuracy and reduced unnecessary excisions and referrals.
- **verbatim**: "23 studies met the review criteria, representing 49 769 lesions and 3708 PCPs ... Dermoscopy, with appropriate training, was associated with improved diagnostic accuracy for melanoma and benign lesions, and reduced unnecessary excisions and referrals."
- **url**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6707687/
- **tier**: peer-reviewed (BMJ Open systematic review)

## .facts — role of the differential in triage

### fact: excision decision rests on the confident exclusion of benign diagnoses

- **fact**: one validated management strategy excises any nodular lesion that cannot be confidently and precisely classified as a benign tumor after clinical and dermoscopic examination — the differential resolves to "confidently benign" versus "excise".
- **verbatim**: "the excision of any nodular lesion that cannot be confidently and precisely classified as a benign tumor after clinical and dermoscopic examination"
- **url**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5661152/
- **tier**: peer-reviewed (JEADV)
