# mhedic roles

roles for medical and health research, assessment, and guidance.

> ⚠️ **not medical advice.** these roles are informational only. consult a licensed clinician for any health matter.

## 🩺 diagnostician

take the history, narrow the differential, decide which tests to order, and judge urgency.

## 💊 prescriber

turn a cause into a concrete plan — medications, procedures, and the care that follows.

## 🧭 referrer

route to the right care — which specialist to see, where to go, and where to get the tests done.

## 🛡️ preventer

stay ahead of disease — screen tests, vaccines, risk factors, and lifestyle.

## 🧬 phylogeneticist

reconstruct where a biological mechanism arose on the tree of life and who inherits it — the clade library that tells other roles whether a mechanism transfers across species.

## ⚕️ physician

compose diagnostician, prescriber, and referrer into one coherent whole-person view.
