# domain.term.choice.reason: accrue

## .etymology

`accrue` = to accumulate value over time, as interest accrues on a principal. the connotation is
exactly right: each case *deposits* cited facts into a shared base, and that base *grows in value*
the more cases contribute — knowledge that compounds, like compound interest. the word also implies
the deposit is **kept and grows**, not discarded.

chosen over:
- **`cache`** — a cache is a throwaway, invalidatable copy for speed; it connotes impermanence and
  no curation. an accrual is durable, cited, and meant to be promoted — the opposite of a cache.
- **`collect` / `stash`** — neutral about permanence and growth; they name the act of collection, not
  the compound-over-time value that is the whole point.
- **`log`** — an append-only record of events, not a curated, reusable, promotable fact base.

## .evidence

- discovery: `rule.require.accrue-research` needed a verb for "lift the reusable fact into a home
  where it compounds across cases." the interest-accrual metaphor captures the compound intent that
  `cache`/`collect` miss.
- composed as the `accrue/` route dir + the `.accrue` clause every research stone carries.

## .disputes

none open. settled this round with the accrue-research rule.
