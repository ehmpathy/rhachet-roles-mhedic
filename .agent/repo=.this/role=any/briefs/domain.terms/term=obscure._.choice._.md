# domain.term: obscure

term.chosen   = obscure
term.kind     = noun · adj · verb (one concept, three inflections — as `fullsun`)
term.glyph    = 🕶️
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- secret (asserts a permanent state. 🔴 obscure is a DELAY with a trigger — the opposite claim)
- private (names the AUDIENCE, not the value at risk)
- confidential (borrows a legal posture that belongs to 🔒 protect, one step up the gradient)
- internal (names a LOCATION. an obscure artifact may sit in a public repo, held by its trigger)
- redacted (names a TRANSFORM already applied; obscure is the verdict that calls for one)
- withheld (names the act of a hold, never the reason value is held)

⚠️ **`scrub` is NOT forbidden — it is the declared verdict alias.** the posture leads, the verdict rides.

## .what

**the posture of an artifact whose value is its RARITY** — value that is first, uncopied, not yet
obvious. exposure costs a competitive lead, and that loss is **reversible**: you can out-execute it.

obscure is the middle step of the light gradient declared in `term=fullsun._.choice._.md`.

## 🔴 .the differentia — obscure is a DELAY, never a state

> **obscurity is a delay, not a right.**

this is the one property that parts it from both neighbours, and the one a reader most often misses:

| | ☀️ fullsun | 🕶️ **obscure** | 🔒 protect |
|---|---|---|---|
| value is… | daylight | **rarity** | a right you hold |
| a leak costs | no loss | **a lead — reversible** | a right — **irreversible** |
| does it move? | it is the destination | 🔴 **yes — it MUST**, on a written trigger | it does not move |

⇒ so an obscure artifact is incomplete without a **graduation trigger** — a real, checkable event
that sends it to fullsun. ⚠️ **an obscure tag with no trigger is not a posture; it is an unbounded
hold**, and it forfeits the fullsun upside to inertia rather than to judgment.

```md
🕶️ **obscure → ☀️ fullsun** — recommendation settles live optionality; hold until <trigger>.
graduation trigger: <a real, checkable event>.
```

## .what earns the posture — three of the four leak tests

`rule.require.publishability-triage` runs four tests; **three of them land here**, and that
concentration is why obscure is the posture most often assigned and most often assigned wrong:

| test | what it catches |
|---|---|
| **optionality** | a recommendation that settles a choice the law did not force |
| **insight** | the aha-cluster — the conclusion survives de-identification. handle by **split**: publish the terrain, hold the synthesis sentence |
| **voice** | advisory frame — one actor always lands safe. handle by **rewrite to neutral** |
| fingerprint (applied narration) | the instrument-narrated half; the mechanic half escalates to 🔒 protect |

## .invariants

- 🔴 **an obscure artifact carries a graduation trigger, or the tag is incomplete**
- obscure is a **posture about VALUE**, never about harm. an artifact held because exposure hurts a
  person is 🔒 protect, never obscure — ⚠️ the two are confused precisely because both mean *"do not
  publish yet"*, and only one of them has a *"yet"*
- **an aha-cluster is not a moat.** it may be worth a hold for hygiene alone; tag it and split it,
  do not overprotect it
- ⚠️ **obscure is a SET-level verdict too.** the voice test fires across a corpus: every brief may be
  individually clean while the set is advisory ⇒ **obscure can be the right tag for a set whose every
  member is fullsun**
