# domain.term.choice.reason: hazard

## .etymology

"hazard" is the safety / epidemiology word for a *source of potential harm* — distinct from
"risk", which is the *probability* that the harm occurs. a hazard exists whether or not it ever
materializes, which matches the rule's need to name every effect an exposure *could* cause,
before any has appeared.

## .disputes

### dispute: risk — raised 2026-08-10 — status: RESOLVED (keep `hazard`)
- raised.by  = owner
- claim      = "risk" names the thing to watch for
- counter    = risk = probability (a number); hazard = the harm itself (what can occur). to record
               a "risk" conflates the effect with its likelihood, which the hazard table keeps as
               separate columns (hazard vs likelihood). keep hazard for the effect.
- resolution = keep `hazard`; record `risk` as a forbidden synonym.

### dispute: side-effect — raised 2026-08-10 — status: RESOLVED (keep `hazard`)
- claim      = "side-effect" is the familiar word for a vaccine's harms
- counter    = side-effect is informal and includes benign, expected reactions; hazard scopes to
               the *adverse* potential that warrants a monitor + alarm.
- resolution = keep `hazard`; record `side-effect` as a forbidden synonym.
