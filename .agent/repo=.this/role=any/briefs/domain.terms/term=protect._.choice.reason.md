# domain.term.choice.reason: protect

## .etymology

**protect** — latin *protegere*, "to cover in front," from *pro-* "before" + *tegere* "to cover."

🔴 **note what it shares with its neighbour and what it adds.** `obscurus` also means covered — so
both middle and top rungs are cover-words, and the etymology alone would not part them. what
*protegere* adds is the **`pro-`**: a shield placed **in front of** something, by someone, for
someone.

⇒ **that is the differentia, and it is the right one.** the posture is not about how dark the cover
is; it is about **whose right is behind it**. a patient's privacy, a client's privilege, an owner's
moat — each is a right held by a party who is not the author.

chosen over the rejected set for one reason each:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `secret` | names a **state of concealment** and no holder. the holder is the point — *"protect whose right?"* is answerable; *"secret whose?"* is not |
| `confidential` | the nearest miss. it names a **legal category**, and this gradient's axis is value-at-risk. it also covers only the privilege species, never privacy or moat |
| `private` | names the **audience**. an artifact can be private and merely 🕶️ obscure |
| `classified` | borrows a **state-security** frame this repo does not have, and imports a clearance model with it |
| `forbidden` | names the **act barred**, never the value guarded. a posture must say why |
| `deleted` | 🔴 **factually wrong.** a protect artifact is KEPT, in its owner's own space |

## .disputes

### dispute: does the verdict alias `prune` mislead? — raised 2026-09-08 — status: RESOLVED (no — `prune` is fine)

- raised.by = me, against the `.reporting` mandate in `rule.require.publishability-triage`
- claim = 🔴 **`prune` denotes removal and the action is RELOCATION**, so the alias mis-reports; the
  rule's own text says the material *"belongs in the client's own private space"* — moved, not
  destroyed. a reader who acts on a bare `prune:` deletes an owner's work
- counter, from the wisher, verbatim: **"prune is fine"**
- 🔴 **and the counter holds, for a reason the claim never checked.** the gradient is
  **horticultural** — `fullsun` is a plant tag (`term=fullsun._.choice.reason.md`). **`prune` is a
  gardener's verb in that same frame**, and in it:

  | the horticultural sense | the mapped action |
  |---|---|
  | a prune cuts a branch **from the plant** | the artifact is cut **from the published tree** |
  | a prune is done **for the plant's health**, never to kill it | the shippable set is healthier without it |
  | a pruned branch's fate is a **separate matter** | where the artifact goes is stated by the rule, not by the verb |

  ⇒ **the verb never claimed to name the destination**, so it under-specifies exactly as `share` and
  `scrub` do. it does not assert a false one
- resolution = **`prune` is fine.** a co-term of equal standing with `share` and `scrub`. the
  posture still leads a report, for the one reason the rule already gives: **severity**

🔴 **the error was mine, and its shape is worth more than the conclusion.** I enumerated `prune`'s
ordinary-english sense and never tested it against the **metaphor its own sibling term had already
established** — with `fullsun`'s horticultural etymology written in the very cluster I authored one
round earlier.

⇒ `rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name` (bhrain/learner) says to list every instance a word must
cover before you judge it. **a frame is an instance.** ⚠️ **a word inside a live metaphor must be
judged inside it** — read against ordinary english alone, an in-frame term reads as a defect.

### dispute: are the three sources of the right one term or three? — raised 2026-09-08 — status: RESOLVED (one)

- raised.by = privacy, privilege, and moat feel like three different concerns with three different
  owners, and `rule.forbid.pii` treats privacy as its own critical rule
- claim = split into `protect.privacy` / `protect.privilege` / `protect.moat`
- counter = **the disposition is identical in all three** — never publish, no graduation trigger,
  relocate to the owner's space, and a red-team recovery is a blocker. ⇒ a split would triple the
  vocabulary and change no action
- resolution = one term. ⚠️ **the species differ in whose right it is, and that difference belongs in
  the ARTIFACT's one-line reason, not in the term.** `rule.forbid.pii` remains a separate rule
  because privacy needs its own strip procedure — a *how*, not a *posture*

## .evidence

### the asymmetry that justifies "when in doubt, be more restrictive"

| step | what a wrong call costs |
|---|---|
| fullsun → obscure | a re-review. the artifact was publishable and was held |
| 🔴 obscure → protect | **no recovery at all.** the artifact was a right, and it was published |

⇒ the gradient's steps are **not evenly spaced**. that is why the tie-break is not caution for its
own sake — it is an asymmetric bet against an irreversible loss.

### the control — an artifact that is true, useful, and still never ships

a research yield that is fully cited, entirely accurate, and broadly useful, which narrates a
client's applied fund choreography. it fails no quality test at all.

⇒ 🔴 **quality is not the axis.** the term discriminates on *whose right the value belongs to*, and
a term that could not reject a high-quality artifact would not discriminate at all.

## .invariants

- a protect artifact has **no graduation trigger** — the absence is definitional, never an omission
- protect is **relocation, never destruction**
- ⚠️ an artifact may be true, well-cited, and useful, and still 🔒 protect
- the red-team gate is **asymmetric**: one recovery of a protect item from the fullsun set is a
  blocker, and one clean pass is not proof
