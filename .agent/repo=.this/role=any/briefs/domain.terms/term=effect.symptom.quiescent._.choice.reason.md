# domain.term.choice.reason: effect.symptom.quiescent

## .etymology

**quiescent** = at rest, dormant, *temporarily* inactive — and the temporariness is carried by the
word itself. that is the part that bears the load: a quiescent volcano is not an extinct one.

it is a term of art in medicine and geology alike, and in both it names **a state that persists
while the process beneath it persists.** ⇒ adopted rather than coined.

## .why it was owed — a defect I committed in a readout to the owner

2026-09-07. the owner reported *"the very next morning after the pain was noticable, it totally went
away"* (verbatim). the readout answered, in a summary table:

> *"the **acute** picture — largely stood down by two days of resolution"*

🔴 **that is elimination by symptom abatement, and no assay ran.**
`rule.require.superposition-until-elimination` grades a scenario *"dropped from the superposition by
probability alone"* a **blocker**; this is the same move by a different lever, and the rule's list of
three non-eliminators did not name it.

⚠️ **the readout DID carry the warn** — a *"the trap"* section named the perforation signature and
the ectopic quiet interval explicitly. ⇒ **the defect is not that the concept was absent; it is that
the concept had no NAME, so the summary row could not carry it.** a warn in prose and a verdict in a
table are read by different readers, and the table is what survives a skim.

⇒ **the summary said `resolved`; the prose said "maybe quiescent"; the artifact had no word to make
them agree.**

## .the disputes

### dispute: `lull` / `interval` — raised 2026-09-07 — status: RESOLVED (keep `quiescent`)

- claim: the readout reached for *"quiet interval"* and *"lull"*, and both read plainly
- counter: 🔴 both name a **span of time**, never a **state of the symptom**. so an artifact would
  still need a second word for the symptom itself — synonym sprawl by construction. ⚠️ **this is the
  third time in three rounds that the same counter has settled a dispute** (`span` for
  `citation.excerpt`, `separation` for `adjacency`, now `lull`): *a measure or a span is not the
  object; do not name an object with a word for its extent.*
- resolution: keep `quiescent`; `lull` and `interval` forbidden

### dispute: `remission` — raised 2026-09-07 — status: RESOLVED (keep `quiescent`)

- claim: the established clinical word for a disease that has gone quiet
- counter: `remission` is a property of a **disease** and it implies a **known diagnosis** — you can
  only be in remission from a named one. 🔴 **this axis exists precisely where the diagnosis is
  UNKNOWN and the superposition is live.** to use it would presuppose the elimination the term is
  built to prevent
- resolution: keep `quiescent`; `remission` forbidden

### dispute: `abated` / `settled` — raised 2026-09-07 — status: RESOLVED (keep `quiescent`)

- claim: plainer register for a readout to the owner
- counter: both are **neutral about cause**, which is exactly the ambiguity that produced the defect.
  ⇒ **the word's job is to carry the doubt**; a word that reads as benign cannot do that job
- resolution: forbidden as the term. ⚠️ *"abatement"* stays legal as the **event** (a symptom
  abates); `quiescent` and `resolved` are the two **verdicts** on that event

## .evidence

**four independent mechanisms, from the cited literature, in which complete relief is a phase:**

| scenario | source-side mechanism |
|---|---|
| perforated appendix / viscus | decompression on perforation ⇒ pain eases before peritonitis |
| ovarian torsion | characteristically intermittent — it untwists and re-twists |
| mesenteric ischaemia | a quiet interval as the wall infarcts |
| ectopic pregnancy | episodic; silent between episodes, up to rupture |

🔴 **the instances are literature-sourced across four independent rows**, never four reads of one
row. ⇒ this is why the term was **paved** where the `ask`/`act`/`test` taxonomy of 2026-09-06 was
**deferred**: that one was a taxonomy I invented, tested against one case; this one is a documented
pattern the sources supply four times over.

## .invariants — checkable

1. a symptom-stop with **no assay** may never be graded `resolved` — the grade is `unknown`
2. a **sudden, complete** stop after a severe peak is presumed `quiescent` until an assay says
   otherwise
3. 🔴 **a scenario may not leave the superposition on a symptom-stop of any grade** — only an assay
   removes it. `quiescent` vs `resolved` changes the *urgency*, never the *membership*
4. a readout that reports a symptom-stop must carry its grade **in the same cell as the report** —
   a warn elsewhere in the document does not discharge it
