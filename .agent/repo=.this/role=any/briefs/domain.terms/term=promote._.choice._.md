# domain.term: promote

term.chosen   = promote
term.kind     = verb
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- publish (names a move into DAYLIGHT — the fullsun gradient's axis. a promote is a move up a SCOPE ladder, and the two are orthogonal)
- copy (a promote **moves** a fact's home; a copy leaves two homes and invites drift)
- extract (already taken by the research capture step — a read out of a source, not a move between homes)
- graduate (🔴 taken: it names the 🕶️ obscure → ☀️ fullsun transition on a trigger. a different ladder)
- share (the declared verdict alias of ☀️ fullsun — a posture word, not a move)
- generalize (names a change to the fact's CONTENT; a promote changes only where it lives)

⚠️ **`lift` is NOT forbidden — it is the declared co-verb.** see below.

## .what

**the deliberate move of a fact from a narrower home to its most-common-denominator home** — the
least-common ancestor that actually consumes it.

the ladder, per `rule.require.accrue-research`:

```
a case yield             (obscure, per-case — the fact must not stay here)
  └─ accrue/             ($route-held, cheap, high-recall — accrue liberally)
       └─ promote        (DELIBERATE, curated)
            ├─ used by ONE role  → src/domain.roles/<role>/briefs/inventory/
            └─ used by 2+ roles  → .agent/repo=.this/role=any/briefs/inventory/
```

## 🔴 .promote is a SCOPE move; fullsun is a LIGHT verdict. they are orthogonal

the two ladders are easy to fuse and must not be:

| | what it changes | its axis |
|---|---|---|
| **promote** | **where** a fact lives | **scope** — how many consumers can reach it |
| ☀️ **fullsun** | **whether** it may be exposed | **light** — whose value a leak would cost |

⇒ a fact can be fullsun and unpromoted (correct, and reachable by one route); it can be promoted and
still owe a red-team. **neither implies the other.**

## .the asymmetry — accrue is cheap, promote is dear

- **accrue liberally.** the `accrue/` hold has no ceremony; a fact there costs one route's namespace
- 🔴 **promote deliberately.** the inventory home must stay clean, not flooded. a premature promote
  pollutes every consumer's namespace with knowledge one owner holds

## .the test, and the trap it names

> **"which role's expertise would AUTHOR this fact?"** — never *"who reads it?"*

🔴 **native-to, not cited-by.** a cross-role citation is normal and earns `role=any` not at all; only
shared **authorship** does. when unsure, prefer the role leaf — **a later promote is cheap, the
reverse is churn.**

## .invariants

- a promote **moves** a home; it never duplicates one. two homes for one fact is drift, not reach
- 🔴 a promote to `role=any` where **one role's expertise owns the fact** is a **premature promote** —
  a blocker in `rule.require.accrue-research`
- ⚠️ **a promote changes no content.** a fact that must be reworded to fit a wider home was never
  ready ⇒ **the rework is a separate act, and it comes first**
- **the reverse move is expensive.** every citation to the old home breaks ⇒ prefer the leaf under doubt
