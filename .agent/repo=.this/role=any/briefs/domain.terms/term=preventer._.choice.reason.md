# domain.term.choice.reason: preventer

## .etymology

from latin *praevenire* — "to come before" (*prae-* before + *venire* to come). the word
names **when the role acts**, which is the whole distinction: every other role in `mhedic`
acts once a complaint exists. this one acts before it does.

## .the rejected alternatives

| word | why not |
|------|---------|
| `screener` | names one tactic (the screen) as if it were the whole role; vaccines and risk counsel are not screens |
| `prophylactic` | an adjective pressed into a noun, and in common english it has drifted to name one specific product |
| `wellness` | a brand word — vague by design, and it invites scope creep into fitness and lifestyle |
| `monitor` | implies passive watch; the preventer acts (orders the vaccine, sets the schedule) |

`screener` is the closest miss and the most instructive: it names a **tactic**, and a role
named for its tactic cannot grow past it. `preventer` names the **motive** (act before harm),
which outlives any particular tactic — per `howto.domain-discovery`, name from the motive.

## .the boundary this term draws

| role | acts | when |
|------|------|------|
| `preventer` | before a complaint | asymptomatic |
| `diagnostician` | on a complaint | symptomatic |

the seam is the complaint. a patient with a symptom belongs to the diagnostician, even if the
answer turns out to be a vaccine.

## .evidence

- discovery: dimensional decomposition on the time axis — the role set was walked as
  before-complaint vs after-complaint, and only one role sat on the before side
- the role's declared purpose spans screens, vaccines, risk factors, and surveillance — four
  tactics under one motive, which is why the motive had to be the name
- invariant: the preventer never reasons from a present symptom. a symptom hands the case to
  the diagnostician.
