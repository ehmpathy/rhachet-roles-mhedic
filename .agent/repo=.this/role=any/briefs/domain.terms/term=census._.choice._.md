# domain.term: census

term.chosen   = census
term.kind     = noun
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- list (too generic; a list makes no completeness claim, a census does)
- roster (a roster names members but carries no proof-of-coverage frame)
- recall (the antonym — a recall is asserted-from-memory, a census is frame-proven; never use recall to mean census)
- shortlist (a curated few; a census is the bounded whole, not a pick)
- directory (a directory is a source read INTO a census, not the census itself)

## .what

a **proof-backed, frame-anchored roster** of every provider of a given specialty within a
drive-radius — enumerated from an authoritative population frame (the NPI registry), cross-checked
against crowd directories, with an explicit `.how coverage was proven` claim. the referrer's
answer to an "exhaustive" ask; the opposite of a **recall** (an asserted-complete list from memory).
a census names its frame, its diff, and its still-unproven edges; a recall names none.

## 🔴 .the genus — a census IS an inventory

> **`inventory` is the genus; `census` is the species that adds a proven frame.**

⚠️ **`inventory` is deliberately NOT a forbidden synonym above.** it names the wider kind, and a
census filed as `inventory.of=…` is correct rather than a drift — which is why this file's own
worked-example ref points at one. settled 2026-09-07 by enumeration: the corpus holds 66
`inventory.of=` artifacts across ten `.of=` kinds, and **`census` fits exactly one of them.**

⇒ what `census` adds, and what an inventory need not have: a declared **population frame**, a
**diff** against it, and an explicit **`.how coverage was proven`** claim.
