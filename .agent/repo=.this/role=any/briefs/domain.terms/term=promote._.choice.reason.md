# domain.term.choice.reason: promote

## .etymology

**promote** — latin *promovere*, "to move forward," from *pro-* "forward" + *movere* "to move."
its familiar modern sense is **a move to a higher rank**, and that is the sense wanted: a fact
promoted does not change; **its rank does** — more consumers can now reach it, and the bar it had
to clear was higher.

⇒ the word carries three properties the rejects do not:

1. **it is a MOVE, never a copy** — *movere*. one home before, one home after
2. **it is EARNED** — a promotion is granted on merit, so *"promote deliberately"* is already in the word
3. **it says naught about exposure** — a promotion raises rank, never visibility. that is exactly the
   orthogonality this term must preserve against the fullsun gradient

chosen over the rejected set for one reason each:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `publish` | 🔴 names a move into **daylight** — the ☀️ fullsun axis. to reuse it here would fuse two orthogonal ladders, and a reader would take a scope move for a light verdict |
| `graduate` | 🔴 **already taken**, and by the neighbour most easily confused: `term=obscure` uses it for the 🕶️ → ☀️ transition on a trigger. one word, two ladders |
| `copy` | asserts **two homes**, which is the failure this act exists to end. a fact in two homes drifts |
| `extract` | taken by the research capture step — a read **out of a source**. and it names a direction (out), where promote names a rank (up) |
| `share` | the declared **verdict alias** of ☀️ fullsun. a posture word, never a move |
| `generalize` | names a change to the fact's **content**. a promote changes only its home ⇒ if content must change, that is a separate act, done first |

## .disputes

### dispute: is `lift` a forbidden synonym? — raised 2026-09-09 — status: RESOLVED (co-verb, never forbidden)

- raised.by = `rule.require.accrue-research` uses both, sometimes in one sentence: *"promotion is
  deliberate — a curated **lift** of a fact proven generic enough…"*
- claim = two verbs, one act ⇒ `rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms`, forbid the weaker
- counter = 🔴 **they name two different rungs of one ladder, and the rule uses them that way
  consistently:**

  | verb | the move | ceremony |
  |---|---|---|
  | **lift** | a yield → `accrue/`, and the raw mechanical act at any rung | cheap, liberal, high-recall |
  | **promote** | `accrue/` → an `inventory/` home | 🔴 **deliberate, curated, earned** |

  ⇒ *"lift liberally, promote deliberately"* is a coherent instruction; **the same sentence with one
  verb twice loses the whole distinction.** and the architect's parent rule
  (`rule.prefer.most-common-denominator`, 🔸 foreign) speaks only of `lift`, so the two words also
  mark **which rule you are in**
- resolution = **co-verbs with declared rungs.** `lift` = the mechanical move, any rung. `promote` =
  the curated move into a shared home. ⚠️ **`lift` used where the curated bar applies under-states
  the ceremony** — a nitpick, and the only real drift risk here

⇒ **fifth pair in this corpus settled by a declared relation rather than a forbid** — after
`census`/`inventory`, `fullsun`/`share`, `scrub`/`anonymize`, `obscure`/`protect`.

### 🔴 dispute: is there an unfilled "promotion duty"? — raised 2026-09-08 — status: RESOLVED (largely no — the round-10 claim was wrong)

- raised.by = a human's *"did you promote everything that could be fullsunned?"*, which round 10
  recorded as an OPEN gap in `term=fullsun._.choice.reason.md`
- claim, as round 10 wrote it = *"neither `publishability-triage` nor `fullsun-facts-not-tactics`
  says fullsun-able knowledge must be LIFTED… `accrue-research` is about the WRITE, and not about
  the MOVE"*
- counter = 🔴 **the last clause is false, and I authored it while I cited the rule.**
  `rule.require.accrue-research` carries **20 lines about the move**: a named promote step, a
  two-axis most-common-denominator destination rule, a native-to-not-cited-by test, and a **blocker**
  for a premature promote to `role=any`
- 🔴 **the shape of the error: a rule characterized wrongly while cited** — verbatim the
  **phantom-claim** class in `term=citation._.choice._.md`, which I had declared the round before,
  in a dispute I raised **against my own prior round's citation discipline**
- resolution = the duty is **largely covered**. what genuinely remains is narrower and stated below

⇒ ⚠️ **the residue, which is real**: `accrue-research`'s ladder starts at a **route's `accrue/` dir**.
it says naught about a fact that lives in a **gitignored or untracked** artifact — no route, no
`accrue/`, so the ladder has no bottom rung to stand on. **the measured instance is
`.agent/.cache/`**, where a full lesson trail lives that no traveler can reach.

⇒ that residue is a **bhrain-scoped** gap (the cache is the learner's), and it is queued rather than
authored here — `rule.always.scope-onetime-lessons-to-the-behavior` (bhrain/learner).

## .invariants

- a promote is a **move**, never a copy
- a promote is **earned** — it has a bar (most-common-denominator) that a candidate can fail
- 🔴 a promote is **orthogonal to the light gradient**. it changes reach, never exposure
- the reverse move breaks every citation to the old home ⇒ under doubt, hold at the leaf
