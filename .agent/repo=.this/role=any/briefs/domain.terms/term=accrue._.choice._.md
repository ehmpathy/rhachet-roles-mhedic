# domain.term: accrue

term.kind     = verb
term.synonyms.forbidden:

- log

## .what

to **lift a generic, cited fact out of a case yield into a reusable, promotable home**
(`$route/accrue/inventory.of=<topic>.md`), so research compounds across cases instead of a death in
one case's conclusion. the verb behind `rule.require.accrue-research` and the `accrue/` dir every
route research stone deposits into. distinct from a mere `cache` (a throwaway copy) — an accrual is a
durable, cited, curated deposit meant to grow and be promoted.
