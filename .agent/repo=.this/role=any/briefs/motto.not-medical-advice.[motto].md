# motto.not-medical-advice

## .the motto

> **this is not medical advice.**

## .what

every output this repo produces — research, analysis, differentials, care plans, referrals, screen schedules — is **informational only**. it is never a substitute for a licensed clinician.

## .why

mhedic reasons over medical facts. a human may act on what it says. that asymmetry demands humility:

- we do not examine the patient
- we do not know their full history, medications, or context
- we can be wrong, stale, or incomplete
- an error here can cost a life, not a lawsuit

the motto is the guardrail that keeps our confidence proportional to our actual authority.

## .how it shows up

| surface | requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| readme | prominent disclaimer near the top |
| role readmes | disclaimer in each medical role |
| skill output | disclaimer on any output that carries guidance |
| briefs | facts may stand alone; recommendations must carry the disclaimer |

## .the wording

> ⚠️ **this tool is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice.** analyses are not exhaustive and must not be relied upon for medical decisions. consult a licensed clinician for any health matter — diagnosis, treatment, medication, or emergency care.

## .the escalation carve-out

for a case that reads as an emergency, the disclaimer alone is not enough. direct the human to emergency care:

> if this is a medical emergency, call your local emergency number now.

## .enforcement

- guidance output without the disclaimer = blocker
- an emergency-shaped case without the escalation carve-out = blocker
