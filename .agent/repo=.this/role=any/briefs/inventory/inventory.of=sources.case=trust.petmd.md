## .why we can trust it

articles carry a named DVM reviewer. good for the owner's view of vaccine-reaction timelines and
the "contact your veterinarian" thresholds that separate normal soreness from a real problem.

> vaccine reactions are "usually mild and short-lived," but a cat that is "still painful or
> lethargic after a day or two" warrants a call to the veterinarian. — petmd.com (vaccine
> side-effects article)

## .caveats — why only *caution*

- consumer publisher; generalized, not primary. never the sole citation for a safety-critical
  claim — back with the label (`case=trust.fda-dailymed`) or peer review (`case=trust.ncbi-pmc`).
- three distinct PetMD articles on three topics are three distinct works that share one publisher;
  count them as distinct evidence items, but note the shared domain for breadth.

## .use

reaction timelines, red-flag thresholds, owner-monitor guidance — corroborate with label +
literature.
