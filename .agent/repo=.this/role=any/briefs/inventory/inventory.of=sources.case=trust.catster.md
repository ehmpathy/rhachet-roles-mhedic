# inventory.of=sources.case=trust.catster

- **source**: Catster — vet-authored / vet-reviewed cat-care articles (Ask-the-Vet column)
- **domain**: catster.com
- **case**: **trust (caution)** — tier 3 (professional / client-education, secondary)
- **bhrowser retrieval**: ok (2026-08-10)

## .why we can trust it

articles carry a named DVM author or reviewer (e.g. Dr. Karyn Kanowski). good for the owner's
view of post-vaccine soreness, "when to call the vet" thresholds, and duration norms.

> "the combination of a local reaction to the injection and the immune response to vaccination can
> sometimes result in a feverish feline with a sore leg." — Dr. Karyn Kanowski,
> https://www.catster.com/ask-the-vet/why-is-my-cat-lethargic-and-limping-after-a-vaccine/

## .caveats — why only *caution*

- consumer-facing publisher; content is generalized, not a primary regulatory or peer-reviewed
  source. never the primary citation for a safety-critical claim — back those with the label
  (`case=trust.fda-dailymed`) or peer review (`case=trust.ncbi-pmc`).
- a distinct article/author is a distinct source; do not count two Catster pages as one domain
  "repeat" — each is a separate vet-authored work, but note they share a publisher.

## .use

soreness duration, red-flag thresholds, vaccine-over-a-limb rationale — corroborate with label +
literature.
