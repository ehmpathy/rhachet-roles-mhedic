# inventory.of=sources.case=trust.ncbi-pmc

- **source**: PubMed Central (NIH/NLM) — peer-reviewed full text
- **domain**: pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov (and pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov for abstracts)
- **case**: **trust** — tier 2 (peer-reviewed literature)
- **bhrowser retrieval**: ok (2026-08-08)

## .why we can trust it

full text of peer-reviewed journal articles, hosted by NLM. best source for mechanism,
pharmacokinetics, and controlled-trial efficacy/safety evidence.

> "Robenacoxib is a veterinary‐approved non‐steroidal anti‐inflammatory drug (NSAID) of the coxib group." — Lees et al. 2022, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9541287/

## .caveats

- a single study ≠ consensus; weigh study design, n, funding, and recency.
- our bhrowser reads captured the **leading portion** (abstract/intro) of long articles — cite what
  was actually returned; pull deeper sections when a claim needs results/discussion.
- inclusion in PMC "does not imply endorsement ... by NLM or the National Institutes of Health."

## .use

mechanism, PK, efficacy, and safety claims; prefer reviews for synthesis, RCTs for specific effects.
