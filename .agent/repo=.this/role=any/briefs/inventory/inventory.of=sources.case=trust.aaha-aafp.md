# inventory.of=sources.case=trust.aaha-aafp

- **source**: AAHA / AAFP — American Animal Hospital Association & American Association of Feline
  Practitioners (feline vaccination guidelines + injection-site guidance)
- **domain**: aaha.org, catvets.com
- **case**: **trust** — tier 1/2 (professional guideline body, consensus standard)
- **bhrowser retrieval**: ok (2026-08-10)

## .why we can trust it

the profession's consensus guideline authority for feline vaccination. the 2020 AAHA/AAFP Feline
Vaccination Guidelines are the standard cited across clinics; published in the Journal of Feline
Medicine and Surgery.

> "the Task Force recommends vaccination in the lower distal limbs to facilitate clean margins if
> surgical amputation is required." — AAHA update on feline injection-site sarcomas

injection-site placement per the AAFP anatomical chart (widely referenced): rabies → right rear
leg, FeLV → left rear leg, FVRCP → right front leg ("below the knee" so a sarcoma is amenable to
amputation).

## .caveats

- a guideline/consensus document, not an incidence study — for a magnitude use the primary
  literature (e.g. Moore 2007, Merck).

## .use

vaccine placement, concurrent-dose guidance, prevention levers, standard-of-care context.
