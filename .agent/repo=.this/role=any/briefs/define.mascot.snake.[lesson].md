# define.mascot.snake

## .what

mhedic's mascot is the snake 🐍 — the **Rod of Asclepius**, the literal and traditional symbol of medicine.

## .why

- **Rod of Asclepius**: a single serpent coiled around a staff, borne by Asclepius, the Greek god of medicine and cures. it has marked the medical arts for millennia and still marks hospitals, ambulances, and the WHO.
- **shed & renew**: the snake sloughs its worn skin — a natural metaphor for restored health.
- **temple-tender**: live non-venomous serpents (the Aesculapian snake) roamed the ancient sanctuaries of care (asclepieia), woven into the rituals of the sick.
- **the dose makes the cure**: venom poisons or cures by the measure alone — the root idea of *pharmakon* and of pharmacology.

### .not the caduceus

the true medical symbol is the *single*-snake Rod of Asclepius — **not** the winged, two-snake **caduceus**, which belongs to Hermes (commerce, messengers, thieves). a common mix-up, especially in the US; mhedic uses the single-snake rod.

### .why snake for this registry

parallels the peer registries, each with one nature mascot:
- 🐢 sea turtle — ehmpathy
- 🦉 owl — bhrain
- 🐙 octopus — rhachet
- 🐍 snake — mhedic
