## .the cue that fired

two cues, one round:

1. 🔴 **a synonym drift, in one sentence, made by me.** the r2 self-review read *"so is a
   **mitigation**… and recorded a **softener**"* — one concept, two words, eleven words apart.
   ⚠️ **and `softener` is actively hazardous in THIS route**: the case is a diarrhoea/constipation
   differential and *stool softener* is a real drug class in it. `G2sa` is literally *"osmotic"*
   medication. ⇒ fixed in place; **the drift is what proves the concept has earned a word.**
2. 🔴🔴 **three of five fulcrum entries mis-stated their own execution** (r2 passes 83–84), and the
   worst was a mitigation recorded at the wrong site.

## .the gap, precisely

**a fulcrum entry routinely records a partial measure taken alongside the fork it chose** — a flag,
a pointer, a bound, a caveat — that reduces the cost of the fork NOT taken. this route produced
several, and **the concept has no name**, so each entry describes it freshly.

⚠️ **the naming gap and the verification gap are the same gap.** an unnamed thing has no rule
attached to it. checkable evidence, this route:

| entry | its mitigation claim | what the artifact holds |
|---|---|---|
| **F5** | *"a pointer was added to the `7.x` row rather than a copy"* | 🔴 the pointer is at **`6.x`**; no `7.x` cell held it. `grepsafe 'bounded twice'` → **1 hit** |
| **F1** | *"the class note instructs `8.1` directly"* | ✅ true — but the same bound reached three `13` sites and **neither** `19` site for 17 passes |
| **F3** | *"a one-line gloss is owed and is **not yet written**"* | 🔴 written at blend line **1362** since pass 61 — an UNDER-claim |

🔴 **the errors have no common direction** — over-claim, under-claim, wrong referent. ⇒ **no
directional sweep finds this class**; only a per-entry check does.

⚠️ **and F5's own re-open trigger could not fire**: it read *"if `7.x` over-weights E3 **despite the
pointer**"* — a test that presumes the mitigation exists is blind to its absence.

## .the shape of the fix — for bhrain, not here

1. **a term cluster** — `term=route.fulcrum.mitigation._.choice._.md` + `.reason.md`
   - **chosen**: `mitigation`
   - **kind**: noun · **boundary**: `route.fulcrum`
   - **forbidden synonyms**: `softener` (informal, and a drug class in a GI domain), `hedge`,
     `caveat` (a caveat is the CONTENT; a mitigation is the ACT of siting it), `patch`
   - **`.what`**: *a partial measure recorded beside a fulcrum's taken fork, which lowers the cost
     of the fork not taken — a flag propagated, a bound copied, a pointer placed*
2. 🔴 **the rule that the term unlocks** — a `where` field records an **INTENTION**, and no extant
   rule requires it be checked against the artifact. candidate:
   `rule.always.verify-a-fulcrums-mitigation-at-its-stated-site` — **one grep per entry, at the
   moment of the call**, since the entry is written while the author still knows where they put it.
3. **a sharper trigger contract** — a re-open condition must be falsifiable when the mitigation is
   **absent**, not merely when it is weak.

⚠️ **the cheap variant, if the rule is judged too heavy**: make the `where` field require a
**quoted line** from the site rather than a description of it. a quote cannot be written for a site
that does not hold the text.

## .why it is not done in this round

- **SAFE?** ✅ yes — a term cluster is additive.
- **CLEAN?** 🔴 **no, and the reason is jurisdiction rather than ripple.** `fulcrum` is declared in
  **bhrain/role=driver** (`rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess`,
  `rule.always.defer-fulcrums-to-last`). ⇒ per `rule.always.scope-onetime-lessons-to-the-behavior`,
  *"a lesson whose home is another repo is re-seeded there, never adopted here"* — to write
  `fulcrum.mitigation` into **this** repo's glossary would mint a term in another repo's namespace,
  which is **exactly the fault F6 records** (`G32` minted in `1.3`'s space).
- ✅ **the in-scope half WAS done now**: the drift is fixed, and all three mis-stated entries are
  corrected in the inventory.

## .the worked example to carry over

⚠️ **the origin route was a private medical case and was removed** (`rule.forbid.pii`) — cite
the **measurement**, which is what carries the argument: a fulcrum inventory of **five entries,
three of them wrong about their own execution, in three different directions** (over-claim,
under-claim, wrong referent), with **one control entry clean on every claim** — which is what makes
the audit a credible result rather than a manufactured fault.

⚠️ **the transferable line**: *a fulcrum is a record of a DECISION; this route had been reading it as
a record of an ACT.* the inventory is what the closing council reads to rule, so a mis-sited
mitigation misdirects a ruling rather than merely a row.
