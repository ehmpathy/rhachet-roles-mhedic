## .the gap, precisely

**every patient.info capture is scoped to the page's `All replies` block, so the patient's own post
is never saved.** two checkable facts over `$route/.play/.out/social=pi-*.txt`:

| the check | result |
|---|---|
| `rhx grepsafe --pattern '^All replies' --glob 'social=pi-*.txt' --count` | **23 — one per thread, all 23 threads** |
| `rhx grepsafe --pattern '^LANDED:' --glob 'social=pi-*.txt'` | **0 of 23 urls end `/1`** — all land `/2`…`/9` |

internal corroboration, independent of both: several captured "OP posts" open by thanks to a named
responder for a reply — so the captured text is itself a reply to a post the corpus does not hold.
the same shape repeats in all 23.

⇒ the search matched correctly **against the patient's post**; the extractor then took the replies
section and discarded it.

### what it cost

| the count | what it actually measured |
|---|---|
| E2 = 1/23, E3 = 1/23, E4 = 1/23 | the replies, not the patients |
| *"10 of 23 carry a confirmed outcome; 13 are responder speculation"* | a corpus of replies is mostly repliers, by construction |
| *"one account wrote in 10 of 23 threads"* | a reply-corpus over-samples prolific repliers, by construction |

## .the shape of the fix

1. **`$route/.play/read.social.patientinfo.play.ts`** — widen the extraction scope so it captures the
   thread body **above** the `All replies` marker, not only the block beneath it.
   ⚠️ **do NOT "just re-capture at `/1`"** — that was the first, wrong diagnosis. `/1` renders the
   same page and the same extractor takes the same block. **the url is a symptom; the scope is the
   cause.**
2. **re-run** the five accepted searches, and assert in the playbook that each thread's capture holds
   text **before** its `All replies` marker — a fail-loud check, so this cannot recur silently.
3. **re-run `2.2`'s ledger** (S18–S40), then **`3.2`'s counts** over the widened corpus.
4. **re-check the pseudo-replication measure** — the most prolific responder's 10/23 share should
   fall once the OPs are present, and the new figure is the honest one.

## .why it is not done in this round

- **safe?** ✅ yes — additive, no data lost
- **clean?** 🔴 **no.** the repair belongs to `2.2`'s capture stage, and it invalidates `2.2`'s
  ledger and every count in `3.2`. it ripples across two completed stones and a peer review
- **clinical yield?** ⚠️ **low.** a lay-forum corpus is survivorship-bound and structurally cannot
  hold the high-acuity rows of any differential. a widened capture would sharpen the **benign** rows
  and change the acute assessment not at all

⇒ **dirty rework, low clinical yield ⇒ deferred, recorded, and handed to `3.3` as handoff 0** so the
absence is read as *unmeasured* rather than as a negative result.
