- this is a **prescriber-role** demo. the prescriber reviews a course of care but

  licensed clinician (`define.prescriber-scope`).
- interaction/safety questions (Onsior alongside other meds/allergies) are **flagged**,
  not asserted — that is pharmacist territory the prescriber must defer.
